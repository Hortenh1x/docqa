# Vacation & Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose

Rest matters. This policy explains how vacation works at Kranich Software: how many days you get, how to request them, and what happens to days you did not use. It is written for employees, not lawyers — when in doubt, ask People & Culture (people@kranich.example).

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all permanent employees of Kranich Software GmbH in Germany and Portugal, full-time and part-time. Part-time entitlements are calculated pro rata. Contractors and working students have their terms defined in their individual agreements.

## 3. Annual entitlement

Every full-time employee receives 24 vacation days per calendar year, in addition to the public holidays of their place of employment. Days accrue monthly from your first day of work.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, joined on 1 July. In her first calendar year she accrues half of the annual entitlement.

## 4. Requesting vacation

Submit requests in Fern, ideally as early as you can. Your manager approves requests; they will only push back when critical team coverage is at risk, and will propose an alternative window when they do.

For any absence longer than 5 consecutive working days, please give at least 4 weeks of notice. Shorter absences are flexible — a same-week request for a single day is usually fine.

## 5. Carryover

We want you to actually take your vacation. Unused days may be carried into the next calendar year, but they expire on March 15 of that year. Days that are not used by then are forfeited. Plan winter and early-spring breaks accordingly.

## 6. Sickness during vacation

If you fall ill during booked vacation, the affected days are converted back to sick leave once you provide the documentation described in the Sick Leave & Medical Absence policy (POL-007). Notify your manager as soon as you reasonably can.

## 7. Leaving the company

When your employment ends, untaken accrued days are paid out with your final payroll, and days taken in excess of accrual are deducted. People & Culture will include the exact calculation in your offboarding summary.

Grove hosts the current version of every policy. If anything here is unclear, write to people@kranich.example — we would rather answer twice than have you guess.
