# Expense Rates & Per-Diems

## 1. Purpose

This sheet is the single source of truth for per-diem rates and related expense figures. Policies such as the Travel & Expense Policy (POL-004) reference this document instead of quoting numbers, so that Finance can update rates once a year in exactly one place.

## 2. Per-diem rates by country

The full-day per-diem covers meals and incidentals for one calendar day of business travel.

| Country | Full-day rate |
| --- | --- |
| Germany (DE) | €28 |
| Portugal (PT) | €24 |
| France (FR) | €32 |
| United Kingdom (UK) | €36 |
| Poland (PL) | €22 |
| Spain (ES) | €26 |
| United States (US) | €55 |

Rates for destinations not listed here are set by Finance on request before the trip — write to finance@kranich.example with the destination and dates.

## 3. Partial travel days

For trips shorter than 8 hours, the per-diem is 50% of the country rate. Arrival and departure days of multi-day trips are treated the same way: each counts as a partial day at half rate.

Example: Deniz flies Berlin → Lisbon on Monday morning and returns Wednesday evening. Monday and Wednesday are partial days at half the Portuguese rate; Tuesday is a full day.

## 4. Meals provided by others

When a meal is already covered — a client dinner, a conference lunch, hotel breakfast included in the room rate — reduce the day's per-diem accordingly in Ledgerly: breakfast counts as 20%, lunch and dinner as 40% each. Ledgerly does the arithmetic for you when you tick the boxes.

## 5. Mileage

Using a private vehicle for a client visit requires prior manager approval and is reimbursed at the statutory rate of the country of employment. Public transport and trains remain the default.

## 6. Currency

All rates are stated in euros. Expenses paid in other currencies are converted by Ledgerly at the card provider's rate on the transaction date.

## 8. Card limits and approvals

Access: Finance only

Corporate cards are issued by Finance to colleagues who regularly buy on behalf of the company — office supplies, cloud subscriptions, conference bookings. A card is a convenience, not a separate budget: every purchase still needs a receipt in Ledgerly and still follows the reimbursability rules in §5 of POL-004.

The single-transaction limit on a corporate card is €1,500. Anything above that needs approval from the CFO, Tomasz Zieliński, before the purchase is made — not afterwards. Send the vendor, the amount and the reason to finance@kranich.example; Tomasz answers from there. Splitting a purchase into smaller charges to stay under the limit is not allowed and counts as a policy breach.

Example: Rui Almeida wanted to book a venue for the Lisbon office day. The deposit alone exceeded the limit, so he sent the quote to Tomasz first and paid with the card once the approval arrived. Card holders and limits are reviewed by Finance each fiscal year.
