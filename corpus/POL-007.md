# Sick Leave & Medical Absence

## 1. Purpose

When you are ill, your only job is to get better. This policy explains how to report an absence, when a doctor's note is needed, and how longer illnesses are handled — with as little bureaucracy as we can legally manage.

## 2. Reporting an absence

Tell your manager and mark the absence in Fern before your working day would normally start, or as soon as you reasonably can. A one-line message is enough — you owe no diagnosis to anyone.

Example: Deniz woke up with a fever, sent "out sick today, moving my two calls" to her manager, and marked Fern from her phone. That is the entire expected process for a short absence.

## 3. When a doctor's note is required

A doctor's certificate is required from the 3rd calendar day of absence. Upload it to Fern; People & Culture handles the rest, including anything your health insurer needs. For absences of one or two calendar days, no certificate is needed.

If your country of employment has stricter statutory rules, the statutory rules win — Fern shows the correct requirement for your contract.

## 4. Sickness during vacation

Illness during booked vacation converts those days back to sick leave once the documentation above is provided. The vacation days return to your balance as described in the Vacation & Leave Policy.

## 5. Longer illnesses

From the second week onward, People & Culture stays in touch with you directly — about pay continuation rules in your country, insurer paperwork, and a return-to-work plan when you are ready. Your manager is only told what they operationally need: that you are out, and roughly until when.

## 6. Medical appointments

Routine appointments that must happen during working hours are simply worked around — block the calendar, no Fern entry needed. Recurring treatments are arranged individually and confidentially with People & Culture.

## 7. Privacy

Medical information is stored in Fern with access restricted to People & Culture. Managers never see diagnoses or certificates — only absence dates.
