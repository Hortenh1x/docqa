# Remote Work Policy

## 1. Purpose

Kranich Software is remote-first. This policy defines what "remote" means for us, what we expect from remote employees, and what you can expect from the company. Unlike the Workation Guideline (POL-003), this policy covers your **permanent** work setup in your country of employment — not temporary work from abroad.

## 2. Where you may work

You may work up to 100% remotely from anywhere **within your country of employment** — the country stated in your employment contract (Germany or Portugal). Working from another country, even briefly, is not covered here; see the Workation Guideline (POL-003) for temporary stays inside the EU/EEA.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, spends most weeks at home and books a desk in the Lisbon office through Perch when her team meets in person. Her setup needs no approval — it is the default.

## 3. Core collaboration hours

Teams agree on overlap windows rather than fixed office hours. The company-wide anchor is 11:00–15:00 CET: schedule meetings inside this window whenever possible, and keep your calendar honest about availability.

## 4. Workspace and equipment

Work from a safe, ergonomic setup. Equipment is provided under the Equipment & Hardware Policy (POL-006) — laptops, monitors and peripherals are ordered through IT, not expensed ad hoc. Kranich contributes to home-office essentials as described there.

## 5. Security expectations

Remote work does not relax security. Follow the IT & Information Security Policy (POL-005): company-managed devices only, the company password manager for every credential, and locked screens whenever you step away. Public Wi-Fi requires the company VPN.

## 6. Communication defaults

Written first: decisions and outcomes belong in Grove or the team's project space, not only in chat. If a discussion runs longer than fifteen minutes in text, switch to a call — then write down the result.

## 7. Office as a tool

The Berlin and Lisbon offices remain open to everyone. Desks and rooms are booked through Perch. Teams gather in person for planning weeks and onboarding — travel for these follows the Travel & Expense Policy (POL-004).
