# Benefits Overview

## 1. Purpose

Everything Kranich offers beyond salary, in one place. Details and country specifics live in the linked policies; this overview is the map.

## 2. Time off

Vacation entitlement, tenure bonus and carryover rules are defined in the Vacation & Leave Policy — always check the current version there. Public holidays follow your place of employment, and sick leave is covered in POL-007 with deliberately little paperwork.

## 3. Health and wellbeing

- **Wellness budget:** €50 per month for anything that genuinely keeps you well — sports memberships, therapy co-pays, meditation apps, massage. Claim it in Ledgerly; no questions asked within the list in Grove.
- Statutory health insurance applies per your country of employment; People & Culture helps with provider paperwork on request.

## 4. Getting around

For colleagues in Germany, the Deutschlandticket is fully covered — order it through Fern and it renews automatically. Lisbon-based colleagues receive the equivalent local transit pass under the same rule.

## 5. Work setup

Hardware, budgets and refresh cycles are defined in the Equipment & Hardware Policy (POL-006). Remote-first specifics — where you may work, collaboration hours — live in the Remote Work Policy (POL-002), and temporary work from abroad in the Workation Guideline (POL-003).

## 6. Family

Parental leave, pay top-ups and return arrangements are described in the Parental Leave Policy (POL-008). The top-up applies automatically once leave is registered; no application forms.

## 7. Growth

A learning budget is agreed per team each year and spent with your manager's sign-off — books and conference tickets rarely get questioned, certifications are planned in the review cycle (HR-003). Internal mobility is real: open roles are posted in Grove before they are posted anywhere else.

## 8. Practical FAQ

- **When does the probation period end?** After 6 months from your start date; your contract states the exact date and Fern shows it.
- **Where do I see my vacation balance?** Fern, including carryover expiry dates.
- **Who answers benefit questions?** people@kranich.example, or your People partner named in Grove.

Example: Sofia used the wellness budget for a climbing-gym membership in Madrid and the learning budget for a product-analytics course — both approved in the same week, both in Ledgerly.

## 9. Hardship advances

Access: People & Culture only

Sometimes life sends a bill before payroll does — an unexpected medical cost, a flat deposit, a family emergency — and Kranich can pay part of future salary in advance. This section is for People & Culture, who handle each request confidentially; colleagues ask their People partner, not their manager.

A hardship advance is limited to €2,000. It is repaid through payroll within 12 months, in instalments agreed with the colleague. There is no interest; a short written request is enough, the agreement is recorded in Fern and Finance sets up the deduction. An open advance is settled with the final payroll on leaving (see §6 of HR-002).

Decide on the situation, not on tenure or role. Example: Lea, people operations specialist in Berlin, arranged an advance for a colleague whose landlord demanded a deposit at short notice; the money went out with the next payroll run and nobody outside People & Culture knew.
