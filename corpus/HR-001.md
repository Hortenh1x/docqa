# Onboarding Guide

## 1. Welcome

Welcome to Kranich Software! This guide walks you through your first weeks — what arrives when, what to complete, and who helps. Your onboarding buddy and your manager have the same checklist, so nothing depends on you alone.

## 2. Before day one

IT ships your laptop and peripherals to arrive before your start date (see the Equipment & Hardware Policy, POL-006, for what is included). Fern sends you a link to complete your personal data, bank details, and emergency contact — ten minutes, best done early.

## 3. Your first day

Your manager and buddy greet you in a short kickoff call. You will activate your accounts, join the team channels, and get your 1Password vault access — from that moment, every credential you create lives there (POL-005 explains why this is non-negotiable).

## 4. Your first two weeks

- **Complete the mandatory security training — see §3 of the IT & Information Security Policy (POL-005) for the deadline.** The training is assigned to you in Fern automatically; do not postpone it, the deadline is firm.
- Meet the people your role touches: your buddy schedules five short intro calls.
- Read the policy starter pack in Grove: Remote Work (POL-002), Travel & Expense (POL-004), Code of Conduct (POL-009).
- Ship something small. Every team keeps a "first-week task" ready — Anna's was a one-line fix that removed a flaky test, and she still brings it up.

## 5. Your first three months

The probation period gives both sides room to check the fit; the terms and length are set in your employment contract and summarized in the Benefits Overview (HR-004) FAQ. Expect a structured check-in with your manager at the end of month one and a mid-probation conversation with People & Culture.

## 6. Tools you will meet

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Fern | HR: personal data, leave, trainings |
| Grove | Intranet and policy library |
| Perch | Desk and room booking |
| Ledgerly | Expenses and travel booking |
| 1Password | All credentials, no exceptions |

## 7. Who helps

- Buddy: everyday questions, no matter how small.
- Manager: role, expectations, feedback.
- People & Culture (people@kranich.example): contracts, leave, anything personal.
- it-help@kranich.example: hardware and access.

## 8. One ask

If any step of this guide was confusing or out of date, tell Lea in People Operations — the next new joiner will thank you.
