# Offboarding Checklist

## 1. Purpose

Leaving well matters as much as starting well. This checklist covers what happens between a signed resignation (or a decided departure) and your last day — for you, your manager, and the back office.

## 2. As soon as the departure is confirmed

- People & Culture confirms your last working day and remaining vacation balance in writing.
- Your manager plans the handover: open tasks, owned systems, running client relationships.
- Fern generates your personal offboarding checklist — both you and your manager see the same list.

## 3. Your handover

Write the handover in Grove, not in private notes: what you own, where it lives, what is fragile, who takes over. Walk your successor (or your manager) through it in a call. Customer-facing colleagues introduce the new contact to clients before the last day.

## 4. Equipment return

Return all company equipment within 10 business days after your last working day. Remote employees receive a prepaid shipping label through the checklist in Fern; office-based colleagues hand everything to IT in person. The full list of what counts as equipment is in the Equipment & Hardware Policy (POL-006).

## 5. Access and accounts

IT disables accounts at the end of your last working day — this is automated from the Fern checklist, not something your manager has to remember. Shared credentials owned by your team are rotated; 1Password vault ownership transfers to your manager.

## 6. Money matters

Finance settles open expense reports with the final payroll. Untaken accrued vacation days are paid out as described in the Vacation & Leave Policy; overtaken days are deducted the same way.

## 7. The exit conversation

People & Culture invites you to a 30-minute exit conversation in your final week. It is optional and honest: what worked, what did not, what we should fix. Summaries are anonymized before leadership sees them.

## 8. After you leave

Your references and employment confirmations come from people@kranich.example — the alias keeps working for you as an alum. We genuinely like hearing where people land; the #alumni channel invitation arrives with your farewell note.
