# Sustainability at the Office

## 1. Purpose

Kranich Software is committed to reducing the environmental footprint of our physical workplaces. This policy describes how we approach sustainability at our offices in Berlin and Lisbon, and what we ask of everyone who uses them. It complements the broader environmental guidance in POL-004 and the travel-related rules in FIN-RATES-2026.

Our approach is practical: we make it easy to do the right thing, we remove single-use items where alternatives exist, and we keep energy use in mind without sacrificing comfort. Workplace & Operations owns this policy and reviews it each year.

## 2. Waste

We follow a simple hierarchy: reduce, reuse, recycle. Before anything is thrown away, ask whether it can be avoided, used again, or given a second life.

**What we provide.** Both offices have clearly labelled recycling stations for paper, packaging, glass, and general waste. Compost bins are available in the shared kitchens. Reusable cups, cutlery, and plates are stocked in every kitchen, and we encourage you to use them for meetings and events.

**What we ask of you.** Please sort your waste at the source and rinse containers before recycling. If you bring in food for a team event, choose reusable servingware over disposable options. When ordering supplies, prefer items with minimal packaging.

**Electronics and batteries.** Old cables, chargers, keyboards, and other small electronics should not go into general waste. Place them in the labelled collection box in each office; Workplace & Operations arranges proper recycling promptly. Batteries go in the separate battery container.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, hosts a demo day for her team. Instead of buying bottled water and paper cups, she books the meeting room with the water dispenser and uses the kitchen's reusable cups. Leftover snacks are shared with colleagues rather than thrown away.

## 3. Energy

We aim to use energy wisely in our offices. This means simple habits, sensible defaults, and equipment that does not draw power when idle.

**Lighting and climate.** Use natural light whenever possible. When you leave a meeting room or are the last to leave a floor, switch off the lights. Heating and cooling are controlled centrally and set to comfortable levels for working hours; please do not override them for extended periods. If you feel too warm or too cold, contact Workplace & Operations rather than adjusting the system yourself.

**Devices and equipment.** Set your laptop and monitor to sleep after a few minutes of inactivity. At the end of the day, shut down your computer and unplug chargers that are not in use. Kitchen appliances such as kettles should be filled only with the water you need. Shared printers are set to duplex by default; print only when necessary.

**Remote work.** Many of us work from home most of the time. The same principles apply there: switch off devices when not in use, and choose energy-efficient settings on your equipment. Kranich does not prescribe how you run your home, but we appreciate thoughtful choices.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, finishes her day and notices the meeting room lights are still on. She switches them off on her way out and shuts down her laptop instead of leaving it on overnight.

## 4. Questions

If you have questions about this policy, want to report a faulty appliance, or have an idea for improving sustainability at the office, contact Workplace & Operations at the usual internal channels. For questions about how sustainability relates to information security or procurement, refer to POL-004.

We also welcome suggestions for the annual review of this policy. Share your thoughts with Rui Almeida or your local office coordinator, and we will consider them in the next update.
