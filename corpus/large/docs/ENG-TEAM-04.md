# Team Charter — Customer Success

## 1. Mission

The Customer Success team helps logistics operators get lasting value from Kranich Route Cloud. We onboard new customers, guide them through adoption, and make sure they see the impact of route optimization in their daily operations. We listen for risks early and act before a customer becomes unhappy.

We succeed when our customers renew, expand, and recommend us. We measure ourselves by customer outcomes, not by activity.

## 2. Team

Customer Success currently has 34 people. The team sits within the Go-to-Market organization and is led by the Head of Customer Success, who reports to the CEO.

The team is distributed. We have colleagues in Berlin, Lisbon, and remote across Germany, Portugal, Spain, Poland, France, and the United Kingdom. We work in pods aligned to customer segments. Each pod has a mix of customer success managers and specialists. Field solutions engineers, like Marek in Poland, join pods when customers need on-site support.

Our day-to-day work is supported by the broader company. We rely on Platform & Infrastructure for technical escalations, Routing Core for product questions, and Fleet Insights for data-driven recommendations.

## 3. How we work

We are remote-first. Most of us work from home, and we use Perch to book a desk when we visit the Ritterstraße or Rua do Alecrim offices. We keep our calendars visible so colleagues know when we are available.

We follow a lightweight operating rhythm. Each pod starts the week with a short planning meeting. We track customer health in our shared workspace and review it together weekly. When a customer is at risk, we act promptly and involve the right people rather than waiting for the next review.

We document everything in Grove. Customer notes, playbooks, and templates live there so anyone can pick up a conversation with context. We use Fern for leave requests and personal data, Ledgerly for expenses and travel, and 1Password for shared credentials.

We value asynchronous communication. We write clear updates, record short videos when helpful, and avoid meetings that could be an email. When we do meet, we are present and prepared.

We give feedback generously and receive it openly. We assume good intent and address disagreements directly with the person involved. If a conflict escalates, our engineering manager or the VP People & Culture can help mediate.

## 4. Interfaces

Customer Success sits between our customers and the rest of Kranich. We are the customer's voice internally and Kranich's voice externally.

- **Routing Core and Fleet Insights**: We share customer feedback and feature requests. Product managers like Sofia use our input to shape the roadmap. We coordinate launches so customers hear about changes from us first.
- **Go-to-Market**: We work with sales on renewals and expansions. We hand over new customers smoothly and keep account context current.
- **Platform & Infrastructure**: We route technical issues through the proper channels and follow the incident process defined in POL-004.
- **Finance**: We flag commercial risks and support the renewal forecast. Pricing questions follow FIN-RATES-2026.
- **People & Culture**: We raise people-related concerns through Fern or by reaching out to people@kranich.example. Security matters go to security@kranich.example, and technical access issues go to it-help@kranich.example.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, notices a customer struggling with a new feature. She documents the issue in Grove, shares it with Sofia in Fleet Insights, and follows up with the customer once the fix is planned.

## 5. Questions

If you are unsure about anything in this charter, start with your pod lead. For questions about tools, processes, or cross-team collaboration, reach out to the Head of Customer Success. For people-related topics, contact people@kranich.example, and Aylin's team will point you in the right direction.

This charter is a living document. We review it with the team each year and update it when our ways of working evolve.
