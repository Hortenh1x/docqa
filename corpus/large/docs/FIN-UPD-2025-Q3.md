# Finance Update 2025 Q3

Access: Finance only

## 1. Summary

This update covers our financial performance for the third quarter of the current fiscal year. The quarter continued the positive trajectory we have seen across the business, driven by steady demand for the Kranich Route Cloud and disciplined execution by all teams. We remain on track with the priorities set out in the annual plan.

The figures below are shared for transparency and to support planning across Finance. As always, please treat this document as confidential and do not circulate it beyond the Finance team. If you have questions about anything in this update, reach out to the Finance mailbox or speak with Tomasz directly.

## 2. Revenue

Revenue grew 17.5% year over year in 2025 Q3. This growth reflects continued expansion within our existing customer base, as well as new customer wins in the mid-size logistics segment across our core markets.

From a product perspective, the Routing Core offering remains our primary revenue driver. Fleet Insights continues to gain traction, particularly among customers who are looking for a more complete view of their operations. The Platform & Infrastructure team has supported this growth by ensuring reliable service delivery, which in turn strengthens customer retention.

Customer Success has played an important role in this quarter's performance. Their work on onboarding and adoption has helped customers realise value from our software more quickly, which supports both renewals and expansion opportunities. The Go-to-Market team has also contributed by focusing on segments where our solution addresses a clear operational need.

## 3. Costs

Our cost base remains well managed. Personnel costs continue to represent the largest share of spending, which is expected given our remote-first setup and the investment we are making in engineering and customer-facing roles. We have kept hiring deliberate and aligned with revenue growth.

Operational costs, including infrastructure and tooling, have remained stable. The Platform & Infrastructure team has continued to optimise our cloud usage, and we have seen the benefit of those efforts in the quarter. Travel and customer-facing expenses were in line with expectations, reflecting normal business activity across our offices in Berlin and Lisbon and with customers in our key markets.

We have also continued to invest in areas that support long-term efficiency, such as internal tooling and security. These investments are modest in scale but contribute to the quality of our product and the productivity of our teams.

## 4. Outlook

Looking ahead to the final quarter of the year, we expect the trends from Q3 to continue. Demand for route optimisation remains strong, and our pipeline for new business is healthy. We will maintain our focus on profitable growth rather than pursuing volume at the expense of margin.

Our priorities for the coming months are to continue delivering value to existing customers, to support the successful launch of upcoming product enhancements, and to manage costs carefully. We will also review the annual plan and begin preparing the budget for the next fiscal year. Details on that process will be shared in a separate communication.

As always, we will provide a further update after the close of the year. We thank everyone across the company for their contribution to a strong quarter.
