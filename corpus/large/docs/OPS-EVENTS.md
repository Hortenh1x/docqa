# Team Events Guide

## 1. Purpose

This guide explains how to plan and run team events at Kranich Software GmbH. Team events include offsites, team lunches, after-work gatherings, and celebrations. They help us build connection across our remote-first company, whether you are in Berlin, Lisbon, or working from home in Poland, France, or elsewhere.

The Workplace & Operations team is here to support you. For questions about a specific event, reach out to Rui Almeida or your local office coordinator. For general guidance, check the Grove intranet or write to it-help@kranich.example.

This guide applies to events funded by the company, whether they happen at the Ritterstraße office, the Rua do Alecrim office, or at an external venue. It does not apply to purely personal gatherings.

## 2. Budget

Team events are budgeted at €25 per person. This amount covers food, drink, and the cost of a shared activity. It is intended for events that bring a team together, not for individual purchases.

The budget is per attendee, including the organizer. Use your team's operational budget for the event; do not charge it to a client project. If you plan an event that includes people from more than one team, agree in advance which team budget covers the cost.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, wants to host a lunch for her team at a nearby restaurant. She confirms the attendee count, multiplies it by the per-person rate, and checks that the total fits her team's budget before booking.

If your event would exceed the per-person amount, you can ask attendees to cover the difference themselves. You may also choose a simpler activity that fits within the rate. The rate is a ceiling, not a target.

## 3. Booking

Book events through the standard expense process in Ledgerly. Submit your request at least a few days before the event so that Workplace & Operations can review it. For events at either office, check availability in Perch and reserve the room or common area first.

When you book, include the event name, the team or group attending, the date, and the expected number of people. Attach the receipt or invoice to your expense entry promptly after the event.

For external venues, compare options and choose a place that is accessible to everyone attending. If your team includes remote colleagues, consider a hybrid format or choose a date when they can travel to the office. Travel costs for offsite events follow the travel policy in FIN-RATES-2026, not this guide.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, organizes a team dinner in Warsaw. He books the venue, submits the details in Ledgerly, and asks his manager to approve the expense before the event.

If you need to cancel, do so as soon as possible and update your Ledgerly entry. Venues may charge a cancellation fee; that fee counts toward the event budget.

## 4. Questions

For questions about budgets or booking, contact Workplace & Operations at it-help@kranich.example. For questions about team culture or inclusion, reach out to People & Culture at people@kranich.example.

If you are unsure whether an event qualifies, describe your plan in a short email and we will help you decide. Related guidance on team conduct can be found in POL-004. We want every team event to feel welcoming, so please consider dietary needs, mobility, and time zones when planning.

Example: Sofia, a product manager in Fleet Insights working remotely from Spain, asks whether a virtual coffee break counts as a team event. Workplace & Operations confirms that it does, and that the budget can cover delivery of a small treat to each participant.

Thank you for making time to bring your team together.
