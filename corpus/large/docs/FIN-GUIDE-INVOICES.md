# Supplier Invoice Approval

## 1. Purpose

This guide explains how supplier invoices are received, approved and paid at Kranich Software GmbH. We work with many external partners — from software vendors and office suppliers to consultants and facility services — and we want to make sure their invoices are handled reliably and without unnecessary delay.

The process described here applies to all invoices from suppliers and vendors that are addressed to Kranich Software GmbH. It covers invoices sent to any of our offices or to individual employees. If you receive an invoice in your inbox or by mail, please forward it to Finance promptly using the address provided in §2, so that we can register it in our system.

## 2. Receiving invoices

Invoices should be sent to the Finance team by email. If you receive a paper invoice or an invoice attached to an email that was sent to you personally, please forward it to the Finance shared mailbox without editing the attachment.

When Finance receives an invoice, we check that it contains the basic information we need: the supplier name, a clear description of the goods or services, the invoice date, and a reference to the purchase or contract it relates to. If information is missing, we will contact the supplier and ask for a corrected version.

Please do not approve invoices yourself, even if you ordered the goods or services. Your role is to confirm that the work was delivered as expected; the formal approval and payment sit with Finance. If you have questions about whether an invoice is legitimate or expected, contact Finance before the approval clock starts.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, receives an email from a conference organiser with an invoice attached. She forwards it to the Finance mailbox and notes in her reply that the conference was part of her team's customer event. Finance registers the invoice and routes it for approval.

## 3. Approval SLA

Supplier invoices are approved within 8 business days of receipt. The clock starts on the day Finance confirms receipt of a complete invoice. If we need to ask the supplier for missing details, the clock restarts once we have the corrected version.

Approval follows a simple route. Finance first checks the invoice against the relevant purchase order or contract. If the invoice matches, Finance approves it. If the invoice relates to a service or project managed by a specific team, Finance may ask that team's lead to confirm the work was delivered before approval is granted.

In practice, most invoices are approved well within the target. Invoices that require additional checks — for example, because the supplier is new or the amount is unusual for that vendor — may take longer, and we will keep the requester informed if that happens.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, arranges a training room rental for a client workshop. The hotel sends the invoice to Marek directly. He forwards it to Finance on a Monday. Finance confirms receipt the same day, checks it against the booking confirmation, and the invoice is approved within the week.

## 4. Payment runs

Once an invoice is approved, it is scheduled for the next payment run. Finance processes payments on a regular cycle, usually every few weeks. Approved invoices are grouped into batches and paid together to keep bank charges low and to make cash flow predictable.

If a supplier asks about payment status, please do not promise a specific date. Refer them to Finance, or let Finance know that the supplier has been in touch. We will confirm the payment date once the invoice is in a payment batch.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, orders a new external hard drive for testing. The supplier sends the invoice to her, she forwards it to Finance, and the invoice is approved in good time. When the supplier emails Anna a week later asking when payment will arrive, she replies that Finance handles payments and offers to connect them.

## 5. Questions

If you have questions about this process, or about a specific invoice, contact Finance by email. For questions about purchase orders or contracts, please reach out to the person who manages the supplier relationship in your team.

You can also find related guidance in POL-004, which covers expense reporting and reimbursement for employees, and FIN-RATES-2026, which explains our internal charge rates for cross-team work. These documents are available on Grove, our intranet and policy library.

If you believe an invoice is fraudulent or you are unsure whether a request to pay is legitimate, contact security@kranich.example before taking any further action. When in doubt, please ask — it is always better to check with us than to ignore an invoice or pay the wrong one.
