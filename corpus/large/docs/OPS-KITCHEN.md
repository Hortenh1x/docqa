# Kitchen & Supplies

## 1. Purpose

Our kitchens are shared spaces. Whether you are in the Ritterstraße office in Berlin, the Rua do Alecrim office in Lisbon, or working from home, we want you to feel looked after. This document explains what we stock, how to keep the kitchen pleasant for everyone, and where to turn if something is missing or broken.

The Workplace & Operations team, led by Rui Almeida, manages the kitchens in both offices. For anything related to supplies, appliances, or cleanliness, please reach out to the team via the it-help alias or the people alias, depending on the nature of your request.

## 2. What we stock

We aim to keep the kitchens stocked with the basics you need to get through your day. This includes coffee, tea, milk alternatives, and a selection of snacks. We also provide standard condiments, dish soap, sponges, and paper towels.

In Berlin and Lisbon, the offering is broadly similar, though we respect local preferences and seasonal availability. If you have a suggestion for a product we should carry, let the Workplace & Operations team know. We appreciate feedback and review it regularly.

For special occasions, such as a team celebration or an off-site, you can request additional supplies. Please make the request in good time so we can arrange everything. See §4 for the best way to reach us.

## 3. Keeping it tidy

A shared kitchen works well when everyone contributes. Please follow these simple guidelines:

- Wash and put away your dishes promptly. Do not leave them in the sink overnight.
- Label any food you store in the fridge or cupboard with your name. Unlabelled items may be removed after a few days.
- Clean up spills immediately, including in the microwave and on the counters.
- Empty the coffee machine grounds and dispose of full bins when you notice them.
- If you finish the last of something, add it to the shopping list or let the Workplace & Operations team know.

We understand that busy days happen. If you forget to tidy up, a colleague may gently remind you — that is normal and welcome. We are all responsible for making the kitchen a place where people want to take a break.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, often hosts client visits. She books the meeting room and lets the Workplace & Operations team know a day ahead so the kitchen has enough fresh coffee and snacks for her guests.

Appliances and equipment should be used with care. If something breaks or stops working, report it promptly rather than trying to fix it yourself. This includes the coffee machine, dishwasher, and refrigerator. The Workplace & Operations team will arrange for a repair or replacement.

Personal items, such as mugs or lunch boxes, should be kept in your workspace or labelled clearly. The kitchen is not a storage area for personal belongings.

For employees working remotely, we encourage you to create a workspace that suits you. While we cannot stock your home kitchen, we do offer guidance on setting up a comfortable home office. Speak to your manager or the People & Culture team if you need advice.

## 4. Questions

If you have a question about the kitchen, the supplies we stock, or how to request something, please contact the Workplace & Operations team. You can reach them through the it-help alias for practical matters, such as a broken appliance, or the people alias for suggestions and feedback.

For anything related to office policies in general, you may also refer to POL-004, which covers workplace conduct, or FIN-RATES-2026, which explains expense and reimbursement rules for purchases you make on behalf of the company.

We want the kitchen to be a place where you can refuel and connect with colleagues. Thank you for doing your part to keep it welcoming for everyone.
