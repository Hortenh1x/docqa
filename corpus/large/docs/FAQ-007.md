# Onboarding FAQ

## 1. Questions

**Where do I find the onboarding schedule for my first week?**
Your manager will share it with you before your start date. It includes introductions, setup sessions, and time with your team. For leave-related timing rules during onboarding, see POL-004.

**Which tools will I use from day one?**
You will receive invitations to Grove, our intranet and policy library, and to Fern, our HRIS. From there you can access the org chart, your personal data, and leave requests. You will also get access to 1Password for credentials and Ledgerly for expenses and travel.

**How do I book a desk or a meeting room?**
Use Perch. Availability differs between the Berlin and Lisbon offices, and most colleagues work remotely. Your team will tell you their usual patterns.

**What equipment will I get?**
Your manager or Workplace & Operations will coordinate your laptop and any accessories before you start. If something is missing or not working, contact it-help@kranich.example.

**Who is my point of contact for people questions?**
People & Culture is here for you. For personal data or leave questions, reach out via Fern or write to people@kranich.example. For security-related concerns, contact security@kranich.example.

**How do I set up my email and calendar?**
Platform & Infrastructure will send you setup instructions before your first day. If you run into trouble, it-help@kranich.example is the quickest route.

**When do I get my first paycheck?**
Payroll runs on the schedule described in the finance policies. Your People & Culture contact can walk you through the details; see also FIN-RATES-2026 for compensation-related provisions.

**How do I request time off during onboarding?**
You can submit leave requests in Fern at any time. During your first weeks, please coordinate with your manager so that training sessions are not disrupted. The approval process is described in POL-004.

**What should I do if I notice a security issue?**
Report it promptly to security@kranich.example. If you are unsure whether something is a security concern, it is always better to ask. See the security policy for guidance on handling sensitive information.

**How do I learn about company culture and remote work expectations?**
Grove contains our values, team guides, and practical advice for working across countries. Your onboarding buddy — a colleague from another team — will also help you get oriented.

**What happens after my first week?**
Your manager will set up regular check-ins. You will receive feedback and can raise questions as they come up. Onboarding is a gradual process, and it is normal to take time to feel fully settled.

**Example:** Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, had questions about booking a desk for her first in-person team week. Her onboarding buddy showed her Perch and introduced her to the Lisbon office norms, which made the transition smooth.
