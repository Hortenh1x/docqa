# Travel FAQ

## 1. Questions

**How do I book business travel?**
Book through Ledgerly, our expenses and travel tool. It shows the approved options for flights, trains, and hotels. If your trip needs an approval, the request routes to your manager automatically. For anything unusual, reach out to your manager before booking.

**What do I do if I need to travel on short notice?**
Book what is available and practical. If your preferred option is not in Ledgerly, you can book directly and attach the receipt when you submit your expenses. Add a short note explaining the situation so the approval is smooth.

**Which travel costs does Kranich cover?**
We cover the reasonable costs of getting you to and from your destination, accommodation, and meals while you are away on business. The detailed rules are in the travel policy, POL-004. For per-diem amounts, refer to FIN-RATES-2026.

**Can I extend a business trip for personal days?**
Yes, you can combine business and personal travel. You cover any extra costs that come from the personal portion, such as the difference in flight price, additional hotel nights, and your own meals on those days. Keep the receipts separate so the business part is easy to identify.

**How do I book a hotel?**
Choose a hotel through Ledgerly whenever possible. Pick something comfortable and practical near where you are working. If the area has no options in Ledgerly, book directly and submit the receipt.

**What about travel between home and the office?**
Regular commuting is your own responsibility. If you are asked to visit another Kranich office for work, the trip counts as business travel and follows the rules in POL-004. Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, visits the Lisbon office for a workshop; her travel and accommodation are covered by Kranich.

**How do I get reimbursed?**
Submit your expenses in Ledgerly within a reasonable time after the trip. Attach scanned or photographed receipts for each item. Your manager approves, and Finance processes the reimbursement on the next scheduled run. See §4 of POL-004 for the submission window.

**What if I lose a receipt?**
Submit the expense anyway with a note explaining what happened. For smaller amounts, a note is usually enough. For larger ones, Finance may ask for a bank statement or another form of proof. Do not let a missing receipt stop you from submitting.

**Am I covered by insurance while traveling?**
Kranich holds a corporate travel insurance policy that covers you on approved business trips. Details are in the travel policy. For personal travel that you add to a business trip, check whether your own insurance covers the personal days.

**Whom do I ask if I have a question not covered here?**
Start with your manager. For policy questions, contact people@kranich.example. For tool issues with Ledgerly, contact it-help@kranich.example. Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer in Poland, asks People & Culture whether a client-site visit counts as business travel; it does, and he books through Ledgerly.
