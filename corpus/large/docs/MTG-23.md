# Meeting Notes — Workplace committee (2025-12-16)

## 1. Attendees

- Aylin (chair)
- Rui
- Lea
- Deniz
- Marek
- Ines Duarte
- Priya (joining for the security portion)

## 2. Discussion

- Aylin opened the meeting and thanked everyone for making time before the holiday break.
- Rui shared an update on the Berlin office. The Ritterstraße space has been busier since more teams returned to hybrid work. Some employees mentioned that meeting rooms are hard to book on short notice, especially in the afternoon.
- Lea noted that the Lisbon office has seen the opposite trend. Several desks sit empty on most days, and the team there would like to make the space feel more welcoming.
- The group talked about the Perch booking tool. Some colleagues find it confusing when rooms are released if not checked in. Rui suggested reviewing the release rules and communicating them more clearly.
- Marek raised a point about remote colleagues in Poland and the UK. They sometimes feel left out of office-related announcements. The committee agreed that communication should work for everyone, not only for people based in Berlin or Lisbon.
- Priya joined briefly to mention that any new signage or room-naming initiative should avoid sensitive information. The committee took note.
- Deniz shared feedback from customers who visited the Berlin office. The reception area feels a bit bare, and a small improvement could make a good impression.
- No budget figures were discussed. Any spending proposal will be reviewed through the usual process with Finance.

## 3. Decisions

- The committee will simplify the Perch release rule so that rooms are freed after a short grace period if nobody checks in. Rui will draft the new wording.
- The Lisbon team will pilot a small initiative to make the office more inviting, for example by rearranging the common area. Lea will coordinate with local colleagues.
- Office-related announcements will be posted on Grove so that remote employees see them in one place. Aylin will ask the communications owner to set this up.
- The committee will not introduce any new perks or benefits at this stage. The focus stays on improving existing spaces and tools.

## 4. Action items

- Rui: update the Perch release guidance and share it with the committee for review.
- Lea: talk to Lisbon colleagues about the common-area pilot and report back.
- Aylin: arrange for office announcements to appear on Grove.
- Deniz: collect a few suggestions for the reception area and share them with Rui.
- The committee will reconvene in the new year to review progress.
