# Expenses FAQ

## 1. Questions

**What expenses can I claim?**
You can claim business-related costs that are necessary for your work, such as travel, client meetings, and work-related supplies. Personal items are not reimbursable. If you are unsure whether a cost qualifies, submit it with a brief note and our team will review it.

**How do I submit an expense report?**
Create your report in Ledgerly, attach a photo or scan of each receipt, and add a short description for every line item. Once submitted, your manager will receive a notification to approve it. After approval, the reimbursement is processed promptly.

**What if I forgot a receipt?**
For smaller amounts, a self-declaration in Ledgerly may be sufficient. For larger expenses, please contact it-help@kranich.example before submitting. We generally ask that you do your best to keep receipts for all purchases, as they help us keep records accurate.

**Can I book travel through Ledgerly?**
Yes. Use Ledgerly for flights, trains, and hotels whenever possible. Booking through the tool ensures that your travel is covered by our corporate agreements and that invoices are sent directly to us. If you book outside Ledgerly, remember to attach the receipt to your expense report.

**What about meals while traveling?**
Meals taken during business travel are reimbursable at a reasonable rate. You do not need to itemize every meal, but you should keep a simple log of when and where you ate. Alcohol is not covered.

**How long do I have to submit an expense report?**
Expense reports are due within 45 calendar days after the trip ends. Submitting on time helps us close our books smoothly and ensures you are reimbursed quickly.

**What happens if I submit late?**
If your report is late, it may be flagged for review. In most cases, we will still process it, but repeated lateness may require a conversation with your manager. If you have a genuine reason for the delay, just let us know.

**Can I claim expenses for remote work?**
Occasional costs like a coffee shop visit for a client call may be claimed, but ongoing home-office costs are not covered under this policy. For recurring needs, please check the relevant policy on Grove.

**Who approves my expenses?**
Your direct manager approves your report. For larger amounts, finance reviews it as well. If you have questions about a specific submission, reach out to people@kranich.example.

**What if my expense is denied?**
You will receive a note explaining why. Common reasons include missing receipts or personal items. You can adjust the report and resubmit, or speak with your manager if you believe the decision was made in error.

**Are there special rules for client entertainment?**
Yes. Client entertainment must have a clear business purpose, and the names of attendees should be listed in the description. Please refer to POL-004 for full details on eligible costs and documentation.

**Do rates vary by country?**
Reimbursement rates for per-diem items follow FIN-RATES-2026. If your travel takes you to a country not listed there, contact finance before your trip to confirm the applicable rate.

**Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, traveled to Berlin for a workshop. She booked her flight through Ledgerly and kept her meal receipts. After returning, she submitted her report within the required period, and her manager approved it without any follow-up questions.**
