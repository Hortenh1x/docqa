# Board Memo 5 — 2025
Access: Leadership only

## 1. Summary

The leadership team presents the quarterly board update for the final stretch of the fiscal year. We remain on track against the strategic priorities set at the beginning of the year: deepening our footprint in mid-size logistics, hardening our platform, and maturing our internal operations. This memo covers growth, cash position, principal risks, and the specific asks we bring to the board.

Overall, momentum in the market is strong. Our customers continue to expand usage of Kranich Route Cloud beyond core routing into fleet insights, which validates our product roadmap. At the same time, we are watching a handful of external factors that could affect the pace of expansion in the coming quarters.

## 2. Growth

ARR grew 43% year over year. This performance is driven by a balanced mix of new logo acquisition and expansion within the existing customer base. Our go-to-market motion in Poland and the United Kingdom has been particularly effective, and we have seen healthy adoption of the Fleet Insights module among established routing customers.

Customer success remains a key growth lever. Retention metrics are healthy, and references from long-standing customers are shortening sales cycles in our target segments. We have also begun to see cross-sell opportunities emerge naturally as customers mature in their use of the platform.

The product team shipped several releases focused on reliability and usability. Feedback from customer success managers, including Deniz in Berlin and Marek working with clients in Poland, indicates that these improvements are resonating. We expect continued investment in the Routing Core and Fleet Insights teams to sustain this trajectory into the next year.

## 3. Cash

Cash runway stands at 33 months. Our disciplined approach to hiring and spend has kept us comfortably funded without compromising on strategic priorities. We continue to operate a remote-first model, which keeps our cost base flexible across Germany, Portugal, Spain, Poland, France and the United Kingdom.

Finance, under CFO Tomasz Zieliński, has implemented tighter forecasting cycles that give us better visibility into quarterly cash consumption. Travel and expense policies remain prudent, and we have seen no material surprises in operating costs this quarter.

We are not seeking external capital at this time. The runway gives us the freedom to invest patiently in product quality and customer relationships rather than optimizing for short-term revenue acceleration at the expense of long-term value.

## 4. Risks

We see three principal risk areas.

First, concentration in the mid-size logistics segment means our growth is sensitive to the health of that sector. A prolonged downturn in freight volumes could delay procurement decisions among prospective customers. We are mitigating this by strengthening our presence in adjacent verticals and by continuing to demonstrate rapid time-to-value.

Second, talent retention in a competitive engineering market remains a watch item. Our engineering managers, including Ines Duarte in Berlin, report healthy team morale, but we are mindful that key-person risk exists in a few specialized roles. We are addressing this through clear career paths and by maintaining the collaborative culture that has served us well.

Third, we carry an increasing amount of customer data, and our security posture must keep pace. Head of Information Security Priya Nayar has led a review of our controls, and we are tracking remediation items through the internal policy framework referenced in POL-004. No material incidents occurred this quarter.

We also note that our Lisbon office, led operationally by Rui Almeida, is approaching capacity. We are evaluating workspace options there, but no decision is required from the board at this time.

## 5. Asks

We bring four asks to the board.

First, we ask for endorsement of the updated hiring plan for the Routing Core team, which will add several engineering roles across Berlin and Lisbon over the coming quarters. This aligns with the growth trajectory described in §2.

Second, we request approval to proceed with a pricing review for new customer tiers, to be completed before the end of the year. The review will be informed by customer interviews and competitive analysis; the finance team will validate the approach against the rate framework in FIN-RATES-2026.

Third, we ask the board to acknowledge the updated security roadmap presented by Priya Nayar. The roadmap prioritizes investments in access management and monitoring, with implementation phased across the next year. No additional funding is required beyond the current operating plan.

Fourth, we request the board's guidance on the timing of the next formal strategy offsite. We propose to hold it early in the next calendar year, allowing the leadership team to incorporate full-year results and external market developments into the planning cycle.

We welcome the board's questions and look forward to the discussion.
