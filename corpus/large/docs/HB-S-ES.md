# Travel Supplement — Spain

## 1. Purpose

This supplement explains how travel expenses in Spain are reimbursed for employees of Kranich Software GmbH. It complements the general travel policy in POL-004 and the country-specific rates in FIN-RATES-2026. If you are travelling to Spain for work, this document tells you what to expect when you submit your expenses in Ledgerly.

## 2. Per-diem

The per-diem for this country is in FIN-RATES-2026. That document sets the daily allowance for meals and incidental expenses. Please check it before you travel, and submit your claim promptly after your trip.

## 3. Hotel cap

When you stay in a hotel for business travel in Spain, your nightly rate is reimbursed up to the cap below. If the hotel costs more, please pay the difference yourself, unless you have prior written approval from your manager.

| Location | Cap |
| --- | --- |
| Spain | €130 per night |

Example: Sofia, a product manager who lives in Spain, books a hotel in Madrid for a client workshop. She chooses a room within the cap so she does not need to ask for approval.

## 4. Mileage

If you use your private car for business travel in Spain, mileage is reimbursed at €0.26 per km. You can claim this for trips that are not part of your regular commute. Keep a simple record of the route and the purpose of the journey.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer, drives to meet a client in Valencia. He logs the distance and claims the mileage in Ledgerly after the visit.

## 5. Questions

If you have questions about this supplement, please reach out to Finance through the usual channels. For help using Ledgerly, contact it-help@kranich.example. For anything related to wellbeing during travel, you can also speak with your manager or People & Culture.
