# Mail & Shipping

## 1. Purpose

This document explains how mail and parcels are handled at Kranich Software. It covers incoming post to our offices, outgoing shipments, and what to do when you need something sent or received on behalf of the company.

We work in a hybrid way, so mail rarely arrives for a single person at a desk. Most of what we receive is addressed to the company or to a team. This policy keeps things simple, traceable, and secure.

## 2. Incoming

Mail addressed to the Kranich offices in Berlin and Lisbon is received by Workplace & Operations. If you are expecting a personal package at the office, please let Workplace know in advance so we can watch for it.

When mail arrives, we sort it into a few categories:

- **Company correspondence** — contracts, invoices, official letters. These are scanned and routed to the right owner in Finance, Legal, or the relevant team.
- **Team packages** — hardware, marketing materials, or client gifts. These are logged in Grove and collected by a team member or delivered to the team's shared area.
- **Personal mail** — small items sent to you at the office. We hold these in the mail rack for collection. Please do not have large personal deliveries sent to the office; use your home address instead.

If you work remotely and are expecting something important, ask the sender to use your home address. We do not forward mail between offices or to private addresses as a standard practice.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, expects a client thank-you gift. She emails Workplace to let them know, and the package is logged and held at the front desk for her.

Mail that is clearly not for Kranich, or that arrives without a clear recipient, is returned to the sender or disposed of after a few days. If you see a package sitting unclaimed, please let Workplace know rather than opening it.

## 3. Outgoing

If you need to send something on behalf of Kranich, use the shipping options provided in Ledgerly. This applies to client documents, signed contracts, hardware returns, and team materials.

Before you send anything, check the following:

- Is the recipient address correct and complete?
- Does the item need a signature on delivery?
- Does it contain sensitive or personal data? If yes, see POL-004 before shipping.
- Is the item covered by our carrier's liability terms? For high-value items, contact Workplace to arrange extra protection.

For routine shipments, book the courier directly through Ledgerly and charge it to your team's cost center. For anything unusual — large pallets, international customs, or time-sensitive legal documents — contact Workplace before booking. We will help you choose the right service and complete any customs paperwork.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, needs to send a signed service agreement back to Berlin. He books a courier through Ledgerly and notes the tracking number in the relevant deal record.

Please do not use Kranich shipping accounts for personal items. If you need to send something personal, use your own carrier and pay for it yourself. Shipping personal items through company accounts creates tax complications and makes our records inaccurate.

When you send a package, share the tracking information with the recipient and with anyone in the company who needs to know. This keeps everyone aligned, especially when a shipment is time-sensitive.

## 4. Questions

If you have questions about mail handling, shipping options, or customs paperwork, reach out to Workplace & Operations. For anything that involves sensitive data, privacy, or security, contact security@kranich.example and reference this document.

For questions about how to book a shipment or claim shipping costs, see the Ledgerly guide on Grove or contact it-help@kranich.example.

For anything related to office access, deliveries to the Ritterstraße or Rua do Alecrim offices, or the mail rack, contact Workplace directly through the #workplace channel in Grove.
