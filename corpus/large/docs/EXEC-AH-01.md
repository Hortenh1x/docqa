# All-Hands Notes — January 2025

## 1. Highlights

Marta opened the first all-hands of the year with a warm welcome, noting how good it is to see everyone after the holiday break. She thanked the whole company for a strong finish to the previous year and set the tone for the months ahead: focus, kindness, and building on our momentum.

Jonas shared an update from Platform & Infrastructure. The team has been working on improving system resilience and has rolled out several internal improvements over the past weeks. He highlighted the collaborative effort between Routing Core and Platform & Infrastructure that made this possible.

Aylin reminded everyone that the annual engagement survey is coming up soon. She encouraged people to take the time to share honest feedback — it directly shapes the People & Culture roadmap for the year.

## 2. People

Aylin walked through a few People & Culture updates. The company continues to grow steadily, and we will be opening several new roles across Customer Success and Go-to-Market in the coming months. Hiring managers will share details with their teams as soon as the roles are approved.

Lea gave a brief reminder about the leave request process in Fern. She asked everyone to submit planned leave well in advance so that teams can plan coverage without stress. If anyone is unsure how to use the system, the how-to guide is available on Grove.

Aylin also announced that we will be holding a series of listening circles over the next few weeks, facilitated by People & Culture. These are open sessions where anyone can share thoughts on how we work together, what is going well, and what could be better. Participation is voluntary, and everything shared will be kept confidential.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, recently used the listening circle to suggest that the Customer Success team would benefit from closer alignment with Fleet Insights. Aylin followed up with both team leads, and they have already scheduled a recurring exchange.

## 3. Customers

Deniz and Marek jointly presented a customer highlight. A long-standing logistics operator in Poland recently expanded their use of Kranich Route Cloud to cover a new part of their fleet. Marek, who works closely with clients in Poland, described how the customer’s operations team embraced the new workflows quickly. The rollout went smoothly thanks to close collaboration between Customer Success, Go-to-Market, and Routing Core.

Sofia added a note from the product side. Fleet Insights has been gathering feedback from several customers about a new reporting feature. The feedback has been positive so far, and the team is incorporating suggestions before the broader release. Sofia thanked the customer success managers who helped arrange the feedback sessions.

Jonas reminded everyone that we should continue to direct customer feature requests through the usual internal process. This helps the product teams prioritize effectively and keeps everyone informed about what is coming next.

## 4. Questions from the floor

Marta opened the floor for questions. The following is a summary of the discussion.

**Question:** Will there be any changes to how we book time off this year?

**Answer:** Lea confirmed that the process remains the same: submit requests through Fern, and your manager will approve them. No changes are planned.

**Question:** Several of us are curious about the roadmap for the new reporting feature in Fleet Insights. When will we hear more?

**Answer:** Sofia explained that the team is targeting a release later in the year. She will share a more detailed timeline on the Fleet Insights channel in Grove once the scope is finalized.

**Question:** What is the best way to suggest improvements to our internal tools?

**Answer:** Rui encouraged everyone to post suggestions in the Workplace & Operations channel on Grove. The team reviews all suggestions regularly and will follow up if more information is needed.

**Question:** Will there be any changes to the remote work policy?

**Answer:** Aylin clarified that there are no planned changes. The current policy remains in effect, and any future updates would be communicated well in advance through the usual channels.

Marta closed the session by thanking everyone for their engagement and reminded the company that the next all-hands will be announced on Grove. She encouraged everyone to reach out to their team leads or People & Culture with any further questions.
