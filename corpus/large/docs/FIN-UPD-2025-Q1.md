# Finance Update 2025 Q1

Access: Finance only

## 1. Summary

This quarterly update provides the Finance team with a concise view of our performance in the first quarter of the calendar year. The headline is positive: our core business continues to grow steadily, and our cost base remains well managed. No figures other than the one noted in §2 are shared in this document; colleagues seeking more detail should refer to the relevant policy and rate documents, such as POL-004 and FIN-RATES-2026.

As always, this update is confidential and intended solely for internal Finance use. Please direct any questions to the Finance team via the usual channels. A more comprehensive review will follow in the next quarterly cycle.

## 2. Revenue

Revenue grew 10% year over year in 2025 Q1. This growth reflects continued momentum across our existing customer base as well as new business wins in the mid-size logistics segment. The Routing Core and Fleet Insights product lines both contributed to this result, with particular strength in renewals and expansions.

The growth is consistent with the trajectory we outlined in our planning for the year. While we do not break down figures further in this update, the underlying drivers are broad-based rather than dependent on a single large deal. Our Customer Success and Go-to-Market teams have done excellent work in supporting this performance.

We remain attentive to currency effects, given our distributed operations across Germany, Portugal, Spain, Poland, France and the United Kingdom. These effects are within expectations and do not alter the overall picture.

## 3. Costs

Our cost base remains in line with the budget set at the beginning of the year. Personnel costs, which form the largest share, are stable. We continue to hire selectively, and the pace of recruitment has been measured.

Travel and entertainment expenses are slightly elevated compared to the same period last year, reflecting a return to more in-person client meetings and internal gatherings. This is a deliberate choice and aligns with our customer-facing priorities. We do not see this as a concern.

Operational costs, including those related to our offices in Berlin (Ritterstraße) and Lisbon (Rua do Alecrim), are consistent with prior quarters. Our remote-first working model continues to keep facilities costs moderate.

We have not identified any material cost overruns. Where minor variances exist, they are being managed within the normal course of business. The Finance team will continue to monitor spending closely for the remainder of the year.

## 4. Outlook

Looking ahead, we expect the positive trend from the first quarter to continue through the rest of the year, albeit with the usual seasonal patterns. We do not anticipate any significant changes to our cost structure.

Our priority remains disciplined growth: investing in product development and customer success while maintaining a healthy margin. We will keep our hiring plans flexible and responsive to market conditions.

The next quarterly update will provide a fuller picture, including any adjustments to our internal planning. Until then, please reach out if you have questions or need additional context on anything covered here.

Thank you for your continued attention to our financial health.
