# Vacation & Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how vacation and other leave work at Kranich Software GmbH. We believe that taking time away from work helps everyone recharge, stay healthy, and do their best work. This version supersedes version 2.0, which was published earlier. We updated the policy to make the rules clearer and to reflect how we work across our offices in Berlin and Lisbon, as well as remotely. If anything in this policy conflicts with a local legal requirement in the country where you are employed, the local requirement takes precedence. Please reach out to People & Culture if you have questions about how this policy applies to you.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees of Kranich Software GmbH, regardless of whether you work from our Berlin office, our Lisbon office, or remotely from your home in Germany, Portugal, Spain, Poland, France, or the United Kingdom. It covers vacation entitlement, how to request time off, carryover rules, and special leave situations. Contractors, interns, and temporary workers are not covered by this policy; their arrangements are handled separately by their engagement contracts.

## 3. Annual vacation entitlement

Full-time employees receive 27 days of vacation per calendar year. This entitlement is calculated on a pro-rata basis for part-time employees, based on the number of hours you work per week. For example, if you work half-time, your annual vacation entitlement is half of the full-time amount.

Tenure adds one day per two full years of service, up to a total of 30 days. This means that after two full years with Kranich, you earn an additional vacation day, and after four full years you earn another one, and so on. The maximum total vacation entitlement, including tenure days, is 30 days per calendar year. Your tenure is counted from your start date as an employee, and the additional days take effect at the beginning of the calendar year following the anniversary of your start date.

You accrue vacation days throughout the calendar year. Your entitlement for a given year is available in full at the start of that year, but if you join or leave Kranich mid-year, your entitlement is adjusted pro-rata. New employees who join during the first half of the year receive the full annual entitlement; those who join in the second half receive a pro-rata amount.

Vacation days are intended for rest and personal time. You do not need to provide a reason for taking vacation, and we do not require you to document how you spend your time off.

## 4. Requesting vacation

To request vacation, submit a request through Fern, our HRIS. Your manager will receive a notification and can approve or decline the request. Please submit your request as early as possible so your team can plan around your absence.

Requests for more than five consecutive days must be submitted 4 weeks in advance. This lead time helps your team plan coverage and ensures that client-facing work continues smoothly. For shorter requests, we ask that you give your manager at least a few days of notice, though we understand that sometimes plans change at the last minute.

When you request vacation, your manager will consider the needs of the team and the workload at that time. We aim to approve all requests, but we may decline or ask you to reschedule if too many team members are away at the same time or if a critical deadline is approaching. If your request is declined, your manager will explain why and help you find an alternative date.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer who works with clients in Poland, wants to take a week off in summer. He submits his request through Fern four weeks ahead of time, and his manager approves it after confirming that no client workshops are scheduled that week.

If you need to change or cancel an approved vacation request, please update the request in Fern as soon as possible so your team can adjust their plans.

## 5. Carryover

We encourage you to use your full vacation entitlement within the calendar year. However, we understand that sometimes it is not possible, for example due to a heavy workload or an unexpected illness during your planned vacation.

Unused vacation days must be taken by March 31 of the following year. This means that if you do not use all of your vacation days by the end of the calendar year, you can carry them over into the next year, but you must use them by March 31 of that next year. Days not taken by that date are forfeited.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, has three unused vacation days at the end of the year. She carries them over and takes a long weekend in early March of the following year, well before the March 31 deadline.

If you are unable to take your carried-over days by March 31 because of a documented illness or another exceptional circumstance, please speak with your manager and People & Culture. We will handle such situations individually and with flexibility.

## 6. Special leave

In addition to vacation, Kranich offers several types of special leave. These are separate from your vacation entitlement and do not reduce your vacation days.

- **Sick leave:** If you are unwell and cannot work, please inform your manager and record your sick day in Fern. You do not need to use vacation days for illness. For absences of more than a few days, we may ask for a doctor's note, in line with local legal requirements.
- **Care leave:** If you need to care for a family member who is ill, you may take care leave. Please notify your manager as soon as possible and use the appropriate category in Fern.
- **Parental leave:** New parents are entitled to parental leave in line with the legal requirements of their country of employment. Please contact People & Culture to discuss your situation and the applicable process.
- **Bereavement leave:** If you lose a close family member, you may take bereavement leave. Please inform your manager and People & Culture, and we will support you during this difficult time.
- **Public holidays:** Public holidays are observed according to the country where you are employed. You do not need to request these as vacation days. If a public holiday falls on a day when you are already on vacation, you will not be charged a vacation day for that date.

Example: Sofia, a product manager on the Fleet Insights team who works remotely from Spain, needs to take a day to care for her child who is unwell. She records a care leave day in Fern and informs her manager in the morning. Her manager approves it, and Sofia's vacation entitlement is unaffected.

If you are unsure whether a situation qualifies for special leave, please ask your manager or People & Culture before making arrangements.

## 7. Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, please reach out to People & Culture at people@kranich.example. For questions about how to record leave in Fern, please contact IT support at it-help@kranich.example. We are happy to help you understand your entitlements and the process for requesting time off.

We review this policy regularly and may update it from time to time. When we make changes, we will communicate them through Grove, our intranet, and update the policy version accordingly. The version published on Grove is always the most current one.
