## 2. Discussion
- Rui opened with an update on the Ritterstraße office. The recent refurbishment of the ground floor is complete, and feedback from employees has been positive. He noted that the new quiet zones are being used more than expected.
- Anna shared observations from Lisbon. The Rua do Alecrim office is running low on ergonomic chairs, and a few desks have faulty power outlets. She suggested prioritizing replacements for those who spend long hours at their desks.
- Deniz raised the topic of meeting-room booking conflicts. Perch sometimes shows rooms as available when they are not, causing delays for client calls. Priya offered to coordinate with Platform & Infrastructure to investigate the sync issue.
- Lea mentioned that the Fern org chart is missing recent joiners in Customer Success. People & Culture will update the data promptly.
- Aylin led a discussion on hybrid-work norms. The committee agreed that current guidance is clear, but some teams have developed inconsistent habits. She proposed a short, plain-language reminder for the Grove intranet.
- Priya reminded the group about the upcoming security awareness campaign. She asked committee members to share it within their teams once launched.
- Marta closed the discussion by asking whether there were any workplace concerns from remote employees in Poland, France, Spain, or the United Kingdom. Marek, who works with clients in Poland, had flagged earlier that some field employees miss office-related announcements. The committee agreed to include a remote-friendly summary in future communications.

## 3. Decisions
- The faulty desks and chairs in Lisbon will be replaced; Rui will coordinate with Workplace & Operations.
- The committee will publish a short reminder on hybrid-work expectations, linked from Grove and Fern.
- Future workplace updates will include a remote-friendly summary for employees outside Berlin and Lisbon.
- Priya will follow up with Platform & Infrastructure on the Perch synchronization issue.
