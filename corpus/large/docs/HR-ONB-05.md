# Onboarding Guide — Go-to-Market

## 1. Purpose

Welcome to Kranich Software. This guide helps you settle into the Go-to-Market team during your first weeks. It covers the practical steps, the tools you will use, and the people who can answer your questions.

Go-to-Market brings together our sales, marketing, and customer-facing functions. Your colleagues work from Berlin, Lisbon, and remotely across several countries. We are a remote-first company, so most of your onboarding happens online, with clear checkpoints and friendly support along the way.

## 2. Before you start

Your manager will send you a welcome note with your start date and an invitation to your first team meeting. Please complete the following before your first day:

- Set up your Kranich account using the invitation you receive by email.
- Install 1Password and store your personal credentials there.
- Book your workspace for the first week using Perch, whether you plan to work from the Berlin office, the Lisbon office, or home.
- Review your personal details in Fern and submit any corrections promptly.
- Read the security basics in the Grove policy library. You will receive a reminder about the security training deadline — see §3 of POL-005 for details.

If anything is unclear before you start, reach out to your manager or to people@kranich.example. We would rather answer the same question twice than have you start with uncertainty.

## 3. Step by step

### First day

Your first day is about connection, not paperwork. You will meet your team in the morning video call, and your manager will walk you through the team's current priorities. You will also receive a tour of the Grove intranet, where you will find the policy library, team updates, and the org chart.

Set up your profile in Fern so that colleagues can find you. Add a short bio and your working location. If you use the office, book your desk in Perch for the week ahead.

### First week

During your first week, focus on learning how we work together. Your manager will assign you a buddy from the Go-to-Market team. Your buddy is your first point of contact for everyday questions, from tool tips to team customs.

You will also get an overview of the customer journey and how our teams collaborate. The Customer Success team will introduce themselves, and you will learn how they hand off to and from Go-to-Market.

Take time to read the relevant policies in Grove, especially those about expenses and equipment. For the equipment budget, see POL-006 §3. Order what you need through the usual process, and your manager will approve it.

### First month

By the end of your first month, you should feel comfortable with the daily rhythm. You will have joined your team's regular meetings and contributed to at least one discussion. Your manager will schedule a one-on-one to review your progress and answer any remaining questions.

You will also complete the security training mentioned earlier. The deadline is set out in §3 of POL-005. If you have not received the invitation, contact it-help@kranich.example.

During this month, you will learn about the tools we use for tracking customer interactions and opportunities. You will also get to know colleagues from the Routing Core and Fleet Insights teams, because your work will often depend on theirs.

### First quarter

In your first quarter, you will take on a small piece of real work with support from your team. This might be preparing a customer presentation, updating a marketing page, or supporting a customer call. The goal is to apply what you have learned in a safe setting.

Your manager will hold a more formal review with you, looking at your first months together and setting goals for the coming period. You will also receive feedback from your buddy and your peers.

## 4. Common questions

**Where do I find the org chart?**  
In Fern. You can search for any colleague and see their team and manager.

**How do I book a desk or a meeting room?**  
Use Perch. You can book for the day, the week, or the month, depending on what you need.

**How do I book travel or submit expenses?**  
Use Ledgerly for both. The approval flow is straightforward, and your manager will guide you through your first submission.

**Whom do I ask if I have a technical problem?**  
Write to it-help@kranich.example. For security questions, write to security@kranich.example.

**What if I need different equipment than what is offered?**  
Talk to your manager. The standard equipment list is in POL-006 §3, and your manager can explain the options within the budget.

**How do I request time off?**  
Submit your request in Fern. Your manager approves it there, and the team calendar will show when you are away.

**Example:** Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, joined during a busy period. She found that asking her buddy a short question in chat was faster than waiting for the next meeting. Her buddy also introduced her to the right people in the Platform & Infrastructure team, which saved her days of searching.

**Example:** Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, started remotely before her first office visit. She booked a desk in Perch for her first office week, and her buddy met her in person for lunch on her first day there.

## 5. Contacts

- **People & Culture** — people@kranich.example, for onboarding questions, personal data, and leave.
- **IT help** — it-help@kranich.example, for account access, equipment, and tool issues.
- **Security** — security@kranich.example, for security-related questions or concerns.

- **Your buddy** — a colleague from Go-to-Market who helps you navigate the team and the company.

We are glad you are here. Take your time, ask questions, and remember that everyone at Kranich Software was new once.
