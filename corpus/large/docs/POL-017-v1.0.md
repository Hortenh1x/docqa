# Corporate Card Policy

## 1. Purpose

The corporate card is a convenient way to pay for business-related expenses when you are travelling for Kranich, hosting a client, or buying something for your team. This policy explains who can get a card, how to use it responsibly, and what you need to do to keep your expenses in order.

We want you to have what you need to do your job well without paying out of your own pocket and waiting for reimbursement. The corporate card is designed to make that easier. At the same time, we are accountable to each other and to the company, so every purchase made with a corporate card must be a legitimate business expense, properly documented and approved.

If you are unsure whether a purchase is appropriate, ask your manager or the Finance team before you use the card. A quick question is always better than an expense that has to be explained later.

## 2. Who gets a card

Corporate cards are issued to employees whose role regularly requires them to make business purchases. This typically includes people in Customer Success, Go-to-Market, and Workplace & Operations, as well as managers who need to buy supplies for their teams.

You do not need to ask for a card just because you travel occasionally. If your travel is infrequent, you can book through Ledgerly and use your personal card for incidental costs, then submit an expense report for reimbursement. If you find that you are travelling or making purchases for Kranich more than a few times a year, talk to your manager. They can request a card on your behalf.

Cards are issued in the name of the employee, not in the name of a team or a role. This means that when you receive a card, you are personally responsible for safeguarding it and for ensuring that every transaction made with it is a valid business expense. If you change roles within Kranich, your card remains valid unless your manager or Finance decides that you no longer need it. If you leave Kranich, you must return the card to Finance on your last working day.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, visits clients regularly and hosts them for lunch. Her manager requests a corporate card for her so that she can pay for those meetings directly. Marek, a field solutions engineer who works with clients in Poland, also travels often and receives a card. Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, rarely makes business purchases, so she does not have a card and books any necessary travel through Ledgerly.

## 3. Using the card

Use the corporate card only for business purposes. Personal purchases are not allowed, even if you intend to reimburse the company later. If you accidentally use the card for a personal item, contact Finance immediately so that the charge can be corrected.

The card can be used for the following types of expenses:

- Flights, trains, and accommodation booked for business travel
- Meals with clients or partners, when you are hosting
- Team supplies and small equipment needed for your work
- Conference fees and other business-related registrations
- Other expenses that your manager or Finance has approved in advance

When you use the card, choose the most reasonable option for the situation. For travel, book standard economy flights and modest accommodation. For client meals, choose a restaurant that is appropriate for the business context but not extravagant. If you are unsure whether a cost is reasonable, ask your manager before you spend.

Do not use the card to withdraw cash. Cash advances are not permitted under any circumstances. Do not use the card to pay for another person's personal expenses, even if they are a colleague. If you are travelling with a colleague and need to pay a shared bill, one of you can pay with the corporate card and the other can settle the difference separately.

If your card is lost or stolen, report it to Finance as soon as you notice. Finance will cancel the card and arrange a replacement. You are not liable for unauthorised charges made after you have reported the loss, but you must take reasonable care of the card at all times.

Example: Sofia, a product manager in Fleet Insights, attends a client workshop in Spain. She uses her corporate card to pay for her train ticket and for a team dinner with the client. She does not use the card for sightseeing tickets or personal shopping during the trip.

## 4. Receipts and reconciliation

Every transaction made with the corporate card must be reconciled in Ledgerly. You are responsible for reconciling your own transactions promptly, even if someone else made the purchase on your behalf.

For each transaction, you must provide a receipt that shows the date, the merchant, and the amount. Digital receipts are acceptable. If you do not have a receipt, you must provide a written explanation of the purchase and ask your manager to approve it. Transactions without receipts or explanations may be treated as personal expenses and deducted from your next payroll run.

Reconciliation should happen as you go, not all at once at the end of the month. When you return from a trip or complete a purchase, take a few minutes to upload the receipt and add a short note explaining the business purpose. A note such as "client meeting in Warsaw" or "team lunch for Routing Core" is usually enough.

Your manager is responsible for approving your reconciled expenses. Finance reviews all corporate card activity regularly and may ask you to clarify a transaction at any time. Please respond to these requests promptly so that our books stay accurate.

If you notice a charge on your card that you did not make, contact Finance right away. Do not wait until the end of the month.

Example: Lea, a people operations specialist in Berlin, buys name badges for an onboarding day using her corporate card. She uploads the receipt to Ledgerly the same afternoon and writes "badges for onboarding" in the note field, then her manager approves it within a few days.

## 5. Limits and approvals

Access: Finance only

The single-transaction limit on the corporate card is €1,100. This means that no single purchase may exceed €1,100 without prior approval from Finance.

Monthly spend per card is capped at €4,500. If your spending in a calendar month reaches this cap, the card will stop working until the next month begins or until Finance raises the limit for a specific reason.

If you need to make a purchase above the single-transaction limit, or if you expect your monthly spending to exceed the cap, contact Finance before you make the purchase. Finance may approve a temporary increase for a specific transaction or for a specific period. Approval must be obtained in advance; purchases made over the limit without prior approval will not be reimbursed automatically and may need to be returned or refunded.

Your manager does not have the authority to raise your card limit. Only Finance can adjust limits, and they will do so only when there is a clear business need.

Example: Ines Duarte, an engineering manager in Berlin, wants to book a training course for her team that costs more than the single-transaction limit. She emails Finance with the details of the course and the number of participants, and Finance approves the purchase before she uses the card.

## 6. Questions

If you have questions about this policy, contact the Finance team. They can help you understand whether a purchase is appropriate, how to reconcile a transaction, or what to do if your card is lost or stolen.

For questions about booking travel or submitting expenses, refer to the guidance in Ledgerly or contact it-help@kranich.example for technical issues with the tool.

If you believe you need a corporate card but do not have one, speak to your manager first. They will confirm that your role requires it and will submit a request to Finance on your behalf.

We want this policy to be easy to follow. If something is unclear, please ask rather than guess. The Finance team is happy to help.
