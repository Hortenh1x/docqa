# Announcement: Office closure over the holidays

## 1. Announcement

Dear colleagues,

We are writing to let you know that both our offices — the Ritterstraße office in Berlin and the Rua do Alecrim office in Lisbon — will be closed for a period over the upcoming holiday season. This closure applies to everyone, regardless of where you usually work.

During this time, the offices will be unavailable for in-person work, meetings, or events. If you normally work from an office, please plan to work remotely for the closure period, as long as your role and client commitments allow. If your role requires you to be on-site for operational reasons, your manager will discuss this with you individually and well in advance.

This is a company-wide closure, not individual leave. You do not need to submit a leave request in Fern for the days the offices are closed. Please make sure, however, that any personal leave you wish to take around this period is recorded in Fern as usual, following the process described in the relevant People & Culture policy.

As always, we ask you to be mindful of client needs. If you support a client whose operations continue during the closure, please coordinate with your manager and the Customer Success team to ensure coverage. We trust you to manage this sensibly and to communicate early with anyone who depends on your work.

The official closure dates and any region-specific arrangements will be shared closer to the time. Please keep an eye on Grove for updates.

We hope this early notice gives you enough time to plan your holidays and time with family and friends. Thank you for another great year — we look forward to seeing everyone rested and recharged in the new year.

Questions? Reach out to people@kranich.example. For anything related to office access or security during the closure, please contact it-help@kranich.example or security@kranich.example.

With warm wishes,

People & Culture
