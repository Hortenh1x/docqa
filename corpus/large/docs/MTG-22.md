# Meeting Notes — Security council (2025-11-13)

## 1. Attendees

- Priya (chair)
- Jonas
- Marta
- Anna (joining remotely from Lisbon)
- Marek (representing field work with clients in Poland)
- Aylin

## 2. Discussion

- Priya opened with a recap of the recent phishing simulation results. While the click rate has improved, she noted that reporting speed remains inconsistent across teams.
- Anna raised a concern about the onboarding flow for new engineers: access to internal tools (Perch, 1Password, Fern) is granted promptly, but the revocation process after a team change is manual and occasionally delayed.
- Marek described a client site visit where he needed to share a screen with sensitive route data. He asked whether the current VPN policy covers temporary guest networks.
- Jonas suggested that Platform & Infrastructure could automate access reviews using the org chart in Fern as the source of truth.
- Aylin mentioned that People & Culture is happy to include a security checklist in the offboarding process, but needs a clear owner for each step.
- The group discussed the classification labels used in Grove. Several attendees felt the labels are not intuitive and that people default to "internal" even for draft documents.
- Priya reminded everyone that the security council meets quarterly and that minutes are stored in Grove for transparency.
- Marta asked whether the council should coordinate with the Customer Success team on security-related messaging to clients. The group agreed that clear communication would build trust.

## 3. Decisions

- Access reviews for internal tools will be automated and tied to role changes in Fern.
- The revocation process for departing or changing employees will include a checklist owned by the employee's manager, with oversight by IT.
- A short guidance note on using VPNs on untrusted networks will be published in Grove, aimed at field engineers and anyone working from client sites.
- The document classification labels in Grove will be simplified. Priya will propose a draft for the next council meeting.
- Security-related questions from clients will be routed through Customer Success, with Priya's team as the escalation point.

## 4. Action items

- Anna to draft the automated access review requirements and share them with Jonas.
- Marek to write the VPN guidance note and send it to Priya for review.
- Aylin to update the offboarding checklist in Fern and coordinate with IT.
- Priya to prepare the classification label proposal before the next council meeting.
- Jonas to confirm the timeline for implementing the access review automation with Platform & Infrastructure.
