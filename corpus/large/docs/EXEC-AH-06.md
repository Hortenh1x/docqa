# All-Hands Notes — November 2025

## 1. Highlights

Marta opened the session by reflecting on a strong autumn for Kranich. The Routing Core team has completed the final phase of the route-optimization engine refresh, and early feedback from pilot customers has been encouraging. Platform & Infrastructure has also made steady progress on improving deployment reliability, which will benefit every team that ships to production.

Cristina from Customer Success shared a short demo of the new onboarding dashboard. It gives new customers a clearer view of their setup progress. The team plans to roll it out to all new customers in the coming months.

Jonas closed the first part by thanking everyone who contributed to the internal hack week earlier this year. Several prototypes from that week are now being evaluated for the product roadmap.

## 2. People

Aylin announced that Kranich now has **145 people**. Growth has been steady across engineering, customer success, and go-to-market. She thanked the hiring teams for keeping the process thoughtful and respectful of candidates' time.

Lea gave an update on the annual engagement survey. Participation was strong, and the overall sentiment is positive. The main themes that emerged were a desire for clearer career paths and more cross-team collaboration. People & Culture will work with the leadership team and managers to turn these themes into concrete actions over the coming quarters.

Aylin also reminded everyone about the upcoming holiday season. Team leads should plan for coverage early and encourage people to take their earned leave. If you have questions about leave balances, please reach out to people@kranich.example or check Fern.

Finally, Aylin welcomed the newest members of the People team and reminded everyone that the org chart in Fern is always up to date.

## 3. Customers

Deniz shared highlights from the recent customer advisory board meeting in Berlin. Customers appreciate the stability of the platform and the responsiveness of the Customer Success team. The main asks were for better reporting exports and more flexible rules for time windows. Both are now on the product roadmap for next year.

Marek reported from the field in Poland. One mid-size logistics operator has been using Kranich Route Cloud for a few weeks and has already simplified its daily planning process. The client's dispatchers are particularly happy with the new map view, which makes it easier to spot potential delays.

Sofia noted that Fleet Insights has seen growing interest from existing customers who want to combine route optimization with telematics data. The team is running a small pilot with a handful of customers to understand which insights matter most. The results will inform the roadmap for the next release.

Priya reminded everyone that customer data protection remains a top priority. If you receive a security-related question from a customer, please direct it to security@kranich.example rather than answering directly. The Information Security team is happy to support you in customer conversations.

## 4. Questions from the floor

**Q: Will the onboarding dashboard be available to all customers at the same time?**
A: No, we will roll it out gradually. Customer Success will prioritize based on customer need and readiness. We will communicate the plan before the rollout begins.

**Q: What is the status of the route-optimization engine refresh?**
A: Routing Core has completed the core work. We are now in the stabilization phase and will share a timeline for general availability once testing is complete. See §3 for related customer feedback.

**Q: How can I get involved in cross-team collaboration?**
A: Aylin and the People team will publish a few options in the coming weeks. In the meantime, you can reach out to your manager or to people@kranich.example if you have a specific idea.

**Q: Will there be an end-of-year celebration?**
A: Yes, Rui and the Workplace team are planning something. Details will be shared on Grove and through the usual channels.

**Q: I have a question about the expense policy for travel next year.**
A: Please check the latest guidance in Ledgerly or refer to FIN-RATES-2026. For anything not covered there, reach out to Finance.

Marta closed by thanking everyone for their focus and care during a busy quarter. The next all-hands will take place in the new year.
