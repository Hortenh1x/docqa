# Company OKRs — Q1 2025

Access: Managers only

## 1. Objectives

This quarter, Kranich Software focuses on three objectives that build on the foundation we laid in the previous year. First, we want to accelerate the adoption of Kranich Route Cloud among mid-size logistics operators in our core European markets. Second, we aim to strengthen the reliability and performance of our platform so that customers experience fewer disruptions as their fleets grow. Third, we will mature our internal ways of working to support the company's next phase of scaling, keeping our remote-first culture intact.

These objectives reflect conversations across the Leadership Team, with input from the Customer Success and Go-to-Market teams. Marta Lindqvist, Jonas Weber and Tomasz Zieliński reviewed the priorities together and agreed that growth, reliability and organizational health are the right areas of focus for the first quarter of the calendar year. We believe these objectives are ambitious yet achievable, and they set the tone for the rest of the year.

## 2. Key results

For the first objective, our primary key result is to achieve 40% growth in active fleets compared with the end of the previous year. This is a demanding target, and we will track progress monthly through the Fleet Insights dashboard.

Supporting key results for the growth objective include improving the onboarding completion rate for new customers, expanding our presence in Poland and France through targeted field activities led by our solutions engineers, and shortening the time from first demo to signed contract. We will measure these through the standard reporting in Perch and the CRM.

For the reliability objective, the key results center on reducing platform incidents that affect customer operations and on improving our mean time to resolve any incident that does occur. The Platform & Infrastructure team will drive this work, with clear escalation paths documented in our internal runbooks.

For the organizational health objective, key results focus on employee engagement, cross-team collaboration and manager effectiveness. We will measure engagement through the pulse survey administered in Fern, and we will track the completion of the leadership development program that Aylin Demir's team launched.

## 3. Owners

## 4. Review

We will review progress against these OKRs at the monthly Leadership Team meeting. The first review will take place in the early part of the quarter, and subsequent reviews will follow at regular intervals. Each owner will come prepared with a short status update, highlighting any risks or dependencies.

If a key result appears off track, we will address it promptly rather than waiting for the end of the quarter. Owners may adjust their approach, reallocate effort across teams, or escalate to the Leadership Team for guidance. We will also share a summary of progress with all managers after each review, so that they can communicate relevant updates to their teams.

At the end of the quarter, the Leadership Team will assess whether we met the objectives and will use the lessons learned to shape the OKRs for the next quarter. We encourage managers to bring feedback from their teams to these conversations, so that our planning remains grounded in the reality of daily work.
