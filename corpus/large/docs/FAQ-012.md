# Meeting Rooms FAQ

## 1. Questions

**How do I book a meeting room?**
Use Perch, our internal tool for room and desk booking. Select the office, pick a room that fits your needs, and confirm the slot. You will receive a calendar invite automatically.

**What if no room is available at my preferred time?**
Check Perch again a few hours later — cancellations happen regularly. You can also join the waitlist for a room; if it becomes free, Perch will notify you promptly.

**Can I book a room for someone else?**
Yes. When booking in Perch, add the other person as the organizer or as a required attendee. The room will be tied to the meeting, not to an individual.

**How far in advance can I make a booking?**
Perch allows bookings within the current month and the upcoming month. For larger events or all-hands, contact Workplace & Operations through the Grove portal.

**What should I do if I am running late?**
If you will not use the room within a few minutes of the start time, release it in Perch so others can book it. If you expect to be late but still need the room, keep the booking and inform your attendees.

**Can I book a room for a hybrid meeting?**
Yes. Every room in both the Berlin and Lisbon offices is equipped for video conferencing. When booking, indicate that remote participants will join so the room is set up accordingly.

**Are there quiet rooms for focused work?**
Some rooms are designated as quiet or focus rooms. You can filter for them in Perch. These rooms are intended for individual deep work, not for meetings with multiple people.

**Can I eat or drink in meeting rooms?**
Drinks with lids are fine. For food, please check the room description in Perch — some rooms are marked as food-friendly, others are not. Always leave the room as you found it.

**What if the room equipment is not working?**
Contact it-help@kranich.example or use the IT help channel in Grove. If the issue is urgent and another room is free, you may move your meeting and update the booking in Perch.

**Can I book a room outside my home office?**
Yes. If you are visiting the other office, you can book a room there just like a local colleague would. The same booking rules apply.

**How do I report a room that was left untidy?**
Let Workplace & Operations know through Grove. Please include the room name and the date. We rely on everyone to keep shared spaces welcoming.

**Where can I find the room booking policy?**
See POL-004 for room usage guidelines and FIN-RATES-2026 for any cost-related questions about external meeting spaces.

**Example:** Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, books a focus room for a deep-work session. Her meeting is cancelled, so she releases the room in Perch immediately, allowing a colleague to book it for an upcoming client call.
