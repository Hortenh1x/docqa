# Travel Booking How-To

## 1. Purpose

This guide explains how to book business travel through Ledgerly, our expenses and travel tool. It is written for everyone at Kranich who travels for work — whether you are visiting a client, attending a conference, or meeting colleagues at one of our offices.

Booking through Ledgerly keeps everything in one place: your itinerary, your receipts, and your expense report. It also makes sure that Finance can reconcile costs promptly and that you get reimbursed without back-and-forth.

If you are unsure whether a trip should be booked at all, talk to your manager before you book. For questions about what counts as a travel expense, see the travel policy in POL-004.

## 2. Ledgerly travel module

Ledgerly has a dedicated travel module. You reach it from the Ledgerly home screen, under "Travel". From there you can search for rail, flights, and hotels, and book them directly.

When you book through Ledgerly, the cost is charged to the company automatically. You do not pay out of pocket and you do not need to submit a separate expense claim for the booking itself. Incidental costs during the trip — such as meals or local transport — are handled through the regular expense flow in Ledgerly.

Your booking is linked to your profile, so the tool already knows your name, your home office, and your usual preferences. If any of those details are wrong, update them in Fern first, then contact it-help@kranich.example if the change does not show up in Ledgerly within a few days.

## 3. Rail and flights

For trips within Germany and to neighbouring countries, rail is the preferred option. The rail-over-flight rule is described in POL-004 §3. Please read that section before you book a flight for a short-haul route.

When you search for a train in Ledgerly, the tool shows you connections that fit the purpose of your trip. Choose a departure that gives you enough buffer for the journey to the station and for any delays. Booking a flexible ticket is recommended when your schedule may shift; the price difference is usually worth the peace of mind.

For flights, Ledgerly shows you options from all major carriers serving the route. You are free to choose the airline, but consider the total travel time, not just the fare. A slightly more expensive flight that avoids an overnight layover is often the better choice.

Always book through Ledgerly rather than directly with the carrier or a third-party site. If you book outside the tool, the company may not be able to cover the cost, and you will need to explain the exception when you submit your expense report.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, needs to visit a client site that is not well served by rail. He uses Ledgerly to compare flights and selects one that lets him arrive the evening before the meeting, so he is rested and prepared.

## 4. Hotels

You can book hotels through the same Ledgerly travel module. The tool shows properties near your destination, with a filter for the company's preferred hotels where available.

Choose a hotel that is reasonably close to where you will be working, so you do not spend excessive time commuting. The room should be a standard single room. Upgrades are at your own expense unless your manager has approved them in advance.

If you are travelling with a colleague and prefer to share a room, that is fine — book one room through Ledgerly and note the colleague's name in the booking comments. Do not book two rooms and ask for one to be cancelled later.

Breakfast is included in most corporate rates shown in Ledgerly. If it is not included, you can claim it as a meal expense through the regular flow. Incidentals such as minibar, pay-per-view, or laundry are personal costs and will not be reimbursed.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, travels to a client workshop in another city. She books a hotel through Ledgerly that is within walking distance of the client's office. The hotel offers a standard rate with breakfast, which keeps her expense report simple.

## 5. Questions

If you have questions about how to use the Ledgerly travel module, write to it-help@kranich.example. The team responds promptly during working hours.

For questions about what is covered, about the rail-over-flight rule, or about how travel expenses interact with your role, please reach out to Finance. For anything related to your personal data in Ledgerly or Fern, contact people@kranich.example.

For reimbursement rates that apply to your destination, see FIN-RATES-2026. That document is updated each year and should be your reference before you travel.

If you notice a problem with a booking — a cancelled train, a missed connection, or a hotel overbooking — contact it-help@kranich.example right away. Do not try to rebook on your own and then ask for a refund later; the team can handle changes more quickly through the tool.

We want travelling for Kranich to be straightforward. Booking through Ledgerly is the first step — thank you for following this guide.
