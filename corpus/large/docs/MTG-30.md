# Meeting Notes — Security council (2025-07-10)

## 1. Attendees

Priya (chair), Jonas, Marta, Deniz, and Anna. Marek and Ines joined remotely for the second half.

## 2. Discussion

- Priya opened with a recap of the recent internal phishing simulation. Participation was broad across teams, and results show we should refresh our awareness materials before the next cycle.
- Anna raised a concern about secrets stored in local development environments. Several engineers keep credentials in plain text for convenience. The team agreed this is a real risk, especially for those working from home.
- Deniz shared feedback from a customer who asked how we handle data during onboarding. The council discussed whether our customer-facing security documentation is current and easy to understand.
- Jonas noted that the Platform & Infrastructure team is planning a routine review of access permissions for internal tools, including Perch and Ledgerly. The review should also cover 1Password vault access.
- Marek mentioned that some field work with clients in Poland involves sharing screens. The council discussed guidance for avoiding accidental exposure of internal data during client demos.
- Priya reminded everyone that the security incident response plan lives on Grove and should be reviewed by each team lead. She asked that any suggested edits be sent to the security alias.
- The group briefly touched on the upcoming change in the org chart. Lea will coordinate with Priya to ensure that access rights are updated promptly when people move teams.

## 3. Decisions

- The council will run a short, informal security refresher for all employees before the next scheduled simulation. No date was fixed; it will happen within a few weeks.
- Anna will draft a short guide on safe handling of secrets in local development. The guide will be published on Grove after review by Priya.
- Access permission reviews for internal tools will become a recurring agenda item for this council. Jonas will propose a simple review cadence at the next meeting.
- Customer-facing security documentation will be updated by the Customer Success team with support from Priya. Deniz will lead the effort.

## 4. Action items

- Anna: draft the local development secrets guide and share it with Priya for review.
- Deniz: collect feedback from customers on the current security documentation and propose edits.
- Jonas: prepare a proposed cadence for access reviews and bring it to the next council meeting.
- Priya: schedule the security refresher and coordinate with Lea on access updates related to upcoming team changes.
- Marek: share notes on screen-sharing best practices for client work with Anna, so they can be included in the guide.
