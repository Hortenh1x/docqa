# Procurement FAQ

## 1. Questions

**Who do I contact when I need to buy something for work?**
Start with your team lead. For anything beyond a small, everyday purchase, your team lead will connect you with the appropriate person in Workplace & Operations. For software or hardware that touches our systems, Platform & Infrastructure reviews the request first.

**Is there a formal process?**
Yes. For purchases above the thresholds described in POL-004, you need approval before you order. The policy explains who approves what and which steps to follow. For smaller items, a quick confirmation from your team lead is enough.

**What do I do if a supplier asks me to sign something?**
Do not sign. Only people with signing authority per POL-004 may commit Kranich Software to a contract. If a supplier sends you terms, forward them to your team lead or to Workplace & Operations, who will route them correctly.

**Which payment method should I use?**
Use the corporate card or the procurement process described in POL-004 whenever possible. Avoid paying out of pocket. If you have an exceptional case, talk to your team lead before you spend anything. Reimbursements are handled through Ledgerly and follow the expense policy.

**Can I buy software or an online service myself?**
No. All software subscriptions, even free trials, must go through Platform & Infrastructure. This keeps our tooling consistent and secure. If you sign up for a service without approval, you may be asked to stop using it and to transfer or cancel the account.

**How long does approval take?**
It depends on the item and the approver. Simple requests are usually handled promptly. Larger or unusual requests take longer, especially if several teams need to review them. If you are waiting, check in with your team lead rather than chasing the approver directly.

**What happens if I order something without approval?**
Orders placed without following POL-004 are not automatically covered. You may be asked to return the item or to cover the cost if it cannot be returned. Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer, once ordered a device directly from a vendor for a client demo. The device was not returnable, and because he had skipped the approval step, the cost was not reimbursed.

**Can I buy gifts or entertainment for clients?**
Client-related spending is covered by the expense policy, not the procurement policy. Check the guidance in FIN-RATES-2026 for what is allowed, and keep receipts and a short note about the business purpose.

**What about furniture or equipment for my home office?**
Workplace & Operations handles home-office setup. Reach out to them first — they may already have a standard setup or a preferred supplier. If you need something special, explain why, and they will let you know whether it is covered.

**Where do I find the full rules?**
POL-004 is the main procurement policy. Your team lead can also point you to internal guides on Grove. When in doubt, ask before you buy.
