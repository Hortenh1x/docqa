# Procurement Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how to purchase goods and services on behalf of Kranich Software GmbH. Whether you need a new monitor for your home office, a software license for your team, or catering for a customer workshop, this policy helps you get what you need quickly and responsibly.

We want every purchase to be transparent, fair, and aligned with our budget. By following the steps below, you help Finance keep accurate records and ensure we spend company money wisely.

This policy applies to all employees, regardless of where they work. If you are based in Berlin, Lisbon, or working remotely from another country, the same rules apply.

## 2. Requesting a purchase

Before you buy anything, check whether the item or service is already available. For software, look in the internal tool catalog on Grove. For hardware, ask your manager or the Workplace team — we may have spare equipment in the Berlin or Lisbon offices.

If the item is not available internally, submit a purchase request through Ledgerly. You will need to describe what you are buying, why you need it, and which budget it should be charged to. Attach a quote or a link to the product page so Finance can verify the price.

Do not place an order before your request is approved. If you order first and ask later, the expense may not be reimbursed, and you could be personally liable for the cost.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, wants to send a welcome package to a new client. She checks Grove for a preferred supplier, submits a request in Ledgerly with the item link, and waits for approval before placing the order.

## 3. Approval thresholds

Access: Finance only

Purchases up to €600 are approved by your manager. Your manager will review the request in Ledgerly and either approve or reject it. If approved, you can proceed with the purchase.

For purchases above €600 and up to €6,000, your manager's approval is not enough. The request must also be reviewed by the head of your department. This second level of review ensures larger commitments are aligned with departmental priorities.

Purchases above €6,000 require CFO approval. Tomasz Zieliński or a delegate in the Finance team will review the request. In most cases, Finance will also ask whether the purchase was included in the annual budget planning. If it was not, we may ask you to explain the business case in more detail.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, needs a specialized device for a pilot project. The device costs more than €6,000, so his request goes to the CFO for approval. Marek includes a short note about the client's expected usage so the review is straightforward.

When you submit a request, Ledgerly automatically routes it to the right approvers based on the amount. You do not need to chase anyone manually. If your request is urgent, mention that in the notes field, and the approver will try to respond promptly.

Always use the same currency in your request as the supplier's invoice. If you are buying from a supplier outside the eurozone, Finance will handle the currency conversion at the time of payment.

## 4. Preferred suppliers

For certain categories, Kranich Software has negotiated agreements with preferred suppliers. You can find the current list on Grove under "Procurement". Using a preferred supplier saves time because contracts and payment terms are already in place.

If you want to use a supplier that is not on the preferred list, that is allowed. Please explain in your purchase request why you chose this supplier. Common reasons include a better feature fit, a more competitive price, or a supplier that is local to the client you are serving.

For software subscriptions, check first whether the tool is already licensed somewhere in the company. The Platform & Infrastructure team maintains a list of approved tools. If you need a new tool, you may also need a security review by the Information Security team. Refer to the security assessment process documented in POL-004 for guidance.

For travel and accommodation, use the booking features in Ledgerly. The system shows options that comply with our travel guidelines. If you book outside the system, reimbursement may be delayed.

Example: Sofia, a product manager working remotely from Spain, needs a new ergonomic keyboard. She checks the preferred supplier list on Grove, finds an approved vendor, and submits her request. Her manager approves it the same day because the amount is within the manager approval threshold.

## 5. Questions

If you are unsure whether a purchase is allowed, ask before you buy. For questions about this policy, contact the Finance team through Ledgerly or reach out to it-help@kranich.example for help with the system.

For questions about budget availability, speak to your manager or the Finance contact for your team. They can tell you whether funds are available in the current fiscal year.

If you believe a purchase is necessary but does not fit the standard process, explain the situation in your request. Finance will review it case by case. We would rather answer a question than process a rejected expense later.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, wants to attend a conference that is not on the approved list. She submits a request with a note explaining the learning value for her role. Her manager approves it, and the purchase is processed through the standard workflow.

Thank you for following this policy. Your care with procurement keeps our spending transparent and helps Kranich Software grow sustainably.
