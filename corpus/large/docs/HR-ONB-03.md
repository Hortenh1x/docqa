# Onboarding Guide — Platform & Infrastructure

## 1. Purpose

Welcome to Kranich Software! This guide helps you get started in the Platform & Infrastructure team. Whether you join us as an engineer, a site reliability specialist, or in another role on the team, this document walks you through your first days and weeks.

Platform & Infrastructure keeps the Kranich Route Cloud running reliably for our customers. We build and maintain the foundation that Routing Core and Fleet Insights rely on. Your manager will help you understand your specific responsibilities within the team.

We are remote-first. You may work from home in your country of employment, or from one of our offices in Berlin or Lisbon. Your manager will confirm your working arrangement during your first week.

## 2. Before you start

Before your first day, People & Culture will send you a welcome email with practical details. Please complete the following before you join:

- Set up your personal device if you received one. If you have not received equipment yet, contact your manager promptly — the equipment budget is described in POL-006 §3.
- Complete the security training before your start date. The deadline is stated in §3 of POL-005. If you have trouble accessing the training, write to security@kranich.example.
- Check that you can log in to your Kranich email account. If you cannot, contact it-help@kranich.example.

Your manager will schedule a short welcome call for your first day. This call is a chance to meet your team, ask questions, and agree on a plan for your first weeks.

## 3. Step by step

### Your first day

- Join the team welcome call with your manager and colleagues.
- Set up your accounts in our internal tools. You will receive invitations by email.
- Read the team page on Grove, our intranet and policy library. It contains useful information about how we work, our rituals, and who to contact for what.
- Introduce yourself in the team channel. Share a few words about your background and your role.
- Review the security policy POL-005 and complete any remaining training steps.

### Your first week

- Meet your manager for a one-on-one. Discuss your role, expectations, and how your work connects to the team's goals.
- Meet colleagues from Platform & Infrastructure and, if useful, from Routing Core and Fleet Insights.
- Explore our code repositories and infrastructure documentation on Grove.
- Set up your local development environment. Ask a teammate for help if you get stuck — we value asking early.
- Book your recurring team meetings in your calendar. Use Perch if you book a desk or meeting room at an office.

### Your first month

- Work on small tasks with close support from your team. This helps you learn our systems and workflows.
- Shadow a teammate during an on-call shift, if your role involves on-call duties.
- Learn about our incident response process and where to document incidents.
- Review the equipment budget guidance in POL-006 §3 if you need additional tools or accessories.
- Schedule a check-in with your manager to review progress and agree on goals.

### Your first months

- Take on more substantial projects with increasing independence.
- Contribute to team discussions about architecture, reliability, and tooling.
- Build relationships with colleagues in Customer Success and Go-to-Market if your work touches their areas.
- Discuss your development goals with your manager. We support continuous learning within your role.

Throughout onboarding, your manager is your main point of contact. Do not hesitate to ask questions — no question is too small.

## 4. Common questions

**Where do I find the policies and procedures?**

All policies are on Grove, our intranet and policy library. You will find the security policy under POL-005 and the equipment policy under POL-006. If you cannot find something, ask people@kranich.example.

**How do I request equipment or accessories?**

Speak with your manager first. They will guide you through the process described in POL-006 §3. For urgent issues, such as a broken laptop, contact it-help@kranich.example.

**Which tools do I need to install?**

You will use Perch for meeting-room and desk booking, 1Password for password management, Fern for leave requests and personal data, Ledgerly for expenses and travel, and Grove for policies and internal information. Your manager or a teammate will help you get set up.

**How do I request leave?**

Use Fern. Your manager will explain the team's leave planning process. For questions about leave, contact people@kranich.example.

**What if I have a security question or concern?**

Contact security@kranich.example. For general IT issues, such as login problems or software access, write to it-help@kranich.example.

**How do I book a desk or meeting room in an office?**

Use Perch. You can book a desk for a day or a meeting room for a specific time. If you have questions about the office, contact Rui Almeida, Head of Workplace & Operations.

**Example:** Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, joined Platform & Infrastructure recently. On her first day, she could not access a shared repository. She wrote to it-help@kranich.example and received help within a few hours. Her manager also introduced her to a teammate who explained the team's deployment process. Within her first week, Anna felt confident navigating the main tools and knew whom to ask for help.

**Example:** Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, sometimes needs access to infrastructure dashboards to help a customer. She asked a Platform & Infrastructure colleague for a brief walkthrough. The team shared a short guide on Grove that explains which dashboards exist and who to contact for access. This saved Deniz time and reduced back-and-forth.
