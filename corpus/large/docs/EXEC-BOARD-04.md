# Board Memo 4 — 2025
Access: Leadership only

## 1. Summary

The company enters the second half of 2025 in a strong position. Our focus on mid-size logistics operators continues to pay off, and we have maintained healthy growth without compromising our balance sheet. This memo summarizes the key developments since the last board meeting, our current cash position, the principal risks we monitor, and the specific items on which we seek board input.

Operationally, the Routing Core and Fleet Insights teams have delivered on their roadmaps, and Customer Success has kept churn low through proactive engagement. We have also made progress on internal efficiency, particularly in how Platform & Infrastructure supports our remote-first way of working.

## 2. Growth

ARR grew 27% year over year. This performance was driven by a balanced mix of new logo acquisition and expansion within our existing customer base. The Polish market, where Marek works closely with clients, has been a notable contributor, as has the broader Central European region.

Our go-to-market motion remains disciplined. We have not increased headcount in sales roles; instead, we have improved conversion by refining our demo process and by having field solutions engineers involved earlier in the sales cycle. This has shortened the time from first contact to signed contract without adding cost.

Product-led growth is beginning to show early signs. A small but meaningful share of new customers now start with a self-serve trial before engaging with sales. We are watching this trend carefully and will decide later in the year whether to invest more in that channel.

Fleet Insights, led by Sofia, has seen strong adoption among existing customers. The feature that helps dispatchers compare planned versus actual routes has become a talking point in customer reviews, and we expect it to support further expansion revenue.

## 3. Cash

Cash runway stands at 31 months. We have not needed to draw on any external financing, and our monthly burn remains within the range we presented at the last board meeting.

We continue to manage expenses conservatively. Travel and events budgets are reviewed quarterly, and we have kept our office footprint stable — the Ritterstraße office in Berlin and the Rua do Alecrim office in Lisbon remain our only leased spaces. Most employees work remotely within their country of employment, which keeps real estate costs predictable.

Our policy on expenses, as documented in FIN-RATES-2026, remains the reference for all spending decisions. We have seen no material deviations from that policy.

We are not planning any significant capital expenditure for the remainder of the year. The main investment areas — engineering talent and customer success capacity — are funded from operating cash flow.

## 4. Risks

We monitor a small set of risks closely.

First, concentration risk in our customer base. While no single customer accounts for a dominant share of revenue, our top tier of customers is concentrated in the logistics sector, which is sensitive to macroeconomic cycles. We mitigate this by continuing to diversify across sub-segments such as last-mile delivery, field service, and long-haul freight.

Second, talent retention in a remote-first setup. We compete for engineers and customer success managers with companies that offer different working models. So far, our culture and the quality of our product have kept voluntary turnover low. We track engagement through the Fern HRIS and address concerns promptly when they arise.

Third, data security. As we handle routing data for our customers, any breach would damage trust. Our Head of Information Security, Priya Nayar, has completed the annual risk assessment, and we have updated our incident response plan accordingly. No material findings were raised.

Fourth, dependency on a few key individuals. In a company of our size, the departure of a senior engineer or manager can have an outsized effect. We mitigate this through documentation and cross-training, and we have succession conversations as part of our regular people processes.

## 5. Asks

We request the board's input on the following items.

First, we ask for endorsement of our plan to keep the current sales structure unchanged for the remainder of the year. We believe the focus on conversion efficiency over headcount growth is the right call, but we welcome the board's perspective on timing for a future expansion.

Second, we ask for guidance on the self-serve trial channel. We see early promise, but we have not yet defined a clear investment threshold. We would like the board's view on whether to explore this further in the next planning cycle.

Third, we ask the board to note the updated risk register, particularly the customer concentration item. We will report on any material change in that area at the next meeting.
