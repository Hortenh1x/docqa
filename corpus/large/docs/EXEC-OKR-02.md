# Company OKRs — Q2 2025

Access: Managers only

## 1. Objectives

This quarter we focus on three company-wide objectives that build on the foundation laid in the previous quarters. First, we deepen our position in the mid-size logistics segment by making Kranich Route Cloud the obvious choice for fleet operators who want dependable, measurable efficiency gains. Second, we strengthen the reliability and scalability of our platform so that customers experience consistently excellent performance as their fleets grow. Third, we mature how we work internally — improving how teams collaborate across offices and time zones, and how we share context and decisions.

These objectives reflect what our customers tell us matters most, and what our teams tell us will make Kranich a better place to work. They are ambitious but achievable with the talent and focus we already have in the company.

## 2. Key results

Each objective is supported by a small set of key results. For our growth objective, the key result targets 45% growth in active fleets. This means we want more mid-size operators to adopt and stay with Kranich Route Cloud, not just try it once. Supporting results include improving our onboarding completion rate and increasing the share of customers who use our routing features daily.

For the reliability objective, we track platform uptime and the time it takes to resolve customer-impacting incidents. We also measure how quickly we restore service after a disruption. The target is to keep incidents rare and resolution prompt, so that customers can plan their operations with confidence.

For the internal collaboration objective, we measure how consistently teams use our documented ways of working, and how quickly new joiners feel productive. We also track the completion of cross-team initiatives that were started in the previous quarter.

## 3. Owners

Each objective has a named owner from the Leadership Team. Marta Lindqvist owns the growth objective and is accountable for the active-fleet key result. Jonas Weber owns the platform reliability objective, working closely with the Platform & Infrastructure team and the Routing Core team. Aylin Demir owns the internal collaboration objective, partnering with the VP People & Culture function and all team leads.

Owners are responsible for removing blockers, making trade-off decisions, and reporting progress. They are supported by the relevant teams: Customer Success and Go-to-Market support the growth objective; Fleet Insights contributes to reliability through customer feedback loops; and all teams contribute to internal collaboration.

## 4. Review

We review progress against these OKRs at the leadership offsite in the middle of the quarter and again at the end of the quarter. In addition, each owner holds a brief check-in with their key result contributors every few weeks. These check-ins are informal and focus on unblocking work, not on status reporting.

If a key result is at risk, the owner escalates promptly to the Leadership Team. We will adjust the OKRs only if the underlying business context changes meaningfully; otherwise we protect the focus for the quarter.

Related people policies and operational guidance are referenced in POL-004, and any travel or expense implications from offsites are handled per FIN-RATES-2026. Managers should review this document with their teams during the first week of the quarter so that everyone understands how their daily work connects to these objectives.
