# Merchandise & Welcome Packs

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains what Kranich merchandise and welcome packs you can expect, whether you are joining the company or have been with us for a while. We want everyone to feel part of the team, no matter where you work from — Berlin, Lisbon, or your home office in any of our countries of employment.

Workplace & Operations manages all merchandise and welcome packs. We keep things simple: clear options, fair distribution, and no complicated requests. If you have a question that this policy does not answer, please reach out to us via the Grove page for Workplace & Operations or write to it-help@kranich.example.

## 2. What you get

When you join Kranich, you receive a welcome pack shortly after your start date. It is sent to your home address or to your local office, whichever you prefer. The pack includes a few practical items to help you settle in: a notebook, a pen, a lanyard, and a Kranich-branded water bottle. We also include a small card from your team to welcome you personally.

Beyond the welcome pack, we offer a standard selection of merchandise that is available to all employees. This includes t-shirts, hoodies, and a beanie. We refresh this selection occasionally, so check the merchandise page on Grove to see what is currently available. You can order from this standard selection at any time, without needing approval from your manager.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, receives her welcome pack at her home in the Rua do Alecrim area. A few weeks later, she orders a hoodie from the standard selection. No approval is needed; the order is processed promptly and delivered to her home.

## 3. Ordering more

We want you to have what you need, and we also want to be mindful of our environmental footprint. Please order only what you will actually use.

You can order from the standard selection once per season, which we define as roughly every few months. If you need a replacement because an item is damaged or lost, just let us know and we will send a replacement without counting against your seasonal order.

For team events, offsites, or client visits, your manager or the event organiser may request additional merchandise. This includes items such as polo shirts or jackets for representing Kranich at external meetings. Please make this request through Workplace & Operations at least a few days before the event, so we can arrange delivery in time. We cannot guarantee delivery for very short notice requests.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, is hosting a client workshop. She requests two polo shirts for herself and Marek, a field solutions engineer who joins from Poland. Deniz submits the request through the Grove form a few days ahead, and the items arrive before the workshop.

If you leave Kranich, you are welcome to keep the merchandise you already have. We do not ask for items back, and we do not require you to return your welcome pack.

## 4. Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact Workplace & Operations via it-help@kranich.example. For questions about your personal data in relation to shipping or merchandise preferences, please see the privacy policy on Grove or reach out to security@kranich.example.

For related policies on equipment and home office setup, please see POL-004. For expense rules related to merchandise for client events, please see FIN-RATES-2026.

We review this policy periodically. Any changes will be communicated through Grove and will take effect on the date stated in the updated version.
