# Holiday Calendar Guide

## 1. Purpose

This guide explains how the holiday calendar works at Kranich Software GmbH. It describes which days are observed as company holidays, how the calendar is published, and what to do when a holiday falls on a weekend or during your leave.

The holiday calendar is part of our commitment to a healthy work-life balance. It gives everyone a shared sense of rhythm across the company, while respecting the local holidays of the countries where we work. We know that a one-size-fits-all approach does not work for a remote-first company with employees in several countries, so this guide sets out the principles we follow.

## 2. Who this is for

This guide is for all employees at Kranich Software GmbH, whether you work from the Ritterstraße office in Berlin, the Rua do Alecrim office in Lisbon, or remotely from home in Germany, Portugal, Spain, Poland, France, or the United Kingdom.

If you are a people manager, you should also read this guide so you can answer questions from your team and plan around holiday periods. If you are in People & Culture or Workplace & Operations, you will use this guide when maintaining the calendar itself.

New joiners receive this guide as part of their onboarding materials. It complements, and does not replace, the leave policy in Fern. For the rules on requesting and approving leave, please see the relevant policy in the Grove policy library.

## 3. Process

### Company-wide holidays

Each calendar year, Kranich Software GmbH observes a set of company-wide holidays. These days are the same for everyone, regardless of where you are based. On these days, the company is closed and no work is expected.

Company-wide holidays typically include days around the turn of the year and other dates that are widely recognised across the countries where we operate. The exact list is confirmed each year by People & Culture, in consultation with the leadership team, and published on Grove.

### Local holidays

In addition to company-wide holidays, you may observe public holidays that apply in your country of employment. Because we are a remote-first company, local holidays are handled flexibly. If a public holiday in your country falls on a day when you would normally work, you may take that day off.

For employees based in Germany, the local holidays that apply depend on the federal state where you live. For employees in Portugal, Spain, Poland, France, and the United Kingdom, the national public holidays of your country apply. If you are unsure whether a particular day is a public holiday where you live, check the official public holiday list for your country or ask People & Culture.

### How holidays are recorded

Company-wide holidays and local holidays are recorded in Fern. When a holiday applies to you, you do not need to submit a leave request. The day is marked automatically. You can see your holiday days in the Fern calendar at any time.

If a holiday falls on a day when you are already on approved leave, the holiday does not count against your leave entitlement. You only use leave for the days that are not holidays. This is handled automatically by Fern, so you do not need to adjust your leave request.

### Weekends and bridge days

When a holiday falls on a weekend, the general principle is that the holiday is not automatically moved to the nearest working day. This is because our team is spread across time zones and countries, and a simple shift would not work fairly for everyone.

Instead, you may take the holiday on an alternative day that suits you, within a reasonable time around the actual holiday date. You do this by submitting a normal leave request in Fern and noting that it is a substitute holiday. People & Culture publishes guidance each year on how this works for the specific calendar.

For bridge days — the working days between a holiday and a weekend — there is no automatic closure. You may request leave for a bridge day like any other working day, subject to your team's planning and the approval of your manager.

### Publication and changes

The holiday calendar is published on Grove before the start of each calendar year. People & Culture aims to publish it early enough that you can plan your leave and personal commitments. If the calendar changes during the year, for example because of a newly announced public holiday, People & Culture will update Grove and notify all employees by email.

## 4. Timelines

The holiday calendar follows the calendar year. People & Culture prepares the draft calendar in the months before the new year and publishes the final version on Grove before the year begins.

If you plan to use a substitute holiday for a weekend date, please submit your leave request in Fern promptly, so that your manager and team can plan around your absence.

For local holidays, no advance notice is needed — the day is recognised automatically in Fern based on your country of employment.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, checks the calendar at the start of the year. He sees that a Polish public holiday falls on a Saturday. He plans to take the substitute day on the following Monday, when he has no client meetings scheduled. He submits a leave request in Fern with a short note explaining that it is a substitute holiday, and his manager approves it.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, is on approved leave during a week when a company-wide holiday occurs. She does not need to do anything; Fern records the holiday separately, and her leave entitlement is only reduced for the days that are not holidays. When she returns, she checks her leave balance in Fern and confirms that everything looks correct.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, notices that a local holiday in her federal state falls on a Tuesday. She thinks about taking the Monday as a bridge day to have a longer break, but she first checks with her team whether anyone will be available for customer calls. After agreeing on coverage with a colleague, she submits her leave request for the Monday in Fern.

## 5. Questions

If you have questions about the holiday calendar, the first place to look is the Grove policy library, where the current year's calendar is posted alongside this guide and related policies.

For questions about how a holiday applies to you personally, or about substitute days and bridge days, please contact People & Culture at people@kranich.example.

For technical issues with Fern, such as a holiday not appearing on your calendar or a problem with recording a substitute day, please contact IT Support at it-help@kranich.example.

If you believe a holiday has been recorded incorrectly and it affects your leave balance, please raise this with People & Culture promptly so we can resolve it. We are happy to help, and we appreciate your patience while we review the details.

For security-related concerns about how your personal data is handled in Fern or on Grove, please contact the Information Security team at security@kranich.example. Our Head of Information Security, Priya Nayar, and her team are available to assist.

Finally, if you have suggestions for how the holiday calendar could work better for our distributed team, we would love to hear from you. Share your ideas with People & Culture or with your manager, and we will consider them when we review the calendar for the coming year.
