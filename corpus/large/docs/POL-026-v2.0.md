# Benefits Policy

## 1. Purpose

Kranich Software GmbH wants every employee to do their best work while feeling supported in their daily life. We know that benefits look different for everyone, depending on where you live, how you work, and what matters to you outside of work.

This policy describes the benefits available to all employees of Kranich Software GmbH, regardless of whether you work from our Berlin office, our Lisbon office, or remotely from your home in Germany, Portugal, Spain, Poland, France, or the United Kingdom. The policy is owned by People & Culture, and we review it regularly to make sure it stays relevant and fair.

We keep things simple. Instead of a long list of perks that few people use, we offer a small set of meaningful benefits that you can shape around your own needs. If you have questions about anything in this policy, please reach out to people@kranich.example.

## 2. Wellness budget

Every employee receives a wellness budget to support their physical and mental health. The wellness budget is €50 per month. You can use it for activities and items that genuinely contribute to your wellbeing — think of sports classes, meditation apps, ergonomic accessories for your home workspace, or a massage after a long week.

The budget is paid out as a monthly allowance and does not need to be used in the same month it is granted. You can save up your allowance for a few months if you prefer to spend it on something larger, as long as the total expense is covered by the budget you have accumulated.

To claim an expense, you submit the receipt through Ledgerly, our expenses tool. The claim is processed promptly, and the amount is added to your next payroll run. You do not need manager approval for wellness expenses within your available budget.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, uses her wellness budget for a weekly yoga class near her home. She saves the receipts and submits them at the end of each month through Ledgerly.

## 3. Mobility

We want to make your commute and your work-related travel as smooth as possible. Kranich Software GmbH supports a range of mobility options, and you choose what fits your routine best.

If you work from an office, we encourage you to use public transport. You can claim the cost of your monthly public transport pass for your commute to the Ritterstraße office in Berlin or the Rua do Alecrim office in Lisbon. The claim is submitted through Ledgerly and reimbursed with your next payroll.

For work-related travel that is not part of a client engagement covered by a separate agreement, you book your transport through Ledgerly, our travel booking tool. This includes train journeys between our offices, travel to team offsites, and trips to conferences where you represent Kranich.

If you travel to a client site as part of your role — for example, as a field solutions engineer — the costs are handled according to the client engagement terms. Marek, who works with clients in Poland, typically travels by train and claims his tickets through the same process as any other work travel.

If you cycle to work, you are welcome to use the secure bike storage at both offices. We also cover the cost of minor repairs to your everyday commuting bike, up to your available wellness budget if you prefer to use it that way. For anything beyond that, please speak with your manager.

## 4. Hardship advances

We understand that sometimes life throws unexpected financial challenges your way. Whether it is an urgent home repair, an unplanned medical expense, or a family emergency, the last thing we want is for you to worry about money while you are already dealing with a difficult situation.

In these cases, Kranich Software GmbH offers hardship advances. A hardship advance is an early payment of salary that you have already earned but that would normally be paid at the end of the month. It is not a loan and does not accrue any interest or fees. You simply receive part of your upcoming salary a little earlier than usual.

To request a hardship advance, you contact your manager or a member of the People & Culture team directly. You do not need to provide detailed evidence of your situation; a short conversation is enough for us to understand and help. We process requests promptly, usually within a few days.

The amount you can request is limited to the net salary you have already accrued in the current month. This keeps the process simple and ensures that your regular payroll at the end of the month simply reflects the amount you have already received.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, had an unexpected plumbing issue at home. She contacted Lea in People & Culture, explained the situation briefly, and received an advance on her salary within a few days. Her regular monthly payroll was adjusted accordingly.

Hardship advances are available to all employees, regardless of your role, location, or length of service. If you are not sure whether your situation qualifies, please ask — we would rather help than have you struggle in silence.

## 5. Questions

If you have any questions about this policy or the benefits described in it, please reach out to People & Culture at people@kranich.example. We are happy to clarify how a benefit applies to your specific situation, whether you are based in Berlin, Lisbon, or working remotely from another country.

For questions about how to submit a claim or book travel through Ledgerly, please contact it-help@kranich.example. For questions about the security of your personal data in connection with any benefit claim, please contact security@kranich.example.

We also encourage you to speak with your manager if you feel that a benefit is not meeting your needs. Your manager can escalate feedback to People & Culture, and we use that input when we review this policy. Related information about leave and time off can be found in POL-004, and the current expense reimbursement rates are published in FIN-RATES-2026.

Kranich Software GmbH is committed to treating all employees fairly and consistently. This policy applies to everyone, and we will always consider individual circumstances with empathy and common sense.
