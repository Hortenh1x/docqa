# Announcement: Equipment refresh wave

## 1. Announcement

Hello everyone,

We are pleased to announce the next equipment refresh wave, rolling out across Kranich Software GmbH over the coming weeks. This refresh is part of our ongoing commitment to make sure everyone has reliable, secure and comfortable hardware to do their best work.

The refresh covers laptops, monitors, docking stations and peripherals for team members whose current equipment is approaching the end of its supported lifecycle. If you are included in this wave, you will receive a personal notification from Workplace & Operations with details about your replacement device and the next steps. Please watch your inbox and respond promptly so we can keep the rollout moving smoothly.

What you can expect:

- A new laptop, pre-configured with our standard security settings and tooling.
- A monitor and docking station where your role benefits from a multi-screen setup.
- Peripherals such as keyboards, mice and headsets, replaced as needed.

Your current equipment should be returned once your new setup is in place. Workplace & Operations will coordinate the return and handle data erasure securely. Please do not dispose of any equipment yourself.

If you have specific accessibility needs or ergonomic requirements, please reach out to Workplace & Operations before your scheduled refresh so we can plan accordingly. We want the new equipment to work well for you from day one.

For questions about eligibility, timing or the return process, please contact it-help@kranich.example. For anything related to your employment or working arrangements, people@kranich.example is happy to help.

This initiative follows the principles set out in POL-004. As always, we are grateful for your patience and cooperation as we work through the wave.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, receives her notification and books a short slot with the local operations contact to collect her new laptop. She returns her old device in the same visit, keeping downtime to a minimum.

Thank you for helping us keep our equipment modern, secure and sustainable.
