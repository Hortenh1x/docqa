# Facilities Requests

## 1. Purpose

This document explains how to request facilities support at Kranich Software. Facilities covers the physical working environment in our offices in Berlin (Ritterstraße) and Lisbon (Rua do Alecrim), as well as support for remote and home working setups where applicable.

The Workplace & Operations team, led by Rui Almeida, manages all facilities requests. We aim to keep our offices safe, comfortable, and functional so that everyone can do their best work, whether they are based in an office or working remotely.

This policy applies to all employees, regardless of their team or location.

## 2. Reporting an issue

If you notice something in an office that needs attention, please report it promptly. Common examples include:

- broken furniture or equipment
- heating, cooling, or ventilation problems
- lighting issues
- kitchen or bathroom maintenance
- cleaning concerns
- safety hazards

To report an issue, send an email to it-help@kranich.example with the subject line "Facilities request". Please include:

- your name and office location
- a clear description of the issue
- the specific area or room where the issue is located
- any relevant photos, if you have them

For urgent safety concerns, such as a fire hazard, a water leak, or an electrical fault, contact Workplace & Operations immediately by phone or in person. Do not wait for an email response.

For remote employees, facilities support is limited to equipment provided by Kranich. If you have a problem with company-issued hardware or your home office setup, please contact it-help@kranich.example instead. The Workplace & Operations team handles physical office spaces; the Platform & Infrastructure team supports your devices and software.

## 3. Timelines

We understand that facilities issues can affect your comfort and productivity. Our team triages every request when it arrives and prioritises based on urgency.

Safety-related issues are addressed immediately. For other requests, you can expect an initial acknowledgement within a few business days. The time to resolution depends on the nature of the issue and whether external contractors are needed. We will keep you informed of progress if a repair takes longer than expected.

For example, if Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, notices a faulty chair in a meeting room, she sends a facilities request with a photo. Rui's team acknowledges her request quickly and arranges for a replacement chair. If Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, reports that the air conditioning in her area is not working, the team may need to coordinate with an external service provider. Anna receives updates until the issue is resolved.

If you have not heard back within a reasonable time, please follow up on your original email thread. You can also reach out to Rui Almeida or any member of the Workplace & Operations team directly.

## 4. Questions

If you have questions about this policy or about facilities matters in general, please contact Workplace & Operations at it-help@kranich.example.

For questions about equipment purchases or budgets, please refer to the relevant finance policy. For questions about office access, security, or visitor management, please refer to POL-004. For questions about travel or booking office space in other locations, please refer to the applicable travel policy.

We encourage you to report issues early. Small problems are easier and faster to fix than large ones. Your observations help us keep our offices welcoming and safe for everyone. Thank you for helping us maintain a great working environment.
