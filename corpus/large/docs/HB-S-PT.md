# Travel Supplement — Portugal

## 1. Purpose

This supplement explains how travel expenses in Portugal are reimbursed for employees of Kranich Software GmbH. It applies to business trips booked through Ledgerly and to expenses submitted after the trip. For general travel rules, refer to POL-004. This supplement is effective from the date shown on the front matter.

## 2. Per-diem

The per-diem for this country is in FIN-RATES-2026. That document also explains when the per-diem applies in full and when it is reduced, for example on the day of departure or arrival. If you have questions about a specific trip, please ask Finance before you travel.

## 3. Hotel cap

When you book a hotel in Portugal for a business trip, the nightly rate must stay within the cap shown below. The cap applies to the room rate before taxes and fees. If the only available hotel exceeds the cap, please contact it-help@kranich.example for assistance before booking.

| Country | Cap |
|---|---|
| Portugal | €195 per night |

## 4. Mileage

If you use your private car for a business trip in Portugal, mileage is reimbursed at €0.23 per km.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, sometimes drives her own car from the Rua do Alecrim office to meet colleagues at a client site nearby. She records the round trip in Ledgerly and attaches a short note about the purpose of the journey.

## 5. Questions

If you have questions about this supplement, please reach out to Finance or to your manager. For practical help with booking or expenses, contact it-help@kranich.example. For questions about how this policy interacts with your employment terms, please contact people@kranich.example.
