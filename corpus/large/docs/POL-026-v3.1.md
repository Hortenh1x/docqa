# Benefits Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes the benefits that Kranich Software GmbH offers to all employees, regardless of their work location or employment country. We believe that fair, transparent benefits help everyone do their best work while feeling supported at home and at work.

This version supersedes version 2.0 of this policy. The main changes clarify how the wellness budget can be used and how hardship advances are requested and repaid.

Benefits are administered by People & Culture. If you have questions about your eligibility or how to use a benefit, reach out to people@kranich.example. Your manager and the People team can also point you to the relevant sections of this policy.

Nothing in this policy creates a contract of employment. Kranich Software GmbH reserves the right to review and adjust benefits from time to time, in line with local legal requirements and business needs.

## 2. Wellness budget

Every employee receives a wellness budget of €50 per month to spend on activities and items that support their physical or mental wellbeing. The budget accumulates and can be used at any time during the calendar year. Unused amounts do not carry over into the next year.

You can use the wellness budget for a wide range of expenses, including sports classes, yoga sessions, meditation apps, ergonomic accessories for your home office, or health-related books and subscriptions. The budget is meant to be flexible: choose what genuinely helps you feel well.

To claim an expense, submit the receipt through Ledgerly under the appropriate category. The People team reviews claims and reimburses them with your next payroll run. You do not need manager approval for individual claims, but please keep receipts for your records.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, uses her wellness budget over several months to pay for a weekly climbing session at a local gym. She submits each receipt in Ledgerly and receives the reimbursement with her salary.

The wellness budget is not a cash benefit. It cannot be exchanged for money, transferred to another person, or used to purchase items that are not related to wellbeing. If you are unsure whether an expense qualifies, ask the People team before making the purchase.

## 3. Mobility

Kranich Software GmbH supports sustainable commuting and business travel through a mobility framework that applies consistently across all offices and remote locations.

For commuting, we encourage the use of public transport and other low-carbon options. In cities where our offices are located, we provide discounted or subsidized transit passes. If you live in an area without good public transport connections, you may use your wellness budget for related mobility costs that support your commute.

For business travel between our offices or to client sites, you can book trains and flights through Ledgerly. The travel booking tool shows you the most cost-effective and environmentally friendly options first. We ask that you choose the recommended option unless there is a compelling reason to do otherwise, such as a tight schedule or an accessibility need.

Local ground transportation during business travel, such as taxis or ride-sharing services, is reimbursable when public transport is not a reasonable option. Always attach the receipt to your expense report in Ledgerly.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, travels by train to client sites most of the time. When a client meeting ends late and no train is available, he takes a taxi and submits the receipt with a short note in Ledgerly.

The mobility framework is not intended to cover personal travel. If you combine a business trip with personal days, you are responsible for the additional costs. See the travel guidance in Ledgerly for details on what is covered.

## 4. Hardship advances

Access: People & Culture only

A hardship advance is a voluntary payment that Kranich Software GmbH may make to an employee who faces an unexpected financial emergency. Examples include urgent medical expenses, essential home repairs, or travel costs related to a family crisis. A hardship advance is limited to €2,000.

Hardship advances are not a regular benefit and are not available for planned expenses such as holidays, large purchases, or routine bills. They are intended as a short-term bridge for situations that are genuinely urgent and outside your control.

To request a hardship advance, contact the People team at people@kranich.example. A member of the People team will discuss your situation with you confidentially and explain what information we need. We treat all requests with discretion; only the People team and the payroll administrator will see the details.

If approved, the advance is paid with your next payroll run. Hardship advances are repaid through payroll within 12 months. The repayment schedule is set at the time of approval and is deducted in equal installments from your salary. You cannot change the schedule after the advance is paid, but you may repay the full remaining amount early without penalty.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, faces an unexpected plumbing emergency in her apartment. She contacts the People team, explains the situation, and receives a hardship advance to cover the repair. The advance is repaid through payroll over the following months.

A hardship advance is not a loan product and does not accrue interest. It is a discretionary payment that Kranich Software GmbH offers as a gesture of support. Approval depends on the circumstances and on your employment status at the time of the request. If your employment ends before the advance is fully repaid, the remaining balance is deducted from your final salary payment.

If you are in financial difficulty and are not sure whether a hardship advance is appropriate, please talk to the People team. We can also point you to external support resources that may be available in your country of employment.

## 5. Questions

If you have questions about any part of this policy, please reach out to the People team at people@kranich.example. We are happy to clarify eligibility, explain how to claim a benefit, or discuss your individual situation.

For questions about the wellness budget or mobility expenses, you can also check the guidance in Ledgerly or contact it-help@kranich.example if you have technical issues with the expense tool.

For matters related to confidentiality or data protection in the context of hardship advances, please contact security@kranich.example. The Information Security team can advise on how your personal data is handled.

This policy is reviewed annually by People & Culture. Related policies include POL-004, which covers leave and time off, and FIN-RATES-2026, which lists the current expense reimbursement rates. If there is a conflict between this policy and another document, the most recent version of the relevant policy takes precedence.

We want everyone at Kranich Software GmbH to feel supported. If you have suggestions for improving our benefits, please share them with your manager or the People team. Your feedback helps us build a workplace where everyone can thrive.
