# Offboarding Checklist

## 1. Purpose

Leaving Kranich Software is a significant transition — for the person departing and for the teams they leave behind. This document describes the offboarding process from the moment a resignation is announced or a departure is agreed, through the final working day and beyond.

A good offboarding is respectful, clear, and thorough. It protects company data, keeps our clients well served, and makes sure that every leaver is treated with the dignity we would want for ourselves. This checklist is the single source of truth for what needs to happen, who does it, and when.

## 2. Who this is for

This checklist is for everyone involved in an offboarding:

- **People & Culture** coordinates the overall process and is the first point of contact for questions.
- **Managers** run the practical steps with their team member and complete the manager-specific actions.
- **Platform & Infrastructure** handles access, accounts, and equipment.
- **Finance** closes out expenses and payroll-related matters.
- **Workplace & Operations** arranges the return of office items and supports colleagues in the Ritterstraße and Rua do Alecrim offices.
- **The departing employee** has a clear set of responsibilities too — we ask them to hand over thoughtfully and to return what belongs to Kranich.

If you are unsure who does what, ask People & Culture. We would rather answer the same question twice than miss a step.

## 3. Process

The process follows the order below. Some steps run in parallel, and some require coordination between teams — the checklist is designed so that nothing depends on a single person remembering everything.

**Step one: notification and kickoff.** When People & Culture learns of a departure, they open an offboarding record and notify the manager, Platform & Infrastructure, Finance, and Workplace & Operations. The manager schedules a conversation with the departing employee to discuss the timeline, the handover, and any open questions.

**Step two: handover planning.** The manager works with the departing employee to identify ongoing work, open client engagements, and recurring responsibilities. A handover document is prepared, naming the colleagues who will take over each area. This document is shared with the receiving team before the final working day.

**Step three: access and accounts.** Platform & Infrastructure revokes access according to the timeline in §4 and the rules in POL-023. This includes system accounts, internal tools, and any client-facing credentials. Access revocation for cause is handled separately — see POL-023 §4, which is manager-only.

**Step four: equipment and property.** The departing employee returns company equipment and any other property. Workplace & Operations coordinates this for office-based colleagues; for remote colleagues, a shipping arrangement is made. The return deadline is set out in POL-006 §5.

**Step five: knowledge and documentation.** The departing employee ensures that their documentation is current, that code and configuration notes are stored in the usual places, and that the handover document reflects reality. Managers should review this together with the leaver.

**Step six: people and culture touchpoints.** People & Culture conducts an exit conversation, if the employee wishes. This is an opportunity to share feedback in a constructive way. The departing employee also receives information about their final payslip, benefits, and any continuing obligations.

**Step seven: closure.** Once all steps are complete, People & Culture confirms closure with the manager and the relevant teams. The offboarding record is archived in line with our data retention practices.

## 4. Timelines

Timing depends on the type of departure and on local notice periods. The following principles apply:

- **Resignation with notice.** The process starts as soon as People & Culture is informed. Most steps are completed before the final working day. Access to systems that are not needed for the handover is revoked promptly — do not wait until the last day.
- **Mutual agreement.** The same process applies, with the timeline agreed between the manager, People & Culture, and the employee.
- **Departure for cause.** Access is revoked immediately, in line with POL-023 §4. This decision is manager-only. Other steps follow as quickly as possible, with the employee’s dignity kept in mind.
- **End of contract.** For fixed-term contracts, People & Culture initiates the process a few weeks before the end date, so that the handover is not rushed.

Whatever the timeline, the handover document should be finalised a few days before the final working day, so that receiving colleagues have time to ask questions.

## 5. Questions

Offboarding can raise personal and practical questions. Please reach out to the right team early — it is always better to ask.

- **People & Culture** — for anything about the process, the exit conversation, or your final employment details: people@kranich.example.
- **Platform & Infrastructure** — for access, accounts, or equipment: it-help@kranich.example.
- **Security** — for anything you are unsure about regarding data or credentials: security@kranich.example.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, resigns with notice. Her manager in Berlin coordinates the handover with her team in the Rua do Alecrim office. Platform & Infrastructure revokes her access to most systems promptly, keeping only what she needs for the handover. She returns her laptop through Workplace & Operations, completes her documentation, and has a final conversation with People & Culture. Nothing is left to the last day, and her colleagues know exactly where to pick up her work.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, is leaving to join a partner company. Her handover document lists every client account and the current status of each engagement. She introduces her successor to the key clients in person, and her access is revoked on her final day. The transition is smooth for clients and for the team alike.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, completes his handover remotely. He ships his equipment back, and Platform & Infrastructure confirms receipt before closing his accounts. His documentation is thorough, and the receiving engineer in the Routing Core team feels confident taking over.

A departure is also a moment to say thank you. We hope that everyone who leaves Kranich Software does so with good memories and a fair process behind them.
