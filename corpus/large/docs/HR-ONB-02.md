# Onboarding Guide — Fleet Insights

## 1. Purpose

Welcome to Kranich Software and to the Fleet Insights team. This guide helps you find
your bearings in your first weeks, whether you are joining as a product manager, an
engineer, a data analyst, or in another role.

We keep onboarding human. You are not expected to deliver anything before you feel
ready. Instead, we focus on context, connections, and small wins. By the end of your
first month, you should know what Fleet Insights builds, who your teammates are, and
where to find answers when you get stuck.

## 2. Before you start

Your manager will send you a welcome note with your start date and your team's meeting
rhythm. Before your first day:

- Check your Kranich laptop and confirm you can log in.
- Set up your accounts in 1Password. You will receive an invite to the Fleet Insights
  vault.
- Look up your org chart in Fern and find the people on your immediate team.
- Book your first-week meetings in Perch if you plan to work from an office.

If you work remotely, your manager will schedule a short video call for your first
morning. If you are based in Berlin or Lisbon and prefer the office, your desk is
waiting — no reservation needed for your first week.

Your equipment budget is described in POL-006 §3. Read that section before ordering
anything extra. If you have questions, ask your manager or it-help@kranich.example.

## 3. Step by step

### First day

- Start with a welcome call with your manager. You will talk about your role, the team,
  and what success looks like in your first months.
- Join the Fleet Insights team channel in Grove. Introduce yourself briefly: your name,
  your location, and one thing you are curious about.
- Complete your personal data in Fern, including your emergency contact and your home
  address for payroll.
- Take a tour of the Kranich Route Cloud product with a teammate. You do not need to
  understand everything — the goal is to see the product your team works on.

### First week

- Meet each member of your immediate team. Your manager will schedule these short
  introductions for you.
- Read the Fleet Insights roadmap and the notes from the last planning session. Both are
  linked in the team's Grove space.
- Watch the recorded product demo for the current release. It is about as long as a
  lunch break, so grab something to eat and enjoy it.
- Complete the security training. The deadline is set in §3 of POL-005 — check that
  document for the exact timing.

### First month

- Shadow a customer call with a member of Customer Success. Fleet Insights builds
  features for real logistics operators, and hearing their questions is the fastest way
  to understand our users.
- Pick one small task from the team backlog and complete it with a buddy. Your buddy is
  a teammate who volunteered to help you navigate your first weeks.
- Meet your skip-level manager once. This is a casual conversation, not a review.
- Review the Fleet Insights metrics dashboard with your manager. You do not need to
  memorize the numbers; you need to know where to find them.

### First quarter

- Present a short "what I learned" update to the team. It can be about the product, the
  customers, or how we work — whatever stood out to you.
- Set your objectives for the coming months with your manager in Fern.
- Schedule a check-in with your manager about your development goals for the year.

## 4. Common questions

**I work remotely from another country. Do I need to come to Berlin or Lisbon?**

No. Most of the Fleet Insights team works remotely. Your manager or a teammate will
visit you in your first months, and you are welcome to visit an office whenever it suits
you. Book a desk in Perch if you do.

**Where do I find the team's documents?**

Everything lives in Grove. Start with the Fleet Insights space, then follow the links to
the roadmap, planning notes, and design documents. If you cannot find something, ask in
the team channel — someone will point you to the right place.

**How do I request leave or update my personal details?**

Use Fern for leave requests and personal data. For expenses or travel, use Ledgerly.
If you are unsure which tool to use, ask people@kranich.example.

**Who do I contact if my laptop or accounts do not work?**

Write to it-help@kranich.example. Include a short description of the problem and your
work email. They usually respond promptly.

**What happens at the end of my probation period?**

Your manager will schedule a conversation to review how you are settling in. You will
also have the chance to give feedback about your onboarding experience. This is a normal
meeting, not a test.

**I am nervous about asking too many questions.**

Ask anyway. Everyone at Kranich was new once. Teammates would rather answer a question
twice than have you stuck silently. If you prefer written questions, post them in the
team channel — you will often get several helpful replies.
