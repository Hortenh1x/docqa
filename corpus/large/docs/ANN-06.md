# Announcement: Vacation policy update

## 1. Announcement

Dear team,

We are pleased to share an update to our vacation policy, which will take effect shortly. The revised policy is documented in POL-004 and will be posted on Grove for your reference.

This update is part of our ongoing commitment to make sure that time away from work is straightforward, fair, and restorative for everyone across Kranich. We listened to feedback from employees in all of our countries of employment, and we have adjusted the policy to better reflect the way our teams actually plan and take their leave.

The key changes are explained in POL-004. In short, the new policy clarifies how leave requests are reviewed, how advance planning works for longer absences, and what to do when plans change. It also harmonizes the way public holidays are handled for colleagues who work remotely from a different country than their employing entity.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, often travels to see family in another region. Under the updated policy, she will find it easier to plan her leave around local holidays there, without needing to coordinate with her Berlin-based team beyond the normal request process.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, sometimes needs to adjust approved leave when a client engagement shifts. The new policy explains how to make such changes promptly and transparently.

Please read the full policy on Grove. If you have questions, reach out to your manager or People & Culture via people@kranich.example. We hope this update makes planning your time off a little easier.
