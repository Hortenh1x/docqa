# Compensation Bands (Berlin)
Access: Leadership only

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the salary bands for roles based in our Berlin office. It exists to support fair, consistent pay decisions and to give managers a clear framework when hiring, promoting, or adjusting salaries. The bands reflect the Berlin market for the fiscal year beginning in 2026 and are reviewed annually by the Leadership Team.

These bands apply to employees whose contractual place of work is the Ritterstraße office. Colleagues in other locations follow separate guidance maintained by People & Culture; see POL-004 for the general principles behind our pay philosophy.

## 2. Bands

The table below lists the annual base salary ranges for the specified roles. The ranges are inclusive; a candidate's or employee's position within a range depends on experience, skills, and demonstrated impact. When an employee's salary falls below the minimum of their band, the manager should work with People & Culture to bring it into range promptly.

| Role | Annual Base Salary Range |
|------|--------------------------|
| Senior Engineer | €78,000–€96,000 |
| Engineering Manager | €92,000–€115,000 |
| Product Manager | €70,000–€88,000 |

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, is promoted into a role not listed here. Her manager consults the nearest comparable band and People & Culture for guidance, rather than applying these numbers directly.

## 3. Annual review budget

Each year, the Leadership Team sets a budget for salary adjustments across the company. For the upcoming review cycle, the annual salary review budget is 3.5% of total payroll. This budget is not an entitlement; it is a ceiling for the total increase pool. Managers allocate from it based on performance, market movement, and internal equity.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, is not covered by this document. Her review follows the local process, and the budget referenced here applies only to the Berlin payroll. Managers should not assume that every employee receives the same increase; the pool is distributed thoughtfully.

## 4. Handling

This document is confidential and intended for the Leadership Team only. Do not share it with employees or external parties. If you need to discuss a specific salary decision, refer to the band structure in general terms, not to this document.

Questions about interpretation should go to People & Culture via people@kranich.example. For questions about the financial planning behind the budget, contact the CFO. This document is reviewed annually; updates are published with a new version number and effective date. Related policies are referenced in FIN-RATES-2026 for financial parameters and in POL-004 for compensation principles.
