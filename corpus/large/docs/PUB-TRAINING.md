# Customer Training Catalogue

## 1. Scope

This catalogue describes the training courses Kranich Route Cloud offers to our customers. The courses are designed for logistics operators who use our route-optimization platform in their daily planning work. We keep the content practical: every course combines guided demonstrations with hands-on exercises using realistic delivery scenarios.

Our trainers are members of the Customer Success and Go-to-Market teams. They know the product deeply and understand the operational challenges our customers face. Courses are delivered remotely, in small groups, so participants can ask questions freely and share experiences with peers from other companies.

This catalogue does not cover onboarding sessions that are part of a new customer implementation. Those sessions are arranged individually with your Customer Success Manager.

## 2. Courses

### Course A — Route Planning Essentials

This course is for planners and dispatchers who are new to Kranich Route Cloud. Participants learn the core workflow: importing orders, setting vehicle capacities and time windows, and generating an optimized route plan.

The course covers the main planning screen, how to interpret the suggested routes, and how to make manual adjustments when real-world conditions require them. We also explain how the system handles common constraints such as driver breaks and delivery time preferences.

By the end of the course, participants can build a complete route plan on their own and know where to find help when something is unclear.

### Course B — Advanced Optimization

This course builds on the essentials and is aimed at experienced planners who want to get more out of the optimization engine. We look at how to fine-tune parameters, what the different optimization objectives mean in practice, and how to compare alternative plans side by side.

Participants learn how to use the what-if analysis feature to test changes before committing to a plan. We also discuss how seasonal patterns and changing customer demand affect planning quality, and how to keep master data clean so the optimizer performs at its best.

### Course C — Fleet Insights for Managers

This course is for team leads and operations managers who oversee planning teams. The focus is on the reporting and analytics side of Kranich Route Cloud.

Participants learn how to read the key performance dashboards, how to export data for their own analyses, and how to set up automated reports for their stakeholders. We also cover how to use historical data to identify improvement opportunities in their daily operations.

No technical background is required. The course uses plain language and practical examples throughout.

### Course D — Integrations and Data Exchange

This course is for customers who want to connect Kranich Route Cloud with their own systems. We cover the standard import and export formats, how to schedule data exchanges, and how to troubleshoot common data quality issues.

Participants should have basic familiarity with their own IT landscape. Our trainers explain the concepts in a way that both technical and non-technical attendees can follow. We do not cover custom development in this course; for that, please contact your Customer Success Manager to discuss a tailored engagement.

### Course E — Refresher and Best Practices

This course is for teams that have used Kranich Route Cloud for a while and want to make sure they are getting the most out of it. We revisit core workflows, share best practices gathered from customers across Europe, and answer questions that have come up in daily use.

The session is interactive. Participants are encouraged to bring their own examples and challenges. Our trainers will work through them live and suggest concrete improvements.

## 3. Booking

Courses are scheduled throughout the year. You can book a place through your Customer Success Manager, who will confirm availability and share the joining details.

Each course takes about half a day, including breaks. We recommend that participants join from a quiet space with a stable internet connection and a second screen if available, so they can follow along while trying things themselves.

If you need to cancel or reschedule, please let your Customer Success Manager know promptly. We are happy to move you to the next available session.

For teams that prefer a private session, we offer dedicated courses for your organization only. This works well when you have several colleagues who need training at the same time, or when you want to discuss scenarios specific to your operation. Ask your Customer Success Manager about availability and pricing.

If you are unsure which course fits your needs, describe your role and your goals to your Customer Success Manager. They will recommend the right starting point, or suggest a sequence of courses if that serves you better.

We review this catalogue regularly. Course content and formats may evolve as the product develops. The version of this document is noted on the front page; your Customer Success Manager always has the latest information.

For questions about the content of a course or about booking, please contact your Customer Success Manager directly. For anything related to your contract or rates, refer to your agreement or FIN-RATES-2026. For security-related questions about the platform, see POL-004.
