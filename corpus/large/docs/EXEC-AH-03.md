# All-Hands Notes — May 2025

## 1. Highlights

Marta opened the all-hands by thanking everyone for the energy brought into the spring. She shared that Kranich Route Cloud continues to mature as a platform, and that the work across all teams is showing in how smoothly customer onboardings have been going lately.

Jonas gave a brief update from Platform & Infrastructure. The team has been focused on hardening our deployment pipeline and improving observability. He acknowledged the Routing Core team for their recent work on a complex optimization edge case that had been surfacing for a subset of customers. The fix is now in production and early feedback has been positive.

Tomasz reminded everyone that the annual planning cycle for the coming fiscal year will begin soon. He encouraged teams to start thinking about their priorities and resource needs, and noted that Finance will share a clear timeline via Grove in the coming weeks.

## 2. People

Aylin shared updates on a few people topics. First, she welcomed the new colleagues who have joined since the last all-hands. She asked managers to make sure new joiners feel settled and know where to find the basics on Grove, including the policy library and the org chart in Fern.

Aylin also mentioned that the annual engagement survey will open shortly. She encouraged everyone to take the time to fill it out honestly, as the results directly inform the People team’s priorities for the year ahead. The survey is anonymous, and the People team will share a summary of findings after the results are in.

Lea reminded everyone about the importance of keeping personal data up to date in Fern. This includes emergency contacts and home addresses, which are used for administrative purposes only.

On the topic of internal mobility, Aylin noted that several teams have shared short-term project opportunities on Grove. She encouraged anyone curious about other parts of the business to reach out to the relevant manager or to People & Culture for a conversation. There is no obligation to move; the goal is simply to make opportunities visible.

## 3. Customers

Deniz gave an update from Customer Success. She highlighted that the team has been working closely with a few larger customers in the DACH region to tailor reporting dashboards to their specific operational needs. The feedback from those conversations has been incorporated into the product roadmap.

Marek, our field solutions engineer, shared a story from a recent client workshop in Poland. The customer operates a mixed fleet and had been struggling with manual planning during peak season. Marek walked them through the Fleet Insights module, and the customer was particularly interested in how the tool surfaces idle time. The conversation is still early, but the relationship is promising.

Sofia, from Fleet Insights, added that the product team is listening carefully to the patterns coming out of these customer discussions. She noted that a few recurring requests have emerged around reporting flexibility, and the team is evaluating how to address them in the next planning cycle.

Aylin closed this section by reminding everyone that customer stories are always welcome at the all-hands. If you have worked with a customer recently and have a moment you are proud of, please reach out to the Customer Success team or to People & Culture.

## 4. Questions from the floor

**Question: When will we hear more about the priorities for the next fiscal year?**  
Tomasz responded that the leadership team will share a draft set of priorities after the summer. In the meantime, teams are encouraged to discuss their own ideas and bring them to the planning sessions. More detail will follow via Grove.

**Question: How is the company thinking about the return-to-office expectations for the second half of the year?**  
Rui Almeida, Head of Workplace & Operations, answered that the company remains remote-first. The offices in Berlin and Lisbon are open for those who want to use them. He reminded everyone that desk and meeting room bookings go through Perch, and that the offices are cleaned and stocked regularly.

**Question: Is there anything we should know about the security awareness training?**  
Priya Nayar, Head of Information Security, clarified that the next round of security awareness training will be assigned soon. She asked everyone to complete it promptly. She also reminded the company that any suspicious emails should be reported to security@kranich.example, and that 1Password should be used for all work-related credentials.

**Question: What is the best way to suggest a new internal tool or improvement to an existing one?**  
Jonas suggested posting in the appropriate channel on Grove or reaching out to Platform & Infrastructure directly. He noted that small process improvements are often the most impactful. He also thanked everyone who has already submitted feedback on our internal tooling this quarter.

Marta closed the meeting by thanking everyone for their continued focus and care. She reminded the company that the next all-hands will be announced via Grove, and that everyone is welcome to submit questions in advance through the usual channel.
