# Security FAQ

## 1. Questions

**What should I do if I suspect a phishing email?**
Do not click any links or download attachments. Forward the message to security@kranich.example, then delete it. If you already clicked a link, contact security immediately and change your password in 1Password.

**How do I report a lost or stolen device?**
Report the loss promptly to security@kranich.example and to IT Help via it-help@kranich.example. The security team will remotely wipe the device if possible. You will receive a replacement through Workplace & Operations.

**When should I use a password manager?**
Always. All work-related credentials belong in 1Password. Do not reuse personal passwords for work systems, and do not share passwords through chat or email.

**What counts as sensitive data?**
Customer route data, fleet locations, pricing information, and personal data of colleagues or clients. Handle these only in approved systems and avoid discussing them in public spaces or on unsecured networks.

**Can I use personal devices for work?**
Yes, provided they meet the requirements in the security policy. Your device must have full-disk encryption enabled, a screen lock, and the latest operating system updates installed. When in doubt, ask IT Help.

**How do I connect to the internet while traveling?**
Use the company VPN whenever you work outside the office or your home. Avoid public Wi-Fi for work tasks unless the VPN is active. If you have no VPN access, wait until you are on a trusted network.

**What is the rule for sending files externally?**
Send files only through approved collaboration tools. For documents containing sensitive data, follow the classification guidance in POL-004. When in doubt, ask the recipient to confirm they are authorized to receive the file.

**How do I grant access to a new team member?**
Access requests go through your manager via the standard onboarding process. Do not create shared accounts or forward access credentials. Fern will reflect the correct team structure, and IT Help provisions access based on that.

**What happens if I accidentally share something confidential?**
Notify security@kranich.example right away. Explain what was shared and with whom. The sooner you report it, the easier it is to contain. There is no blame for honest mistakes reported promptly.

**Where do I find the full security policies?**
All policies live in Grove under the Policy Library. Start with POL-004 for data handling and classification. For questions not covered there, reach out to security@kranich.example.

**Example:** Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, once received a message that looked like an internal notice but came from an unknown sender. She forwarded it to security before opening anything. The team confirmed it was a phishing attempt and thanked her for the quick report.

For questions about access, devices, or tools, contact it-help@kranich.example. For questions about policy or training, contact people@kranich.example.
