# Benefits FAQ

## 1. Questions

**Who is eligible for Kranich benefits?**
All regular employees, regardless of their work location or whether they work from home or from one of our offices. Eligibility starts on your first working day. If you are unsure about your specific situation, check your contract or reach out to people@kranich.example.

**Where can I find the details of my benefits package?**
The full policy set is available on Grove. Start with POL-004 for the core benefits overview. For anything related to travel or expenses, please refer to Ledgerly and the policy referenced from it.

**How do I book time off?**
All leave requests go through Fern. You will find the leave types available to you there, along with the approval workflow. For questions about how much leave you can carry over from one year to the next, please see POL-004.

**What does Kranich offer for my retirement or pension?**
This depends on your country of employment. In Germany, we provide a workplace pension arrangement as described in POL-004. For colleagues in other countries, we offer a local equivalent where available. Please reach out to People & Culture if you would like to understand what applies to you.

**I am planning to become a parent. What support is available?**
We offer parental leave in line with local legal requirements in each country where we employ people. You will find our internal guidelines on Grove, and we encourage you to speak with your manager early so we can plan together. Lea in People Operations can walk you through the process.

**Does Kranich offer private health insurance or health-related perks?**
We provide a contribution toward private health coverage in countries where this is common practice. The exact arrangements vary by country, so please check POL-004 or ask People & Culture. We also run regular wellbeing initiatives throughout the year — keep an eye on Grove for upcoming events.

**What about home-office equipment?**
If you work remotely, you can order the equipment you need through the standard IT procurement process. Platform & Infrastructure will help you choose what fits your role. For anything broken or faulty, contact it-help@kranich.example.

**Can I use my benefits for things not listed here?**
Some benefits come in the form of a flexible allowance that you can spend on eligible items or services. The list of eligible categories is defined in POL-004. If you have an idea that is not on the list, please suggest it to People & Culture — we review the catalogue each year.

**Who do I contact if I have a benefits question that is not answered here?**
Write to people@kranich.example. For anything related to expenses or travel, Ledgerly support or the Finance team can help. For security-related questions about benefit platforms, contact security@kranich.example.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, recently used the flexible allowance to cover the cost of a professional development course. She found the eligible categories in POL-004 and submitted the receipt through Ledgerly the same day.
