# All-Hands Notes — March 2025

## 1. Highlights

Marta opened the all-hands by thanking everyone for a strong start to the year. The company continues to grow steadily, and the focus across all teams remains on building a reliable, scalable platform that our customers can depend on every day.

Jonas shared that the Routing Core team has made solid progress on the next generation of the optimization engine. The work is iterative and collaborative, with regular input from Customer Success and field engineers. He emphasized that quality comes before speed, and the team is taking the time to get the details right.

Tomasz gave a brief update on the financial health of the company. He noted that the fiscal year is progressing as planned and that the leadership team remains cautious and deliberate about where we invest. No specific numbers were shared — the message was simply that the fundamentals are sound.

## 2. People

Aylin announced that the People & Culture team has been reviewing our internal policies with fresh eyes. The goal is to make sure every policy is clear, fair, and easy to find on Grove. A few documents have already been updated, and more will follow over the coming months. If you have questions about a specific policy, please reach out to people@kranich.example.

Lea reminded everyone that the annual people review cycle will open soon. Managers will receive guidance in advance, and employees will have time to prepare their own reflections. The process is meant to be a conversation, not a formality — please come with examples of your work and your aspirations for the year ahead.

Aylin also welcomed the new colleagues who have joined since the last all-hands. She encouraged everyone to say hello in the appropriate Slack channels and to make use of the buddy system for anyone who is still finding their feet.

On the topic of working arrangements, Aylin confirmed that the remote-first approach remains unchanged. The Ritterstraße and Rua do Alecrim offices are open for those who want to come in, and Perch makes it easy to book a desk or a meeting room in advance. As always, the expectation is that we communicate clearly about where we are working from, so that teams can plan accordingly.

## 3. Customers

Deniz gave an update from Customer Success. The team has been working closely with several customers who are preparing for seasonal peaks. The conversations have been productive, and customers appreciate the proactive advice they are receiving. Deniz encouraged everyone to keep sharing customer feedback in the relevant channels — small observations often lead to meaningful improvements.

Marek spoke about his recent visits with customers in Poland. He highlighted that field engineers play an important role in bridging the gap between what customers ask for and what the product can deliver. He also noted that customers are increasingly interested in the Fleet Insights roadmap, and he invited Sofia to share a bit more.

Sofia presented a preview of what Fleet Insights is exploring for the next few quarters. The team is looking at ways to make reporting more intuitive and to help customers understand their operational patterns at a glance. She stressed that these are early ideas and that customer input will shape what eventually gets built. Anyone with customer conversations that touch on reporting or analytics is welcome to share notes with her team.

Jonas added that Platform & Infrastructure continues to invest in reliability and security. Priya reminded everyone to keep using 1Password and to report anything unusual promptly to security@kranich.example. She also mentioned that the annual security awareness training will be scheduled soon — watch your inbox for the invitation.

## 4. Questions from the floor

**Q: When will the new optimization engine be available to customers?**  
Jonas explained that there is no fixed date yet. The team is testing thoroughly and will communicate a timeline once there is more certainty. Customers will hear about it through their usual contacts first.

**Q: Will there be changes to how we book time off this year?**  
Lea clarified that the process in Fern remains the same. The People & Culture team is only simplifying the wording of related policies, not changing the rules themselves.

**Q: Can we get more clarity on company priorities for the remainder of the year?**  
Marta responded that the leadership team will publish a short priorities document on Grove within the next few weeks. It will be concise and will reference the relevant policy documents where needed.

**Q: How can we support colleagues who are new to remote work?**  
Aylin suggested that existing team members can help by being explicit about communication preferences and by inviting new colleagues to meetings even when attendance is optional. A little inclusion goes a long way.

Marta closed the session by thanking everyone for their attention and for the thoughtful questions. The next all-hands will be announced on Grove in due course.
