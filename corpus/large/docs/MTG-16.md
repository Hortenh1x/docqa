# Meeting Notes — People ops sync (2025-05-22)

## 1. Attendees

- Aylin (VP People & Culture)
- Lea (People Operations)
- Deniz (Customer Success, joined for the onboarding segment)

## 2. Discussion

- Lea walked through the open onboarding checklist for new joiners across the Lisbon and Berlin offices. Most steps are in good shape, but the buddy assignment step is lagging behind. Deniz offered to help route new Customer Success joiners to buddies earlier.
- The team discussed how to make the org chart in Fern more useful for people who work remotely. Aylin noted that several colleagues in Spain and Poland had mentioned they find it hard to tell who sits on which team just from the current view. Lea will check with Platform & Infrastructure whether a team-based view is feasible.
- Deniz raised a pattern she has seen in Customer Success: a few people have been unsure whom to ask when they need equipment or access. The group agreed that the welcome email should point more clearly to the relevant aliases, and that managers should repeat this during the first check-in.
- Aylin shared feedback from the last engagement survey, without quoting figures. The general theme was that people value flexibility but would like more clarity on internal career paths. The team agreed to revisit the topic at the next sync with concrete ideas.
- Lea mentioned that several leave requests for the summer have already come in through Fern, and the approval flow is working smoothly. No bottlenecks were reported.
- The group briefly touched on the upcoming all-hands. Aylin will confirm the agenda with Marta and Jonas, and Lea will prepare a short segment on how to use the policy library in Grove.

## 3. Decisions

- The onboarding buddy assignment should be completed before the new joiner’s first day, not after. Deniz will communicate this expectation to Customer Success managers.
- The welcome email will be updated to include the security and IT help aliases more prominently. Lea will draft the change and run it past Priya.
- The team will not change the Fern leave flow; the current process stays as is.

## 4. Action items

- Lea: draft the updated welcome email and share it with Aylin and Priya for review.
- Lea: check with Platform & Infrastructure whether a team-based org chart view is possible in Fern.
- Deniz: tell Customer Success managers about the buddy timing decision.
- Aylin: prepare the all-hands agenda with Marta and Jonas, and bring career-path ideas to the next sync.
