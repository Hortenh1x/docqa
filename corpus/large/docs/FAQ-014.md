# Data Protection FAQ

## 1. Questions

**Who is responsible for data protection at Kranich?**
Our Head of Information Security, Priya Nayar, leads data protection strategy. For day-to-day questions, reach out to security@kranich.example. For questions about your personal data as an employee, contact people@kranich.example.

**What personal data does Kranich collect about me as an employee?**
Kranich collects the data needed to manage employment, pay, benefits, and IT access. This includes contact details, bank information for salary, emergency contacts, and records of leave and expenses. Details are described in our internal privacy notice, which you can find on Grove.

**Who can see my personal data?**
Only people who need it for their role. People & Culture sees HR records, Finance sees payroll and expense data, and IT sees system logs. We follow a need-to-know principle, and access is reviewed regularly.

**How long is my data kept?**
Retention periods follow legal requirements and operational needs. For specifics, see the retention schedule in POL-004. When a period ends, data is deleted or anonymized.

**Do you share my data with third parties?**
We use a small number of processors, such as Fern for HR, Ledgerly for expenses, and 1Password for credentials. Each processor is bound by a data processing agreement. We never sell personal data.

**How is my data protected?**
We use encryption in transit and at rest, multi-factor authentication, and strict access controls. Security incidents are handled according to our incident response plan, which is owned by Information Security.

**What are my rights as an employee?**
You have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal data, and to restrict or object to certain processing. To exercise a right, email people@kranich.example. We will respond promptly and explain any limitations.

**Example:** Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, wants to see what data Kranich holds about her. She emails people@kranich.example and receives a clear summary of her records within a reasonable time, without needing to explain why.

**Do I need to worry about data protection when working remotely?**
Yes, a little. Use only approved tools, lock your screen when you step away, and avoid discussing customer data in public spaces. If you work from a co-working space, use a privacy screen. For guidance, see POL-004.

**What should I do if I lose a device or suspect a breach?**
Report it immediately to security@kranich.example. Do not try to fix it alone. Early reporting helps us contain any risk. There is no blame for honest mistakes.

**Who is the Data Protection Officer?**
Kranich has appointed a Data Protection Officer. Their contact details are listed on Grove. You may contact them directly if you feel your concerns were not addressed.

**Where can I find the full policy?**
The complete data protection policy is POL-004 on Grove. If you have a question not answered here, ask your manager or People & Culture.
