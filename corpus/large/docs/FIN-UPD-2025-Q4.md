# Finance Update 2025 Q4

Access: Finance only

## 1. Summary

This update covers the financial performance of Kranich Software GmbH for the final quarter of 2025. The document is intended for the Finance team only and provides a concise overview of revenue, costs, and the outlook heading into the new year. Detailed line items and supporting schedules are available in the financial system; please refer to FIN-RATES-2026 for the approved rate card applicable to the coming year.

## 2. Revenue

Revenue grew 5% year over year in 2025 Q4. The increase reflects continued adoption of the Kranich Route Cloud platform across our core customer base in the mid-size logistics segment. Growth was broad-based, with contributions from both the Routing Core and Fleet Insights product families.

Customer retention remained strong throughout the quarter. Several existing customers expanded their usage of the platform, while a number of new logos came on board, particularly in the German and Polish markets. The Customer Success team reported healthy engagement metrics, which we view as a leading indicator for future renewals.

Our sales pipeline entering the quarter was robust, and the Go-to-Market team executed well on the planned campaigns. The field solutions engineer team, including colleagues like Marek working with clients in Poland, played a key role in demonstrating value to prospective customers. We also saw continued interest from prospects in the United Kingdom and France, though those conversations are at an earlier stage.

## 3. Costs

Operating costs for the quarter were in line with the budget set at the beginning of the year. Personnel costs, which form the largest component of our expense base, remained stable as we maintained a deliberate approach to hiring. The Platform & Infrastructure team continued to manage our cloud spend carefully, and we saw no material unexpected cost items during the period.

Travel and meeting expenses were modest, consistent with our remote-first working model. Most employees continue to work from home within their country of employment, and in-person gatherings were limited to essential events. We did not incur significant costs related to office operations beyond the regular maintenance of our Ritterstraße office in Berlin and the Rua do Alecrim office in Lisbon.

Expenses related to internal tools, including our HRIS Fern, the expense platform Ledgerly, and the password manager 1Password, were within the planned annual amounts. No material adjustments or reclassifications were required at quarter end.

## 4. Outlook

Looking ahead to the new year, we expect the positive revenue trend to continue, supported by our product roadmap and the ongoing expansion of the Fleet Insights offering. The product management team, including colleagues like Sofia working remotely from Spain, is focused on delivering features that address the most pressing needs of our logistics customers.

On the cost side, we will maintain the same disciplined approach. We anticipate a few targeted hires in engineering and customer-facing roles, but we will keep overall headcount growth measured. The Finance team will continue to monitor spending closely and will provide updates through the regular quarterly cadence.

We do not foresee any significant changes to our cost structure in the near term. The leadership team, including CEO Marta Lindqvist and CFO Tomasz Zieliński, remains aligned on the strategic priorities for the coming year. Should the business environment change materially, we will communicate promptly through the usual channels.

This update is the final finance communication for the calendar year. As always, please direct any questions to the Finance team.
