# Accessibility at Our Offices

## 1. Purpose

Kranich Software is committed to making our offices welcoming and usable for everyone, including employees, visitors, and clients. This document describes the accessibility features available at each office and how to request adjustments.

We know that accessibility needs are personal and varied. If something described here does not meet your needs, or if you encounter a barrier, please reach out. We will work with you to find a practical solution promptly.

## 2. Berlin

Our Berlin office is located in the Ritterstraße building. The entrance is step-free and accessible from street level. An automatic door opener is available at the main entrance.

Inside, the following features are available:

- Accessible restrooms on every floor
- Elevator access to all levels
- Height-adjustable desks in most team areas
- Quiet room for focused work or sensory breaks
- Clear signage with high-contrast lettering

Meeting rooms have varying layouts. If you need a specific room configuration, such as additional space for a wheelchair or a quieter location, please book through Perch and add a note, or contact Workplace & Operations.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, uses a wheelchair. When she facilitates client workshops, she books a ground-floor meeting room through Perch. The room has a wide entrance and removable chairs, allowing her to position herself comfortably at the table.

If you are hosting an external guest with accessibility needs, let Workplace & Operations know in advance. We can arrange reserved parking near the entrance or adjust the room setup.

## 3. Lisbon

Our Lisbon office is in the Rua do Alecrim building. The main entrance has a ramp, and the reception desk is lowered for easier access. Elevators serve all floors.

Available features include:

- Accessible restroom on each floor
- Step-free routes throughout common areas
- Height-adjustable desks available on request
- Quiet room for sensory breaks or focused work
- Induction loop available in the largest meeting room

The building has a historic facade, and some interior doorways are narrower than in our Berlin office. If you use a mobility aid or have specific space requirements, please speak with Workplace & Operations before your visit or move. We will identify the most suitable desk or meeting room for you.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, has a visual impairment. She requested a desk near a window with adjustable lighting. Workplace & Operations arranged this before her first day, and she also received a larger monitor to reduce eye strain.

If you need assistive technology, such as screen-reading software or ergonomic equipment, please submit a request through the IT help alias or speak with your manager. We will respond promptly and coordinate with Platform & Infrastructure where needed.

## 4. Questions

For questions about accessibility at either office, contact Workplace & Operations via Rui Almeida or the people team. For technology-related accommodations, such as software or hardware adjustments, contact it-help@kranich.example.

If you feel an accessibility concern has not been addressed, you may raise it with your manager, with People & Culture, or directly with Head of Workplace & Operations Rui Almeida. We treat all concerns with care and discretion.

Related policies: see POL-004 for general workplace conduct and FIN-RATES-2026 for travel and expense rules that may apply when arranging accessible transport for business trips.

We continuously review our office environments and welcome suggestions for improvement. Accessibility is not a one-time fix; it is an ongoing commitment to making Kranich a place where everyone can do their best work.
