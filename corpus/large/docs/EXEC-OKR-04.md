# Company OKRs — Q4 2025

Access: Managers only

## 1. Objectives

This quarter, our objectives focus on three areas that will carry Kranich Software into the next fiscal year. First, we want to deepen our footprint with mid-size logistics operators by making our route-optimization platform indispensable to their daily operations. Second, we aim to strengthen the reliability and performance of the Kranich Route Cloud, so that our customers experience fewer disruptions and faster response times. Third, we will invest in our people and internal ways of working, ensuring that our remote-first culture remains a source of strength as we grow.

These objectives build on the progress we made earlier in 2025. They are deliberately ambitious but achievable, and each one is paired with measurable key results that will tell us clearly whether we have succeeded. Managers should use this document to align their team-level goals and to communicate priorities to their reports.

## 2. Key results

For the objective on market penetration, the key result targets 25% growth in active fleets. This means we want the number of fleets actively using our platform to increase by that measure by the end of the quarter. We will track this through our internal product analytics and through the Customer Success team’s account reviews.

For the objective on platform reliability, we will track the reduction of critical incidents and the improvement of our average response time to customer-reported issues. We will also measure the successful rollout of a planned platform upgrade, which should complete without major disruption to existing customers.

For the objective on people and culture, we will measure employee engagement through our quarterly pulse survey and track the completion of manager training on remote leadership. We will also review our internal tools, including Fern and Grove, to ensure they support a smooth experience for employees across all countries where we operate.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, will use the platform reliability key results to prioritize which customers to check in with after the planned upgrade. Her outreach will focus on those who have reported issues in the past, so that we can proactively address concerns before they escalate.

## 3. Owners

The Leadership Team owns this document and its overall outcomes. Each objective has a designated lead from the Leadership Team, who is accountable for coordinating the work across relevant teams.

- Market penetration objective: Marta Lindqvist, CEO, with support from the Go-to-Market team and Customer Success.
- Platform reliability objective: Jonas Weber, CTO, with support from Platform & Infrastructure and Routing Core.
- People and culture objective: Aylin Demir, VP People & Culture, with support from all managers.

Ines Duarte, engineering manager in Routing Core, will act as the liaison between the engineering teams and the Leadership Team for the platform reliability objective. Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, will contribute to the planned upgrade as part of her team’s quarterly commitments. Sofia, product manager for Fleet Insights, will align her product roadmap with the market penetration objective.

## 4. Review

We will review progress against these OKRs at the Leadership Team’s weekly meeting. A formal mid-quarter review will take place in November, where each owner will present an update on their key results. At that point, we may adjust the key results if the underlying assumptions have changed, but we will not change the objectives themselves.

Managers should hold a team-level review within a few days after the mid-quarter leadership review, using the same structure. They should share relevant updates with their teams and collect feedback on any obstacles. The final review for the quarter will happen in January of the next year, during our annual planning cycle.

If you have questions about this document or how it applies to your team, please reach out to your manager or to the relevant owner listed in §3. For questions related to people processes, you can contact people@kranich.example. For technical questions about the platform upgrade, please involve your engineering manager. We appreciate your commitment to making this quarter a success for Kranich Software and for our customers.
