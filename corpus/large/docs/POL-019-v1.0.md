# Business Travel Insurance

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains the business travel insurance that Kranich Software GmbH provides to employees when they travel for work. It describes what is covered, how to make a claim, and where to direct questions.

This version supersedes version 0.9 of this policy. We have updated the policy to reflect our current insurance arrangements and to make the claims process clearer.

Travel is an important part of working at Kranich. Whether you are visiting a client, attending a conference, or spending time at one of our offices, we want you to travel with confidence. This insurance is meant to protect you against unexpected events while you are away on company business.

## 2. What is covered

The business travel insurance covers you for trips that are approved by your manager and booked through our travel process. This includes travel between our offices, travel to meet clients, and travel to industry events where you represent Kranich.

The policy covers a range of situations that can arise during business travel. These include:

- Medical costs abroad are covered up to €100,000 per trip. This covers treatment you need because of an illness or injury that happens while you are traveling.
- Emergency medical evacuation, if you need to be moved to a facility that can provide appropriate care.
- Trip cancellation or interruption, if you must cancel or cut short your trip because of a covered reason, such as a serious illness or a family emergency.
- Lost or delayed baggage, up to reasonable replacement costs for essential items.
- Travel delay, if your journey is delayed for a covered reason and you incur additional expenses for meals or accommodation.
- Personal liability, if you are held responsible for damage or injury to a third party while traveling.

The insurance applies from the moment you leave home for your trip until you return home. It covers you for the entire duration of your business travel, including personal time you take during the trip, such as an evening out or a free day on the weekend.

Travel that is not covered includes trips that are entirely personal and not connected to your work at Kranich. If you extend a business trip for personal reasons, the insurance continues to cover you only for the portion of the trip that is related to business. The personal extension is not covered.

The insurance also does not cover situations that arise from deliberate acts, reckless behavior, or the influence of alcohol or non-prescribed substances. If you have a pre-existing medical condition, please check with the insurance provider before you travel to confirm whether treatment for that condition is covered.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, travels to Berlin for a project workshop. While she is there, she slips on a wet floor in the office and injures her ankle. She visits a local clinic for treatment. The medical costs she incurs are covered under this policy, up to the coverage limit for medical costs abroad.

## 3. Making a claim

If something happens during your business trip that may lead to a claim, you should act promptly. The first step is to gather the relevant documentation. This includes receipts, medical reports, police reports if applicable, and any correspondence with airlines, hotels, or other providers.

Claims must be filed within 13 calendar days of the event. This deadline applies from the day the incident occurred, not from the day you return from your trip. For example, if you have a medical emergency on a Tuesday, your claim must be filed within 13 calendar days of that Tuesday.

To file a claim, you will need to complete the claim form provided by our insurance provider. The form asks for details about the trip, the incident, and the costs you incurred. You should attach all supporting documentation when you submit the form.

Once you have submitted the claim form, the insurance provider will review your case. They may contact you if they need additional information. You should respond to their requests promptly so that your claim can be processed without delay.

After the insurance provider approves your claim, the reimbursement is handled through the expense process described in POL-004. You will receive the approved amount through that process, just as you would for any other business expense.

If the insurance provider rejects your claim, you will receive an explanation. You may appeal the decision if you believe it was made in error. Please contact the Finance team if you need help understanding the decision or the appeals process.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, travels to a client site in France. During the trip, her luggage is delayed for several days. She buys a few essential items and keeps all her receipts. When she returns, she files a claim within the required timeframe. The insurance provider approves the claim, and the reimbursement is paid through the expense process.

## 4. Questions

If you have questions about this policy or about a specific trip, please reach out. The Finance team can help you understand what is covered and how the claims process works.

For questions about whether a particular trip is eligible for coverage, or about how to book travel, please contact your manager or the Workplace & Operations team.

If you need to file a claim or want to check the status of an existing claim, you can contact the insurance provider directly. The provider's contact details are available on the travel page of our intranet, Grove.

For general questions about expenses and reimbursement, please refer to POL-004, which explains the expense process in detail.

We want you to travel safely and to feel supported when things do not go as planned. If you are ever unsure about whether a situation is covered, please ask before you assume. It is better to check in advance than to discover after the fact that a cost is not covered.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer who works with clients in Poland, plans a trip to visit a client in the United Kingdom. He is unsure whether the insurance covers a planned stopover for a personal visit. He contacts the Finance team before booking, and they confirm that the business portion of the trip is covered while the personal portion is not. With this clarity, Marek books his travel with confidence.
