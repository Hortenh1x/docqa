# Meeting Notes — People ops sync (2025-01-19)

## 1. Attendees

- Aylin (VP People & Culture)
- Lea (People Operations)
- Priya (Head of Information Security)
- Rui (Head of Workplace & Operations)
- Deniz (Customer Success, joining for a segment)

## 2. Discussion

- **Welcome & onboarding flow.** Lea walked through the current onboarding checklist. The group agreed the handoff between People Operations and Platform & Infrastructure could be smoother, especially for remote hires in Poland and Spain. Priya suggested adding a security briefing earlier in the sequence, before new joiners receive device access.

- **Fern updates.** Aylin mentioned that the org chart in Fern is occasionally out of date when people change teams. Lea will coordinate with team leads to make updating Fern a habit rather than a periodic cleanup. Rui noted that desk booking in Perch works well but that meeting-room names in the Lisbon office confuse visitors; he will rename them with clearer labels.

- **Internal mobility.** Deniz joined to share a Customer Success perspective. She observed that some colleagues are interested in moving between teams but do not know whom to ask. The group discussed publishing short "day in the life" notes on Grove from people who recently switched roles. This would be informal and voluntary.

- **Policy review cadence.** Aylin mentioned that several policies are due for a light review. The team agreed to check which documents are referenced most often by employees and to prioritize those. No specific policies were named yet.

- **Remote work ergonomics.** Rui raised that some home-office setups could be improved, particularly for newer colleagues. The group discussed creating a simple guide with practical tips rather than a formal policy. Priya added that the guide should not include any sensitive information and should link to the security basics already published.

## 3. Decisions

- A security briefing will be added to the onboarding sequence, placed before device access is granted.
- Meeting-room names in the Lisbon office will be renamed for clarity.
- People Operations will publish voluntary "day in the life" notes on Grove to support internal mobility conversations.
- A practical home-office ergonomics guide will be drafted as an informal resource.

## 4. Action items

- Lea: update the onboarding checklist and coordinate with Platform & Infrastructure on the new security briefing.
- Rui: rename Lisbon meeting rooms and update Perch accordingly.
- Aylin: reach out to a few colleagues who recently changed teams and invite them to write short notes for Grove.
- Lea: draft the ergonomics guide and share it with Rui for review before publishing.
- Priya: confirm the security briefing content and provide it to Lea within the next week.
