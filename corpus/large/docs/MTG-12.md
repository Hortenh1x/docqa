# Meeting Notes — Customer Success review (2025-01-10)

## 1. Attendees

Deniz (Customer Success, Berlin), Marek (Field Solutions, Poland), Sofia (Product, Fleet Insights), Anna (backend engineer, Routing Core, Lisbon), and Jonas (CTO).

## 2. Discussion

- Deniz opened with a walkthrough of the recent quarterly pulse we ran with customers. The overall sentiment is positive, but a few recurring themes came up around onboarding and reporting clarity.
- Marek shared feedback from two logistics operators in Poland. Both appreciate the routing engine’s reliability, yet they find the initial setup documentation dense. They asked for more hands-on guidance during the first weeks.
- Sofia noted that several Fleet Insights customers have requested a simpler way to export daily summaries. She believes a small product tweak could reduce support tickets noticeably, though she did not quantify the expected impact.
- Anna mentioned that the Routing Core team has seen an uptick in questions about how the algorithm handles time windows. She offered to prepare a short technical explainer for the Customer Success team to share.
- Jonas reminded everyone that the platform team is currently focused on stability work. Any new feature requests from customers should go through the usual prioritization process rather than being handled ad hoc.
- The group briefly discussed the handoff between Customer Success and Go-to-Market. Deniz feels the current process works, but there is room to improve how customer feedback reaches the product teams.
- Marek raised the idea of recording a short demo video in Polish. Sofia and Anna both offered to review the script for technical accuracy.

## 3. Decisions

- The group will create a short, plain-language onboarding guide for new customers, drawing on Marek’s field notes and Anna’s technical input.
- Fleet Insights will explore a lightweight export option for daily summaries. Sofia will draft a brief proposal and share it with Jonas before the next review.
- Customer Success will route technical questions about routing behavior to Anna’s explainer once it is ready.
- No changes to the current support escalation path were agreed; the group will revisit this topic in a future meeting.

## 4. Action items

- Anna: draft the technical explainer on time-window handling and share it with Deniz and Marek by the end of the month.
- Sofia: prepare the export proposal and send it to Jonas for feedback within a few days.
- Marek: collect examples of common onboarding questions from his recent client sessions and send them to Deniz.
- Deniz: compile the feedback themes from the pulse and share a summary with Sofia and Anna.
- Jonas: review Sofia’s proposal and Anna’s explainer promptly once they are shared.
