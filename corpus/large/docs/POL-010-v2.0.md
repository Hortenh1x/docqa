# Internal Data Protection Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how Kranich Software GmbH protects the personal data of our employees, applicants, clients and business partners. It applies to everyone who works with us, no matter where they are based or whether they work from home or from one of our offices.

This version supersedes version 1.5. We have updated the policy to reflect how our teams work today and to make our data protection commitments clearer.

We take data protection seriously because it is the right thing to do, and because our clients trust us with sensitive routing and fleet information. When we handle personal data, we do so lawfully, fairly and transparently. We only collect what we need, we only use it for the purpose we collected it for, and we delete it when we no longer need it.

If you are unsure whether a particular use of personal data is allowed, ask before you act. The Legal team and the Head of Information Security are happy to help.

## 2. Personal data we handle

We handle personal data in several contexts. The most common ones are described below.

**Employee data.** We hold the personal data of our employees in Fern, our HRIS. This includes contact details, emergency contact information, bank details for payroll, and records relating to leave and absences. We also hold performance-related notes that managers keep in line with our people processes.

**Applicant data.** When someone applies for a role with us, we collect their CV, cover letter and any other documents they choose to share. We may also collect notes from interviews. We only use this data to assess the application.

**Client and partner data.** Our Customer Success and Go-to-Market teams hold contact details for people at client and partner organisations. We use this data to deliver our services, to send relevant product updates, and to manage our commercial relationships. We do not sell this data to anyone.

**Operational data.** Some personal data appears incidentally in the operational data we process. For example, routing data from a client may contain driver names or delivery addresses. Fleet Insights data may include vehicle usage patterns that relate to an individual driver. We treat such data with the same care as any other personal data.

**Office and workplace data.** We operate access systems at our offices in Berlin and Lisbon. These systems log who enters the offices and when. We also hold booking records from Perch, our desk and meeting-room tool. This data helps us keep our workplaces safe and well organised.

We do not collect special categories of personal data unless the law requires it or you have given us your explicit consent. If you become aware of special category data being processed without a clear legal basis, flag it to security@kranich.example promptly.

## 3. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it for the purpose for which it was collected. After that, we delete it securely or anonymise it so that it can no longer be linked to an individual.

Employee data is kept for the duration of the employment relationship and then for the period required by law. We review our retention schedules regularly to make sure we are not keeping more than we need.

Applicant data is deleted 5 years after the hiring decision. This applies whether the application was successful or not. If you are involved in hiring, make sure you do not keep applicant documents in personal folders, email inboxes or on your device. All applicant data belongs in the recruitment tooling that Legal and People & Culture have approved.

Client and partner contact data is reviewed at least once a year. If we have not had meaningful contact with someone for a while, we remove their details from our systems.

Office access logs and Perch booking records are kept for a limited period, after which they are deleted automatically. If you need to keep a record for a specific purpose, such as investigating an incident, speak to the Head of Workplace & Operations first.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, receives a CV from a friend who has applied for a role on her team. Anna saves the CV to her desktop so she can review it over the weekend. When the hiring process concludes, Anna remembers to delete the file from her desktop. She does not need to keep it because the recruitment tool already holds the official record, and that record will be deleted 5 years after the hiring decision.

## 4. Requests from data subjects

Anyone whose personal data we hold has the right to ask us what we hold, why we hold it, and what we do with it. They may also ask us to correct, restrict or delete their data.

Data subject requests are answered within 20 calendar days. If a request is complex or we need more time, we will tell the person promptly and explain why. We never charge a fee for a simple request.

If you receive a data subject request, do not ignore it. Forward it to security@kranich.example on the same day you receive it. Do not try to handle the request yourself, even if the person asks you directly. The Legal team and the Head of Information Security coordinate our response.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, receives an email from a contact at a client company asking what personal data Kranich holds about them. Deniz forwards the email to security@kranich.example right away. She does not reply to the client herself because she knows the response must be coordinated centrally to ensure it is complete and timely.

If a former employee, an applicant or a client contact makes a request, the same process applies. We verify the identity of the person making the request before we disclose anything. This protects everyone's data.

## 5. Working with processors

We use external service providers to help us run our business. Some of these providers process personal data on our behalf. Examples include our cloud hosting provider, our payroll provider and the vendors behind our internal tools such as Fern, Ledgerly and Grove.

Before we engage a processor, we make sure they can provide sufficient guarantees about their technical and organisational measures. We have written contracts with all our processors. These contracts set out what they may do with the data, how they must protect it, and what happens when the engagement ends.

You must not sign up for a new tool or service that will process personal data without going through our procurement process. This applies even if the tool is free or has a trial period. If you need a tool for your team, raise it with your manager and with Platform & Infrastructure. They will involve Legal and Information Security as needed.

If you work with a processor directly, for example by uploading a client contact list to a marketing tool, check first that the tool is on our approved list. The approved list is maintained by Platform & Infrastructure and published on Grove. If a tool is not on the list, do not use it for personal data.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, wants to use a new analytics service to understand client meeting patterns. The service would require him to upload client contact details. Marek checks the approved tool list on Grove and sees that the service is not listed. He contacts Platform & Infrastructure before proceeding, and they confirm that the service has not yet been reviewed. Marek waits until the review is complete.

We review our processors regularly. If a processor suffers a data breach that affects personal data we are responsible for, they must notify us without undue delay. If you hear about a breach involving one of our processors, tell security@kranich.example immediately.

## 6. Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, or about data protection in your day-to-day work, there are several ways to get help.

For questions about a specific piece of personal data, or about a request you have received, contact security@kranich.example. This mailbox is monitored by the Head of Information Security and the Legal team.

For questions about people-related data, such as employee records in Fern or applicant data, contact people@kranich.example. The People & Culture team can help you understand what you may collect and how long you may keep it.

For questions about tools and processors, contact it-help@kranich.example. The Platform & Infrastructure team can tell you whether a tool is approved and how to request a review.

If you are unsure whether a situation falls under this policy, ask. There is no penalty for asking a question. There can be consequences for processing personal data carelessly, particularly if it leads to a breach or harms someone whose data we hold.

Thank you for helping us keep personal data safe. Data protection is everyone's responsibility, and your care makes a real difference to the people we work with and the clients we serve.
