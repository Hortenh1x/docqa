# Data Processing Summary

## 1. Scope

This summary explains how Kranich Software GmbH processes personal data when customers use the Kranich Route Cloud. It is written for customers and prospective customers who want a clear, plain-language overview of our data handling practices.

The Kranich Route Cloud is a route-optimization platform for mid-size logistics operators. Our customers provide us with data about their vehicles, drivers, stops, and delivery schedules so that our software can generate efficient routes. In the course of providing this service, we process personal data on behalf of our customers.

This document covers the core processing activities that take place when a customer uses the Kranich Route Cloud. It does not cover how we process data about visitors to our website or how we handle our own business contacts — those activities are described in separate documentation available on request.

## 2. Roles

Kranich Software GmbH acts as a processor for most of the personal data that flows through the Kranich Route Cloud. Our customers are the controllers: they decide which driver names, contact details, and delivery instructions to upload, and they determine the purposes of the processing.

For example, when a customer in Poland uploads a delivery manifest that includes driver names and phone numbers, the customer remains responsible for ensuring that it has a lawful basis to share that data with us. We process the data only according to the customer's documented instructions, as set out in our Data Processing Agreement.

In some limited situations, we act as a controller in our own right. This applies, for instance, to basic account administration data such as the name and work email address of the person who manages the customer's subscription. We need this information to run our business relationship, send service notices, and handle billing. We also act as a controller for aggregated, de-identified usage statistics that help us improve the reliability and performance of the platform. These statistics never identify individual drivers or end customers.

Where we act as a processor, our customers are responsible for responding to data subject requests. We support them by providing the technical means to export, correct, or delete data within the platform, and we respond promptly to any direct requests we receive by pointing the individual to the relevant customer.

## 3. Sub-processors

To deliver the Kranich Route Cloud, we engage a small number of sub-processors. A sub-processor is a third party that we authorise to process personal data on our behalf. We review our sub-processor list regularly and update it whenever we add, replace, or remove a provider.

Our sub-processors fall into a few categories. First, we use cloud infrastructure providers to host the platform. These providers operate data centers in the European Union and give us the computing and storage capacity we need. Second, we use a provider for our customer support ticketing system, where support conversations and related case notes are stored. Third, we use a provider for error and performance monitoring; this provider may receive limited technical data, such as error logs, that can occasionally contain fragments of personal data.

Before we engage any sub-processor, we assess its security posture and enter into a data processing agreement with it. Each sub-processor is bound to process personal data only for the purposes we specify, and only in accordance with our instructions.

Customers who have signed our Data Processing Agreement receive advance notice of any new sub-processor. If a customer objects to a new sub-processor on reasonable grounds, we work together to find an alternative arrangement. A current list of sub-processors is always available in the customer portal, and customers may also request it directly from their account team.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not permit our sub-processors to use personal data for their own purposes, including advertising or profiling.

## 4. Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as it is needed to provide the Kranich Route Cloud service or to comply with legal obligations.

The primary rule is simple: we keep the personal data that customers upload for the duration of their subscription. When a customer deletes data through the platform interface, we remove it from our active systems promptly. When a subscription ends, we begin a deletion process that removes customer data from our systems within a reasonable period, unless we are required by law to keep certain records for longer.

Some data is retained for shorter periods by design. For example, operational logs that support troubleshooting and security monitoring are kept only for a limited time before they are automatically overwritten or deleted.

In cases where we act as a controller — such as account administration data — we keep the data for as long as the business relationship continues and for a short period afterwards to handle any outstanding matters. After that, we delete or anonymise it.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, works with a customer that decides not to renew its subscription. Deniz coordinates the offboarding, and the customer exports its historical route data through the self-service export feature. Once the subscription period ends, the deletion process runs automatically, and the customer receives confirmation that its data has been removed.

Customers who need to understand our retention practices in more detail should refer to the retention schedule in their Data Processing Agreement. We are happy to answer specific questions through the customer portal or the account team.
