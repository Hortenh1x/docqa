# Announcement: New Travel Policy version

## 1. Announcement

Dear colleagues,

We are pleased to announce that a new version of our Travel Policy, POL-004, is now in effect. This update reflects how we work today — across borders, from home offices, and with our teams in Berlin and Lisbon — and makes the rules clearer and more consistent for everyone.

The new version of POL-004 has been revised with your feedback in mind. In recent months, we heard from many of you that the previous guidance left room for interpretation, especially around booking practices, expense approvals, and what to do when plans change. The updated policy addresses these points directly.

What has changed:

- **Clearer booking guidance.** The policy now explains, step by step, which tools to use and when to involve our Workplace & Operations team.
- **Simplified expense rules.** We have consolidated the reimbursement guidance so that it is easier to follow, whether you travel occasionally or regularly for client work.
- **Updated sustainability considerations.** For shorter journeys, the policy encourages you to consider rail alternatives before booking flights.
- **A dedicated section on cancellations and changes.** We know plans shift; the new policy explains what to do promptly and whom to contact.

A few practical notes:

- The policy applies to all travel booked on or after the effective date above, regardless of when the trip was originally planned.
- If you have an upcoming booking that was made under the previous version, you do not need to rebook. The new rules will apply from your next booking onward.
- Questions about specific trips can be directed to your manager or to the Workplace & Operations team via it-help@kranich.example.

We encourage you to review POL-004 in the policy library on Grove. For a quick overview, the summary page has been updated as well.

Thank you for your patience while we worked on this revision. We believe the new policy will make travel simpler for everyone — whether you are heading to a client site for the day or joining a team gathering abroad.

If you have suggestions for future improvements, please share them with People & Culture at people@kranich.example.

Warm regards,

Aylin Demir
VP People & Culture
