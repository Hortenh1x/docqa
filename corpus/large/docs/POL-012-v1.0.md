# Home Office Allowance Policy

## 1. Purpose

Kranich Software GmbH is a remote-first company. Most of us work from home within our country of employment, and we want that experience to be comfortable and productive. This policy explains the financial support Finance provides for setting up and maintaining your home workspace.

This version of the policy supersedes version 0.9. It clarifies what the allowance covers, how to claim it, and who to contact with questions.

The allowance described here is separate from the equipment budget. For information on company-provided hardware and peripherals, please refer to POL-006 §3.

This policy applies to all employees, regardless of whether you work from Berlin, Lisbon, or remotely from another country. The allowance is paid in your local currency at the applicable exchange rate, but the amounts in this policy are stated in euros for clarity.

## 2. One-off setup allowance

When you first join Kranich, or when you move to a new permanent home office location, you may claim a one-off setup allowance. The one-off home office setup allowance is €450.

This allowance is intended to help you make your workspace comfortable and ergonomic. You can use it for items such as a desk, an office chair, a monitor stand, a footrest, or a lamp. You may also use it for smaller accessories like a keyboard, a mouse, or cable management.

You are not required to spend the full amount, and you do not need to return the unused portion. The allowance is yours to use as you see fit for your workspace. Any items you purchase with the allowance remain your personal property, including if you leave Kranich later.

If you move to a new home and need to set up a second workspace, you may claim the setup allowance again. This applies to a move to a new residence, not to rearranging a room within the same home.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, recently moved to a new apartment. She used the setup allowance to buy a standing desk and a new office chair for her home office.

## 3. Monthly connectivity allowance

To support the cost of a reliable internet connection and mobile data, you receive a recurring allowance. The connectivity allowance is €25 per month.

This monthly allowance is intended to contribute toward your home internet service and, if relevant, a mobile data plan for work purposes. It is paid regardless of whether you use your home internet for work every day or only occasionally.

The allowance is paid in your regular monthly payroll. It is subject to the same tax treatment as your salary in your country of employment. If you have questions about how this is handled in your specific country, please reach out to People & Culture or Finance.

You do not need to submit receipts for the connectivity allowance. It is a flat amount, not a reimbursement of actual costs. Whether your internet bill is higher or lower than the allowance does not affect the payment.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, relies on a fast home connection between client visits. The monthly allowance helps cover his internet bill.

## 4. Claiming

You claim the one-off setup allowance through Ledgerly, our expenses and reimbursement tool. The process is straightforward and takes only a few minutes.

To claim, follow these steps:

- Log in to Ledgerly with your Kranich account.
- Select "New expense" and choose the category "Home office setup."
- Attach a photo or PDF of your receipt. The receipt must show the date of purchase and the total amount. A receipt in any currency is accepted; Ledgerly will convert it for Finance.
- Add a short note describing the item, for example "office chair" or "desk lamp."
- Submit the expense for approval.

Your manager will receive a notification to approve the expense. Once approved, Finance processes the reimbursement promptly. The money is transferred to your bank account via payroll or a separate transfer, depending on your country of employment.

You may submit several smaller receipts together, as long as the total does not exceed your remaining setup allowance. You can also submit a single receipt for the full amount. There is no need to wait until you have purchased everything.

If you are claiming the setup allowance after a move, select the same expense category and add a note that says "new home setup" so Finance can distinguish it from your initial claim.

The monthly connectivity allowance does not require a claim. It is added automatically to your payroll each month, starting from your first full month of employment. If you join mid-month, the allowance for that first partial month is included in the following month's payroll.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, bought a monitor stand and a keyboard shortly after joining. She submitted both receipts in one claim through Ledgerly and received the reimbursement within a few days.

## 5. Questions

If you have questions about this policy, your first point of contact is your manager. They can help with practical questions about what to purchase or how to set up your workspace.

For questions about the claim process, receipts, or payment timing, please contact Finance through Ledgerly or by reaching out to the finance team. For questions about how the allowance interacts with your employment contract or tax situation in your country, please contact People & Culture at people@kranich.example.

If you believe your setup allowance was not applied correctly, or if you did not receive your monthly connectivity allowance, please raise this with Finance promptly. We will investigate and correct any errors.

Example: Sofia, a product manager in Fleet Insights working remotely from Spain, was unsure whether a second monitor stand qualified under the setup allowance. She asked her manager, who confirmed it did, and she submitted the receipt the same day.

We want everyone at Kranich to have a workspace that supports their wellbeing and productivity. If there is something about your home office that is not working well, please talk to us. This policy is meant to be helpful, and we review it periodically to make sure it stays that way.
