# Meeting Notes — Workplace committee (2025-04-19)

## 1. Attendees

- Aylin (VP People & Culture, chair)
- Rui (Head of Workplace & Operations)
- Priya (Head of Information Security)
- Lea (People Operations)
- Deniz (Customer Success, Berlin)
- Ines (Engineering Manager, Routing Core, Berlin)

Apologies: Marta, Jonas.

## 2. Discussion

- **Berlin office refresh.** Rui walked the group through the proposed layout changes for the Ritterstraße office. The main topic was how to add more quiet zones without reducing collaboration space. The committee discussed feedback collected from employees who use the office a few days per week; several mentioned that the current open-plan areas get loud in the afternoon.
- **Lisbon office.** Ines raised a request from the Lisbon team for better video-conferencing equipment in the Rua do Alecrim office. Remote participants in hybrid meetings often struggle to hear. Priya noted that any new equipment must go through the standard security review before being connected to the network.
- **Desk booking behaviour.** Deniz shared an observation from colleagues: some people book a desk via Perch but then work from home without cancelling, which leaves empty desks for others. The group agreed this is a courtesy issue rather than a policy gap.
- **New joiner onboarding space.** Lea mentioned that the Berlin office will host more new joiners later this year. The committee discussed whether the current welcome area is sufficient or whether a dedicated onboarding corner would help.
- **Sustainability.** Rui briefly mentioned that the office supply ordering process could be reviewed to reduce waste. No concrete proposal yet; Rui will bring options to the next meeting.

## 3. Decisions

- **Proceed with the Berlin quiet-zone pilot.** The committee approved the proposed layout for a trial period. Rui will coordinate with the affected teams before implementation.
- **Prioritise the Lisbon video-conferencing upgrade.** The committee agreed this should move ahead promptly. Priya will confirm the security review timeline.
- **Reminder about Perch bookings.** The committee decided not to introduce automatic penalties. Instead, a friendly reminder will be posted on Grove, and managers will be asked to mention it in team meetings.

## 4. Action items

- Rui: share the Berlin layout plan with affected teams and set a start date for the quiet-zone pilot.
- Rui: prepare the Lisbon equipment request and send it to Priya for the security review.
- Priya: complete the security review for the Lisbon equipment.
- Lea: draft the Grove reminder about cancelling unused Perch bookings.
- Aylin: bring the onboarding space topic to the next leadership meeting for input.
- All: review the sustainability options Rui prepares and come to the next meeting with feedback.
