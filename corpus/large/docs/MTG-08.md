# Meeting Notes — People ops sync (2025-09-25)

## 1. Attendees

Aylin, Lea, and Priya joined from Berlin; Anna dialed in from Lisbon. Deniz and Marek sent apologies but shared written updates beforehand.

## 2. Discussion

- **Welcome and onboarding flow.** Lea walked through the current experience for new joiners, from offer to first day. She noted that feedback from recent hires has been consistently positive, but there is room to improve how equipment arrives before the start date. Anna shared that the Lisbon office sometimes sees delays with local vendors; the group agreed to revisit the vendor list there.

- **Remote and hybrid patterns.** The group discussed how teams are using the Ritterstraße and Rua do Alecrim offices. Rui had shared a summary from Perch showing that desk usage varies a lot by team. Platform & Infrastructure tends to book desks more often than Customer Success, for example. No one sees a problem yet, but Priya suggested keeping an eye on whether hot-desking creates any security concerns when people work from shared spaces.

- **Performance review season.** Aylin reminded everyone that the next review cycle is approaching. Routing Core and Fleet Insights managers have asked for clearer guidance on writing constructive feedback. Lea will prepare a short refresher note and share it with all managers ahead of the cycle.

- **Well-being and workload.** Deniz’s written update flagged that some Customer Success managers have mentioned feeling stretched during peak implementation weeks. The group talked about how to make workload visible earlier, without adding bureaucracy. Anna suggested that engineering already uses a lightweight check-in for this; the people team will look at whether a similar approach fits other teams.

- **Learning budget usage.** Sofia had asked whether the team learning budget is being used evenly across departments. Lea confirmed that usage varies, and Go-to-Market has been particularly active. The group agreed that underspending is not a concern this year, but communication could be clearer about what kinds of learning are eligible.

## 3. Decisions

- The Lisbon office will switch to a new equipment vendor, pending a quick check by Rui.
- Lea will draft a manager refresher on feedback writing, to be shared before the review cycle opens.
- People & Culture will not change the current well-being approach; instead, the team will explore light-touch workload check-ins modeled on engineering practice.

## 4. Action items

- Lea: prepare the manager feedback refresher and share it with Aylin for review.
- Anna: coordinate with Rui on the Lisbon vendor switch and confirm timing.
- Priya: share a short note on hot-desking security tips with Rui.
- Aylin: bring the workload check-in idea to the next leadership meeting.
