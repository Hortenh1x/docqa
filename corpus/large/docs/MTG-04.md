# Meeting Notes — Customer Success review (2025-05-13)

## 1. Attendees

- Deniz (Customer Success, Berlin)
- Marek (Field Solutions, Poland)
- Anna (Routing Core, Lisbon)
- Sofia (Fleet Insights, remote from Spain)
- Jonas Weber (CTO)
- Aylin Demir (VP People & Culture)

## 2. Discussion

- Deniz opened with a recap of recent client conversations. Several mid-size logistics operators have asked for clearer guidance on how the Kranich Route Cloud handles last-mile exceptions, especially during peak season.
- Marek shared feedback from Polish clients: the onboarding demo is well received, but follow-up documentation could be more tailored to each operator's fleet size and typical delivery patterns.
- Anna noted that Routing Core is about to release a change that affects how routes are recalculated mid-shift. The Customer Success team should be aware so they can set expectations with clients.
- Sofia flagged that Fleet Insights has seen an uptick in questions about driver app notifications. She proposed a short knowledge-base article, but the team agreed to first align on the underlying product behaviour.
- The group discussed the current rhythm of daily standups. Several attendees felt meetings drift beyond their intended scope, especially when multiple teams join.
- Jonas suggested that Customer Success and Product should share a single, focused standup rather than running separate ones. This would reduce context switching for engineers like Anna who support both sides.
- Aylin reminded everyone that any change to meeting cadence should be communicated through the team calendar and noted in Grove so remote colleagues in different time zones are not left out.

## 3. Decisions

- Daily standups are capped at 14 minutes.
- Customer Success and Product will hold one joint daily standup, effective starting next week.
- A short knowledge-base article on driver app notifications will be drafted only after the product behaviour is confirmed.

## 4. Action items

- Deniz will draft a short summary of client questions about last-mile exceptions and share it with Anna by the end of the week.
- Anna will post a note in the Routing Core channel about the upcoming recalculation change, with a pointer for Customer Success.
- Sofia will check with the Fleet Insights team on the notification behaviour and report back at the next review.
- Marek will share the Polish client feedback on documentation with the broader Customer Success group.
- Aylin will update the team calendar and add a note to Grove about the new standup format and time cap.
