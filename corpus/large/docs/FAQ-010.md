# Parental Leave FAQ

## 1. Questions

**Who is eligible for parental leave?**
All employees, regardless of gender, parental role, or how the child joins your family, are eligible. This includes birth parents, adoptive parents, and parents through surrogacy. Eligibility starts on your first day of employment.

**How do I request parental leave?**
Submit your request through Fern at least a few weeks before your intended leave start date. Lea, our people operations specialist, will confirm receipt and guide you through the next steps. For birth parents, we ask that you also notify your manager as early as reasonably possible so we can plan coverage.

**What does the leave period look like?**
The total leave period and how it can be split are described in POL-004. You may choose to take your leave continuously or in blocks, depending on your circumstances and what your team can accommodate. Please discuss your preferred schedule with your manager before submitting the formal request.

**What will I be paid during leave?**
Your pay during parental leave follows the provisions in POL-004. In some locations, government schemes provide additional support, and we coordinate with those programs where applicable. For details specific to your country of employment, refer to POL-004 or ask Lea.

**Can my partner and I both take leave?**
Yes. Each parent is entitled to their own leave under POL-004, regardless of what your partner’s employer offers. You do not need to coordinate your schedules unless you wish to.

**What happens to my benefits while I am on leave?**
Your employment continues throughout your leave. Health insurance and other standard benefits remain in place as described in POL-004. If you have questions about a specific benefit, contact people@kranich.example.

**Example:**
Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, is expecting a child. She reads POL-004 to understand her entitlements, then discusses her preferred schedule with Ines, her engineering manager. Anna submits her request in Fern, and Lea confirms everything is set. During her leave, Anna stays in touch with her team through optional monthly check-ins, but she is not expected to work.

**Can I change my leave dates after approval?**
Yes, within reason. Life rarely goes exactly as planned. If your circumstances change, contact Lea as soon as possible. She will help you adjust your request, provided the new dates still meet the requirements in POL-004 and your team can adapt.

**Do I keep my job when I return?**
Yes. You return to the same role, or to a comparable role if your team structure has changed while you were away. Your manager will arrange a reintegration conversation before your return so you are up to date on any developments.

**Who do I contact with further questions?**
Start with Lea in People & Culture at people@kranich.example. For questions about how leave interacts with your specific situation, she will connect you with the right person. If you have concerns about confidentiality or fairness, you may also reach out to Aylin Demir, VP People & Culture, directly.
