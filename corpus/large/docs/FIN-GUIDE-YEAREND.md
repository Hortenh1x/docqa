# Year-End Close Guide

## 1. Purpose

This guide explains how Kranich Software GmbH closes its books at the end of each calendar year. The year-end close is how we make sure our financial records are complete, accurate, and ready for our annual reporting.

We know that year-end can feel like a busy time for everyone. This guide is meant to take the mystery out of the process. It tells you what happens, when it happens, and — most importantly — what we need from you so that everything runs smoothly.

The Finance team runs the close, but a good close depends on all of us. When every team submits their information on time and in the right format, we can finish the close promptly and get back to our regular work.

## 2. Timeline

The year-end close follows the calendar year. It begins right after the new year starts and runs for a few weeks. We publish a detailed schedule on Grove before the close begins, so please check there for the exact dates.

The close has three broad phases:

- **Preparation.** Before the year ends, Finance sends reminders about what is coming. This is the time to tidy up anything you know will be needed.
- **Submission.** After the new year starts, all teams submit their final information for the year that just ended. This is the busiest part of the close.
- **Review and reporting.** Finance reviews everything, follows up on anything unclear, and prepares the annual figures. Once this phase is done, the year is officially closed.

We will send regular updates on progress through the company newsletter and on Grove. If you are unsure where we are in the process, those are the best places to look.

## 3. What we need from you

Most of the year-end close is handled by Finance. However, there are a few things that need input from people across the company. Here is what we ask of you.

**Submit your expenses promptly.** If you have any business expenses from the past year that you have not yet claimed, please submit them in Ledgerly before the close begins. Expenses submitted after the close starts may have to wait until the following year. Check your receipts and submit anything outstanding as soon as you can.

**Confirm your travel bookings.** If you travelled for work near the end of the year, please make sure your bookings in Ledgerly are finalised. Cancel any trips you did not take so that we do not carry unused bookings into the new year.

**Review your leave balance.** Your leave balance is tracked in Fern. Before the year ends, please check that your recorded balance looks right to you. If something seems off, contact People & Culture promptly. Once the year closes, correcting historical leave data becomes much harder.

**Approve pending requests.** If you are a manager, please review and approve any pending expense reports, leave requests, or other financial items from your team before the close. Approvals that sit open during the close slow everyone down.

**Tell us about anything unusual.** If you know of a payment that is coming in or going out around the turn of the year, or if you have signed a contract with a client or vendor that spans the year boundary, please let Finance know. A short note is enough. It helps us record things in the right year.

**Example:** Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, realises in early December that she has a few business meals from November that she never claimed. She submits them in Ledgerly right away, before the close begins. She also checks her leave balance in Fern and confirms that it matches her own records. Nothing else is outstanding, so she needs to do nothing further for the close.

**Example:** Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, is a manager. Before the year ends, she goes through her team's pending approvals in Ledgerly and Fern and clears them all. She also notices that one of her team members has an open travel booking for a client visit that was cancelled. She asks the team member to cancel it in Ledgerly so it does not linger.

If you are not sure whether something is needed for the close, please ask. It is always better to check early than to discover an issue after the year is closed.

## 4. Questions

If you have questions about the year-end close, please reach out to Finance. For questions about leave balances or anything related to people data, please contact People & Culture at people@kranich.example. For technical issues with Ledgerly, Fern, or any other tool, please contact it-help@kranich.example.

We also host a short Q&A session on Grove before the close begins. The recording and a written summary will be available afterwards, so you can watch it at a time that suits you.

Thank you for helping us close the year cleanly. A smooth close means less rework for everyone and a faster start to the new year.
