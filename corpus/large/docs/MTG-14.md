# Meeting Notes — Security council (2025-03-16)

## 1. Attendees

- Priya (chair)
- Jonas
- Marta
- Rui
- Aylin (joined for the last item)
- Deniz (guest, representing Customer Success)

## 2. Discussion

- Priya opened with a recap of the quarterly security review. Overall posture remains solid; no critical incidents since the last council meeting.
- Jonas raised a concern about the growing number of personal devices connecting to internal tooling. The team has seen an uptick in support requests from employees who want to use their own laptops for routine work.
- Priya noted that the current policy on device management is clear, but enforcement has been uneven across teams. Some managers are unaware of the approval workflow.
- Deniz shared a customer-facing angle: several clients have started asking whether Kranich's own security practices align with the standards we recommend to them. She suggested the council consider publishing a short summary internally that Customer Success can reference.
- Rui reported that the Berlin and Lisbon offices both have visitors from time to time, and the current visitor Wi-Fi onboarding process relies on a manual step that occasionally gets skipped.
- Aylin joined to discuss the people side of security: she proposed weaving security awareness into the onboarding experience for new joiners rather than treating it as a one-off session.
- The group briefly discussed the recent phishing simulation. Results were mixed across teams; the council agreed that follow-up coaching should be targeted rather than company-wide.
- Jonas mentioned that Platform & Infrastructure is planning to rotate a set of credentials soon and wants to coordinate the timing with Customer Success so that no client-facing demo environment is affected.

## 3. Decisions

- The council approved a refresh of the internal security awareness materials, to be led by Priya with input from Aylin.
- Personal device requests will continue to follow the existing approval workflow, but managers will receive a short reminder about it via the Grove policy library.
- Rui will implement a simple check-in step for office visitors so that the Wi-Fi onboarding step is not skipped.
- The council agreed that Customer Success may reference the existing security documentation in client conversations, but should not share internal audit details. Deniz will receive a short list of approved talking points.
- Credential rotation will be scheduled for a weekend to avoid impacting client demos. Jonas will confirm the exact timing with Deniz.

## 4. Action items

- Priya to draft refreshed awareness materials and share with Aylin for review.
- Rui to update the visitor onboarding checklist and notify the Workplace team.
- Jonas to coordinate the credential rotation date with Deniz.
- Aylin to add a security awareness segment to the onboarding flow for new joiners.
- Deniz to circulate the approved client-facing talking points within Customer Success.
- Next council meeting to be scheduled by Priya for the coming weeks.
