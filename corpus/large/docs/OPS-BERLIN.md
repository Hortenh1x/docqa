# Berlin Office Guide

## 1. Welcome

Welcome to the Kranich Software Berlin office at Ritterstraße. This guide helps you find your way around, whether you are visiting for the day, joining us from another team, or based here full-time.

Our office is a place for collaboration, focused work, and connection. We are remote-first, so you will often find colleagues from other countries here for team weeks or project sessions. If you need anything during your visit, the Workplace & Operations team is happy to help. For policy questions, see the relevant documents in the Grove policy library, such as POL-004 for general workplace conduct.

## 2. Getting in

The main entrance is on Ritterstraße. You will need your employee badge to enter during business hours. If you are visiting and do not have a badge, please ring the intercom at the entrance, and the reception team will let you in.

If you have forgotten your badge or are having trouble with the door, contact it-help@kranich.example and they will assist you promptly. For security-related concerns, reach out to security@kranich.example.

When you enter, you will find the reception area straight ahead. Please sign in at the visitor book if you are not a regular employee. The cloakroom is to the left of the reception desk.

## 3. Desks and rooms

The Berlin office has open-plan seating areas on each floor. You can book a desk through the Perch tool, which also handles meeting-room and desk reservations. If you are visiting from another office, please book a desk in advance so we can ensure space is available.

Meeting rooms are named after European rivers. You can see their availability on the screens outside each room. If you need a quiet space for a call, there are focus booths on the upper floor — no booking required, but please be mindful of others waiting.

When you leave a meeting room, please tidy up and return the furniture to its original arrangement. If you notice any equipment issues, report them via Perch so our team can fix them promptly.

## 4. Kitchen

The kitchen is located on the ground floor, next to the reception area. You will find coffee, tea, and a selection of cold beverages. There are also microwaves and a dishwasher for your use.

Please label any food you store in the shared refrigerators with your name and the date. We clear unlabelled items at the end of each week to keep things fresh. The kitchen is a shared space, so please clean up after yourself and wash your dishes promptly.

If you have dietary preferences or allergies, you are welcome to bring your own food. We also keep a small supply of basic condiments and spices for everyone to use.

## 5. Parking and deliveries

The Berlin office has 12 spaces for visitors and deliveries. These spaces are located in the courtyard behind the building, accessible via the side gate on the eastern wall.

If you are driving to the office, please reserve a parking space through Perch in advance. Visitor parking is limited, so we encourage using public transport when possible. Delivery drivers should use the intercom at the side gate and follow the signage to the loading area.

For any questions about parking or deliveries, contact the Workplace & Operations team. They manage the courtyard schedule and can help you coordinate larger deliveries.

## 6. Questions

If you have any questions about the Berlin office, please reach out to the Workplace & Operations team or write to people@kranich.example for general inquiries. For technical issues with Perch, desks, or rooms, contact it-help@kranich.example.

We look forward to seeing you at Ritterstraße. If you have suggestions for improving this guide or the office experience, please share them with Rui Almeida, Head of Workplace & Operations. We update this document as things change, so check the Grove intranet for the latest version.
