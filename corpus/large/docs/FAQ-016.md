# Workation FAQ

## 1. Questions

**What is a workation?**
A workation is a period when you work remotely from a location other than your usual home office or company office, while remaining in your country of employment. It is regular work, just from somewhere else.

**Am I eligible for a workation?**
Most employees in good standing are eligible. Your role must allow remote work, and your manager must agree that your tasks can be performed from the chosen location. If you work in a client-facing role, check with your manager that coverage is in place.

**How long can a workation last?**
The permitted duration is set out in the workation policy. Please refer to POL-004 for the exact maximum length and any cooling-off period between workations.

**Do I need to ask for approval?**
Yes. Submit a request through Fern before booking anything. Your manager and, where relevant, your team lead will review it. Approval depends on team coverage and business needs.

**Which locations are allowed?**
You may work from any location within your country of employment. Working from another country is generally not covered by this policy; if you have a specific need, speak with People & Culture. Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer in Poland, works with clients across the country, so a workation near a client site is fine with manager approval.

**What about my working hours?**
Your regular working hours and schedule apply. You must be reachable during your core collaboration hours, and you are responsible for complying with local time zone differences.

**What does my manager need to know?**
Tell your manager where you will be, for how long, and how you plan to stay connected. Agree in advance on availability for meetings and on how urgent matters will reach you.

**What if something goes wrong with my internet or equipment?**
You are responsible for having a reliable connection and a suitable workspace. If you experience connectivity issues, try to resolve them promptly. For hardware problems, contact it-help@kranich.example.

**Are there any costs involved?**
The company does not reimburse travel or accommodation expenses for workations. You cover your own costs. If you need to book a coworking space occasionally, check the expense policy in Ledgerly for what is eligible.

**What about insurance and safety?**
Your regular work insurance applies while you work from your country of employment. Follow the same safety guidelines as at home. If you have questions, contact security@kranich.example.

**Can I take a workation while on leave?**
No. Workation is for working days only. If you want to combine a trip with annual leave, request your leave separately in Fern and plan the dates so they do not overlap.

**What happens if my plans change?**
Notify your manager and People & Culture as soon as possible. If you need to cancel, update your request in Fern so your records stay accurate.

**Where can I find more details?**
Read POL-004 for the full policy. If you have questions, reach out to people@kranich.example.
