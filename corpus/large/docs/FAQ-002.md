# Remote Work FAQ

## 1. Questions

**How much of my working time can I spend remotely?**
Employees may work up to 60% of their working time remotely. This applies to all teams and all countries where Kranich has employees.

**Do I need to ask for approval before working remotely?**
Yes. Please speak with your manager first and record your arrangement in Fern. Your manager will confirm that your plan fits team needs, such as customer meetings, on-site collaboration days, and support coverage.

**Can I work from a different country than my employment country?**
Only within your country of employment. If you are employed in Germany, you work remotely from Germany; if employed in Portugal, from Portugal, and so on. Working from another country for even a short period requires prior approval from People & Culture and may involve tax and social security implications. Please contact people@kranich.example before making plans.

**What does my remote work arrangement mean for my home office?**
You are responsible for setting up a safe and ergonomic workspace. Kranich provides the equipment you need to do your job. For guidance on reimbursable home-office supplies, please refer to the relevant finance policy (FIN-RATES-2026) and log any purchases through Ledgerly.

**How do I book a desk or meeting room when I come to the office?**
Use Perch to reserve a desk at either the Berlin Ritterstraße office or the Lisbon Rua do Alecrim office. Perch also shows which colleagues are in the office on a given day.

**How do I stay connected when working remotely?**
We are a remote-first company, so most collaboration already happens over video calls and chat. Join team standups, use the appropriate channels, and keep your calendar current. If you have connectivity problems, contact it-help@kranich.example.

**What if my manager and I disagree about a remote work request?**
Start by talking it through. If you cannot find a solution, reach out to your HR business partner or to people@kranich.example. We want remote work to work for you and for your team.

**Does remote work affect my career development?**
No. Promotion and development decisions are based on your impact and growth, not on where you sit. Managers are trained to include remote colleagues equally in feedback, project opportunities, and visibility.

**What about security when I work from home?**
The same security rules apply at home as in the office. Use your Kranich laptop, keep 1Password updated, and follow the guidance in POL-004. If you notice anything unusual, report it to security@kranich.example promptly.

**Example:** Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, works remotely on most days and comes to the Rua do Alecrim office twice a week for pairing sessions with her team. Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, prefers the office and books a desk through Perch each morning. Both arrangements fit within the 60% remote threshold when applicable and were agreed with their managers in advance.

**Where can I find the full policy?**
The complete rules are in POL-002. If you have questions that are not answered here, please contact people@kranich.example or your manager.
