# Overtime FAQ

## 1. Questions

**When does overtime apply?**  
Overtime applies when your manager asks you to work beyond your regular contracted hours. Occasional extra minutes to finish a task do not count as overtime. We expect overtime to be the exception, not the rule, and to be balanced with rest.

**Do I need approval before working overtime?**  
Yes. Please agree overtime with your manager in advance, either in person or in writing. If an urgent situation arises, notify your manager as soon as possible, ideally before you start the extra work. For guidance on requesting and recording overtime, see POL-004.

**How do I record overtime?**  
Record your overtime in Fern, our HRIS, in the same place you log your regular working time. Your manager will confirm the entry. If you have questions about how to enter the time, reach out to people@kranich.example or your local People & Culture contact.

**How is overtime compensated?**  
Overtime is compensated with time off within 18 weeks. You take the time off at a mutually convenient moment with your manager, considering team workload and client commitments. The aim is to take the time off before it expires, so please plan it early rather than waiting until the end of the period.

**Can I receive overtime as extra pay instead?**  
In most cases, no. Our policy is time off in lieu, which supports sustainable work habits. Exceptions are rare and only considered where local law or a specific contract requires payment. If you believe an exception applies, speak with your manager, who will involve People & Culture.

**What if I cannot take the time off within 18 weeks?**  
If you have not been able to take your overtime as time off within 18 weeks, talk to your manager promptly. Together you will find a solution, for example by planning the time off in the next scheduling cycle. Please do not let the time accumulate silently; we want you to rest.

**What about overtime during travel or client visits?**  
Time spent traveling for work, outside your regular hours, counts as overtime when your manager has approved it in advance. Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer in Poland, travels to a client site and works late. He agrees the extra hours with his manager and takes time off the following week.

**Who do I contact with further questions?**  
Start with your manager. For anything related to policy or recording, contact People & Culture at people@kranich.example. For questions about how overtime interacts with expense claims or travel bookings, see FIN-RATES-2026 or reach out to the Finance team via Ledgerly.

**Where can I find the full policy?**  
The complete overtime rules are in POL-004. Your local employment contract and applicable law always take precedence, and we will always follow the stricter rule.
