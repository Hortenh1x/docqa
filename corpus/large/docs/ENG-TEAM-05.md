# Team Charter — Go-to-Market

## 1. Mission

The Go-to-Market team turns Kranich Route Cloud's technical strengths into customer outcomes. We listen to what mid-size logistics operators need, show them how our routing and fleet tools solve real problems, and stay with them after the sale to make sure they get value from day one.

We are the voice of the market inside Kranich. What we learn from prospects and customers feeds directly into the product roadmap, so the software we build stays relevant to the people who use it every day.

## 2. Team

Go-to-Market currently has 22 people. The team is led by the VP of Go-to-Market and is organized around the regions and segments we serve. You will find us across Germany, Portugal, Spain, Poland, France and the United Kingdom, working remotely from home as well as from our offices in Berlin and Lisbon.

The team includes account executives, customer success managers, field solutions engineers and revenue operations specialists. We work closely with Customer Success, which sits alongside us, and we share a common goal: helping customers run more efficient fleets.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, works with a mid-size parcel carrier that recently adopted the Routing Core product. Her job is to make sure they adopt the workflows fully, not just at launch.

## 3. How we work

We work in short, outcome-focused cycles. Each quarter, we agree on a small number of priorities, and we review progress together every few weeks. We keep our planning lightweight so we can respond to what we hear from the market.

We are remote-first. This means we default to asynchronous communication in Grove and in our team channels, and we are deliberate about when we meet live. We hold a weekly all-team sync, and each sub-team schedules its own cadence.

We document decisions in Grove so that anyone can catch up on context, whether they are in Berlin, Lisbon or on the road with a client. We use Fern for leave requests and personal data, Ledgerly for expenses and travel, and Perch when we need a room or desk in an office.

We aim to be transparent with each other and honest with customers. If something is not working, we say so early and adjust.

## 4. Interfaces

We rely on several teams to do our work well.

- **Routing Core** and **Fleet Insights** build the products we sell. We share customer feedback with them through the product managers, and they share roadmap plans with us so we can communicate honestly with prospects.
- **Platform & Infrastructure** keeps the product reliable. When a customer raises a technical issue, we pass it to them promptly through the agreed channel.
- **Customer Success** is our partner after the initial sale. We hand over accounts with clear context, and we stay involved for complex implementations.
- **People & Culture** supports us with hiring, onboarding and people questions. Policies that affect us are published in Grove by People & Culture, Finance or Operations; see, for example, POL-004 for expense handling and FIN-RATES-2026 for travel rates.
- **Information Security** sets the rules for how we handle customer data. We follow their guidance closely, especially when we are on site with a client.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer in Poland, often works from a client's depot. He coordinates with Platform & Infrastructure when a customer environment behaves unexpectedly, and he keeps the account team informed so they can manage expectations.

If you have questions about this charter, start with your manager or the VP of Go-to-Market. For practical matters about tools and access, write to it-help@kranich.example. For anything related to expenses or travel, Ledgerly and the finance team can help. For people-related questions, reach out to people@kranich.example. We are a friendly team. Ask early, ask often, and we will figure it out together.
