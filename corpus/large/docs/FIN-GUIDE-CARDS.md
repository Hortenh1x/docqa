# Corporate Card How-To

## 1. Purpose

This guide explains how to get, use, and reconcile a Kranich corporate card. It is written for everyone at Kranich — whether you are in Berlin, Lisbon, or working remotely from another country.

A corporate card is a convenient way to pay for business expenses without using your own money. It also makes expense reporting simpler, because purchases are recorded automatically.

This guide covers the everyday basics. Spending limits and eligibility rules live in POL-017 §5, which is maintained by Finance. If you have questions after reading this guide, see §5 for who to contact.

## 2. Getting a card

Most roles at Kranich qualify for a corporate card. If you travel for client meetings, attend conferences, or regularly buy software or office supplies, a card will make your work easier.

To request a card, submit a request through Ledgerly. Your manager will receive a notification to approve the request. Once approved, Finance will order the card for you.

Cards are sent to your home address, not the office. This is important because Kranich is remote-first. If you move, update your address in Fern before requesting a card.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, requested a card when he joined. The card arrived at his home within a few days, and he used it for train tickets and client lunches from his first week.

If you need a card for a one-off event, such as a team offsite, you can request a temporary card instead. Temporary cards are issued for a short period and are disabled automatically afterwards.

## 3. Everyday use

Use your corporate card for business expenses only. Personal purchases are not allowed, even if you intend to reimburse Kranich later.

Common approved uses include:

- Travel: flights, trains, hotels, and taxis for business trips
- Client meetings: meals and coffee when you are with a client or prospective client
- Office supplies: small items you need for your home office
- Software and subscriptions: tools that are not covered by an existing Kranich contract

If you are unsure whether an expense is appropriate, ask your manager before you pay. When in doubt, leave the card in your wallet.

Keep your receipts. For online purchases, save the confirmation email or PDF. For in-person purchases, take a photo of the paper receipt with your phone. You will need these for reconciliation.

If your card is lost or stolen, report it immediately by writing to it-help@kranich.example. They will block the card and arrange a replacement. You will not be responsible for unauthorized charges made after you report the loss.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, used her card to take a client to dinner. She kept the paper receipt, photographed it the next morning, and attached the photo to the expense in Ledgerly.

## 4. Reconciliation

Reconciliation is how you confirm that each charge on your card was a legitimate business expense. Kranich uses Ledgerly for this.

After a purchase, the charge appears in Ledgerly automatically. You need to attach a receipt and assign a category — such as travel, client entertainment, or software — within a few days of the purchase.

If you do not reconcile a charge promptly, you will receive a reminder. Repeatedly missing reconciliation deadlines will be handled according to POL-017, which also describes the approval workflow for unusual expenses.

For purchases made in a foreign currency, Ledgerly will show the charge in the original currency and the equivalent in euros. You do not need to calculate anything yourself.

When you travel for work, reconcile as you go. A short check each evening keeps the workload small and prevents a pile-up when you return.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, attended a conference in Berlin. Each evening, she spent a few minutes attaching receipts for the day's meals and transport. When she flew home, her expenses were already fully reconciled.

## 5. Questions

If you have questions about this guide, start by checking the Finance section on Grove. You will find answers to common questions there.

For questions about a specific charge or a missing receipt, write to Finance through Ledgerly. For technical problems with the card itself — such as a declined payment or a lost card — contact it-help@kranich.example.

If your question is about spending limits or eligibility, refer to POL-017 §5. That document is maintained by Finance and is the authoritative source for those rules.

For anything else, you are welcome to reach out to your manager or to the Finance team directly. We are happy to help you use your card confidently and correctly.
