# Home Office Allowance Policy

## 1. Purpose

Kranich Software GmbH is a remote-first company. Many of us work from home for most or all of the week, whether we live in Berlin, Lisbon, or elsewhere in the countries where we employ people. Working from home should not mean working with a personal financial disadvantage. This policy explains the allowances we provide so that your home workspace is comfortable and your internet connection is reliable.

The policy covers two types of support: a one-off setup allowance to help you furnish and equip your home office, and a monthly connectivity allowance toward your internet service. Both allowances are administered by Finance. If you have questions about eligibility or how to claim, please reach out to Finance or see §5 of this policy.

This policy applies to all employees, regardless of their contractual working time. It does not apply to interns or to contractors engaged through external agencies. If you are unsure whether you qualify, please ask Finance before you make any purchases.

## 2. One-off setup allowance

When you join Kranich, or when you first begin working from home on a regular basis, you may claim a one-off home office setup allowance. The one-off home office setup allowance is €450. This allowance is intended to help with the practical costs of making a dedicated workspace at home.

The allowance may be used for items such as a desk, an office chair, a monitor, a keyboard, a mouse, a desk lamp, or cable management. You are free to choose the items that suit your needs and your space. We trust your judgment about what makes your workspace productive and comfortable.

You may claim the setup allowance once per employment. If you move from one country of employment to another within Kranich, you are not eligible for a second setup allowance. The allowance is not tied to a specific address; it is tied to you as an employee.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, recently joined Kranich and moved into a new apartment. She used the setup allowance to buy a height-adjustable desk and an ergonomic chair. She did not need a new monitor, so she kept the receipt for what she bought and claimed the amount she actually spent, up to the allowance.

If your setup costs less than the allowance, you may claim only what you spent. If your setup costs more, the difference is your own responsibility. We do not reimburse for decorative items, plants, or furniture that is not primarily for work use.

The setup allowance is paid as a reimbursement against receipts. Please keep your receipts and submit them together with the claim form described in §4 of this policy. We will process the reimbursement promptly after approval.

## 3. Monthly connectivity allowance

Reliable internet access is essential for your work. To support this, the connectivity allowance is €25 per month. This allowance is paid to all employees who work from home on a regular basis, regardless of how many days per week they work remotely.

The allowance is intended to contribute toward your home internet service. You do not need to submit receipts for the monthly allowance. It is paid as a fixed amount alongside your regular salary, and it appears as a separate line item on your payslip.

You are eligible for the monthly allowance from your first full month of employment. If you join Kranich partway through a month, the allowance for that first month is not pro-rated. You will receive the full monthly amount starting the following month.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer who works with clients in Poland, spends most of his week at home between client visits. He relies on a fast fiber connection for video calls and for uploading route data. He receives the monthly connectivity allowance automatically and does not need to track his internet bill.

The monthly allowance is not intended to cover mobile data or a second connection for personal use. If you share an internet connection with others in your household, you are still eligible. If you do not have home internet because you work exclusively from the office or from client sites, please speak with your manager and with Finance; the allowance may not apply in your situation.

The allowance continues while you are on ordinary leave. It is not paid during periods of unpaid leave that last more than a month. If your circumstances change and you no longer work from home, please inform Finance so that the allowance can be adjusted.

## 4. Claiming

There are two separate processes for claiming, depending on which allowance you are claiming.

For the one-off setup allowance, you must submit your claim within a reasonable time after making your purchase. Please use the expense form in Ledgerly and attach your receipts. In the description field, write "Home office setup allowance" so that Finance can identify the claim correctly. Your manager will approve the claim, and Finance will process the reimbursement.

For the monthly connectivity allowance, there is no claim process. The allowance is added to your payroll automatically. If you do not see it on your payslip after your first full month, please contact Finance or write to it-help@kranich.example, and we will sort it out.

Please be mindful of the following points when claiming:

- Keep original receipts for any setup purchases. Digital receipts are acceptable.
- Submit setup claims within a few weeks of the purchase date. Claims submitted much later may still be accepted, but please add a note explaining the delay.
- Do not claim the setup allowance for items that are already reimbursed through another policy. If you are unsure whether an item is covered, ask Finance before purchasing.
- If you leave Kranich, you are not required to return any items purchased with the setup allowance, and no repayment of the monthly allowance is due.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, bought a second monitor and a footrest for her home office. She submitted her receipts in Ledgerly on the same day and wrote the correct description. Her manager approved the claim the next week, and Finance reimbursed her promptly.

Finance reviews claims on a regular basis. If a claim is incomplete or if a receipt is missing, Finance will return the claim to you with a note explaining what is needed. Please respond promptly so that we can close the claim.

## 5. Questions

If you have questions about this policy, please start with your manager or with the People & Culture team. For questions about amounts, payment timing, or tax treatment in your country of employment, please contact Finance directly. For technical questions about setting up your home office equipment, such as connecting a second monitor or configuring your router, the Platform & Infrastructure team and it-help@kranich.example are happy to assist.

We also encourage you to review related policies on the Grove intranet. The expenses policy (POL-004) explains general reimbursement rules, and the travel and business expenses guidelines (FIN-RATES-2026) contain information about daily rates and related topics. If any conflict arises between this policy and those documents, this policy takes precedence for home office setup and connectivity claims.

We know that home offices look different for everyone. Some people have a dedicated room, while others work from the kitchen table. This policy is designed to be flexible and to support you in the way that works best for your role and your living situation. If you have a particular circumstance that is not covered here, please talk to us. We would rather find a sensible solution together than have you hesitate to ask.

Finally, a note on honesty. The allowances in this policy are provided because we trust you and because we believe a good home office benefits everyone. Please claim only what you are entitled to, and keep accurate records. If you are ever in doubt, ask. The Finance team and the People & Culture team are here to help, and a quick question is always welcome.
