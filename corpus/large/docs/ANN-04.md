# Announcement: Perch desk booking launch

## 1. Announcement

We are happy to announce that Perch, our meeting-room and desk booking tool, is now available to everyone at Kranich Software GmbH. Starting today, you can use Perch to reserve a desk at our Ritterstraße office in Berlin or the Rua do Alecrim office in Lisbon on the days you plan to come in.

Perch replaces the informal, first-come-first-served way of choosing a workspace. It gives you a clear view of where your teammates are sitting, helps you find a spot near the people you are collaborating with, and makes it easier for Workplace & Operations to keep the offices comfortable and well prepared.

Using Perch is straightforward. Log in with your Kranich account, pick the office and the day, and choose an available desk on the floor map. You can book a desk for a single day or for several upcoming days at once. If your plans change, please cancel your reservation promptly so that a desk does not go unused.

A few practical notes:

- Desk bookings are intended for the days you are actually in the office. If you work remotely, there is no need to book.
- If you arrive and find an issue with your desk or the surrounding area, please report it through the Perch help flow so that Rui’s team can take care of it.
- For longer stays, such as a full working week in Berlin or Lisbon, you may book the same desk for consecutive days.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, plans to spend a few days at the Berlin office to work with Ines’s team. She opens Perch, sees which desks are free near her colleagues, and books one for each day of her visit.

Perch is managed by Platform & Infrastructure. If you have questions about using the tool, please reach out to it-help@kranich.example. For feedback about the office experience itself, contact Rui Almeida or the Workplace team.

We hope Perch makes your time in the office easier and more enjoyable.
