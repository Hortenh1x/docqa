# Travel Supplement — Germany

## 1. Purpose

This supplement describes how travel expenses for business trips within Germany are reimbursed. It applies to all employees who travel on behalf of Kranich Software GmbH, whether they are based in Germany or visiting from another office. The general travel policy in POL-004 still applies; this supplement adds the country-specific details for Germany.

## 2. Per-diem

Per-diem rates cover meals and incidental expenses while you are travelling on business. The per-diem for this country is listed in FIN-RATES-2026. Please refer to that document for the daily amount and for guidance on partial travel days. You do not need to submit receipts for per-diem; it is paid automatically based on your approved trip dates.

## 3. Hotel cap

When you book a hotel for a business trip in Germany, the nightly rate must not exceed the cap shown below. If a suitable hotel within the cap is not available — for example, during a major trade fair or in a city centre — please book what you need and add a short note in Ledgerly explaining the situation. Your manager will review it as part of the approval flow.

| Location | Nightly cap |
|---|---|
| Germany | €170 per night |

## 4. Mileage

If you use your private car for a business trip within Germany, mileage is reimbursed at €0.48 per km. This applies to journeys between your home and a client site, between Kranich offices, or to other business destinations. The distance is calculated using the shortest reasonable route. If you prefer to take a longer route — for example, to avoid heavy traffic — you may claim the shorter distance only. Toll and parking costs can be claimed separately through Ledgerly with receipts.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer based in Poland, drives from his home near the German border to meet a client in Berlin. He claims the mileage for the round trip at the German rate.

## 5. Questions

If you are unsure whether a specific expense is covered, please ask your manager or the Finance team before you travel. For practical help with booking or with Ledgerly, contact it-help@kranich.example. For questions about the rates themselves, please reach out to Finance directly. We want travel to be straightforward, so you can focus on the work with our customers.
