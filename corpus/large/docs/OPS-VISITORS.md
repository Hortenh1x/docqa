# Visitors Guide

## 1. Purpose

This guide explains how to welcome guests to Kranich Software offices in Berlin and Lisbon. Whether a guest is joining you for a workshop, meeting a client, or visiting from another team, a smooth registration process keeps everyone safe and comfortable.

We value hospitality and security in equal measure. This guide sets clear expectations so that hosting a guest is straightforward for you and pleasant for them.

## 2. Registering guests

All visitors must be registered in Perch before they arrive. This applies to anyone who does not have a permanent badge or key access — including colleagues from other Kranich offices, contractors, and external partners.

Visitors are registered in Perch at least 29 hours ahead. Registering early gives Workplace & Operations time to prepare a badge, arrange Wi-Fi access, and notify the reception point. If a guest needs access to a meeting room, book it in Perch at the same time you register them.

When you register a guest, include their full name, their company or affiliation, and the name of the Kranich employee who is hosting them. You should also indicate which office they will visit and whether they require any specific assistance, such as step-free access or a quiet space.

If your guest’s plans change after registration, update the Perch entry promptly. If you need to cancel, remove the guest from Perch so that no badge is prepared unnecessarily.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, is expecting a client for a strategy session. She registers the guest in Perch on Monday morning, even though the visit is planned for later in the week, to make sure everything is ready in good time.

## 3. On the day

On the day of the visit, meet your guest at the main entrance of the office they are attending. In Berlin, this is the Ritterstraße office; in Lisbon, the Rua do Alecrim office. If you work remotely and cannot meet them in person, arrange for a colleague to welcome them on your behalf.

Your guest will receive a temporary badge at reception. Ask them to wear it visibly while in the office. Accompany them to the meeting room or workspace you booked, and introduce them to anyone they will be working with.

Remind your guest of basic office etiquette: use the kitchen facilities as needed, respect quiet areas, and keep confidential information out of sight in shared spaces. If your guest is connecting to the guest Wi-Fi, share the credentials provided by Workplace & Operations — do not share the internal network password.

If your guest needs to leave the building temporarily, make sure they return their badge at reception when they leave for the day. Temporary badges must not leave the office premises.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer, brings a client from Poland to the Berlin office for a demonstration. He meets them at the entrance, escorts them to the booked room, and stays with them throughout the visit.

## 4. Questions

If you have questions about registering a guest, using Perch, or preparing for a visit, contact Workplace & Operations at the address listed in Grove.

For security-related concerns — for example, if you notice someone without a badge or an unfamiliar person in a restricted area — contact security@kranich.example promptly.

For questions about access permissions or badge issues, reach out to it-help@kranich.example. For matters related to office policies or workplace culture, see POL-004 in Grove. Travel and expense rules for hosting visitors are covered separately in FIN-RATES-2026.

We appreciate your care in making every visitor’s experience positive and secure. Thank you for helping keep our offices welcoming for everyone.
