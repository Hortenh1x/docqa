# Lost or Stolen Device Runbook

## 1. Purpose

This runbook explains what to do when a Kranich-issued laptop or mobile device is lost or stolen. Acting quickly protects company data and helps us get you back to work with minimal disruption. You do not need to wait for approval from your manager or team lead before following the steps below.

This runbook applies to all Kranich employees, regardless of where you work. Whether you are in the Ritterstraße office, the Rua do Alecrim office, or working remotely from home, the same process applies.

## 2. Report

As soon as you realise a device is missing, report it through the appropriate channel. If you believe the device was stolen, also report the incident to local law enforcement in your country of employment. A police report is not required to start our internal process, but it helps with insurance and any follow-up investigation.

You must report a lost or stolen device within 4 hours of realising it is missing. Do not spend time searching first; report promptly and continue searching while we work with you.

To report, send an email to security@kranich.example and copy it-help@kranich.example. In your message, include:

- Your name and team
- The device type and model
- Where you last had it
- Whether you believe it was lost or stolen

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, realises her laptop is missing after a client meeting at a café. She sends the report email from her phone within the hour, describing the café and the time she last used the device.

## 3. Remote wipe

After you submit your report, the Information Security team will begin a remote wipe of the device. This erases company data and revokes access to Kranich systems. The wipe starts as soon as we confirm the device is missing; you do not need to wait.

If you have a company phone, it will be wiped too. Your personal data on that phone cannot be recovered after the wipe, so please note that anything stored locally will be lost. If you used the device for multi-factor authentication, the wipe will also remove those credentials. You will need to re-enrol a new device using 1Password and any other authentication tools you normally use.

While the wipe is in progress, do not attempt to log in to any Kranich service from the missing device, even if you think you have found it. If you find the device before the wipe completes, contact security@kranich.example immediately to stop the process if possible.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, has his work tablet stolen from a rental car. He reports it within a few hours, and Security initiates the wipe while he files a police report.

## 4. Replacement

After the remote wipe is underway, your manager or the People team will arrange a replacement device. The process differs slightly depending on your location:

- If you are near the Berlin office, you can collect a replacement from the Ritterstraße office.
- If you are near the Lisbon office, you can collect one from the Rua do Alecrim office.
- If you work remotely, IT will ship a replacement to your home address.

While you wait for the replacement, you can use a personal device to access essential tools through the browser, provided you follow the security guidance in POL-004. Do not download company files to your personal device; use only the web versions of our tools.

When your replacement arrives, set it up following the standard onboarding steps. You will need to:

- Install 1Password and log in with your credentials
- Request access to any shared vaults you previously used
- Re-enrol your multi-factor authentication
- Verify that your access to Grove, Fern, and Ledgerly works correctly

If you need a temporary device for a client meeting or an urgent deadline, speak to your manager. They can request a loaner through it-help@kranich.example.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, loses her work laptop on a weekend trip. She reports it on Monday morning, and by the afternoon IT has shipped a replacement to her home in Lisbon. She sets it up the same day and is back to work by the next morning.

## 5. Questions

If you have questions about this runbook or about a specific incident, contact the Information Security team at security@kranich.example. For questions about your Kranich device, hardware, or software access, contact it-help@kranich.example.

For questions about travel or booking a desk or meeting room at either office while you wait for a replacement, contact people@kranich.example or use Perch to book a workspace.

If your device was stolen while you were travelling for work, your manager should also notify the Workplace & Operations team led by Rui Almeida, so they can review any travel-related coverage under our internal policies. Refer to FIN-RATES-2026 for questions about expense claims related to the incident, such as replacing a lock or a bag.

Remember: the most important thing is to report promptly. Do not worry about blame or paperwork. Losing a device happens, and our process is designed to get you secure and productive again with as little friction as possible.
