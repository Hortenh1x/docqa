# Lisbon Office Guide

## 1. Welcome

Welcome to the Kranich Route Cloud Lisbon office, our home in the Rua do Alecrim. Whether you are based here full-time, visiting for a few days, or simply passing through, we want you to feel at ease and productive.

This guide covers the essentials: where to sit, how to get in, and where to find coffee. If something is missing, please reach out to Workplace & Operations or write to it-help@kranich.example. Our colleagues in Berlin follow a similar set of arrangements under the Berlin office guide, and the policies referenced throughout the internal corpus apply here as well.

## 2. Desks

The Lisbon office seats 35 people. We use the Perch tool to book desks and meeting rooms, just as we do in Berlin. When you arrive, check Perch for your assigned spot or an available desk for the day.

Most desks are arranged in open clusters, with a few quiet corners for focused work. You will find monitors, keyboard trays, and adjustable chairs at every workstation. If you need a standing desk or ergonomic accessories, submit a request through Perch and our Workplace team will set it up promptly.

Please keep your area tidy at the end of the day. Laptops and personal belongings should not be left overnight; lockers are available near the entrance. If you are visiting from another office, Perch will show you which desks are free on your chosen days.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, visits Lisbon for a workshop. He books a desk in Perch the week before and finds everything ready when he arrives.

## 3. Getting in

The office is accessible during regular business hours. Outside those hours, you will need your Kranich badge or the office code shared by Workplace & Operations. If you forget your badge, use the intercom at the main door and the reception team will let you in.

Visitors must be registered in advance. If you are hosting someone, add them through Perch at least a few days ahead. They will receive a temporary badge at the front desk. Please accompany your guest while they are in the office.

For security matters, including lost badges or unfamiliar people in the building, contact security@kranich.example. General access questions go to it-help@kranich.example. Note that access-related practices are described in POL-004, which you should review if you handle sensitive data or host external parties regularly.

## 4. Kitchen

The kitchen is on the same floor as the main workspace, near the window side. You will find tea, coffee, and a filtered water station. Milk and plant-based alternatives are restocked each week.

Please label any food you store in the shared fridges with your name and the date. Unlabelled items are cleared at the end of each week to keep things fresh. Dishwasher cycles run daily; please place dirty dishes in the rack, not in the sink.

The kitchen has a microwave, a toaster, and a coffee machine. If the machine runs out of beans or shows an error, check the note taped to the side — most issues are simple to resolve. For anything else, write to it-help@kranich.example.

Example: Sofia, a product manager working remotely from Spain, spends a week in Lisbon. She appreciates the quiet corner near the kitchen for her morning calls and the clearly labelled fridge shelf for her lunch.

## 5. Questions

If you cannot find what you need in this guide, start with Perch or the Grove intranet. The policy library holds related documents, including finance topics in FIN-RATES-2026, which covers expense and travel rules that apply when you visit Lisbon from another office.

For desk, room, or access issues, contact Workplace & Operations or it-help@kranich.example. For anything related to how we work together, your manager or the People & Culture team is happy to help — reach people@kranich.example.

We hope you enjoy working from the Rua do Alecrim office. Welcome in, make yourself at home, and do not hesitate to ask.
