# Equipment & Hardware Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how Kranich Software provides equipment to its employees, how equipment is refreshed, and what happens when you leave the company or when hardware is lost or damaged. We want everyone to have the tools they need to do their best work, whether they sit in the Ritterstraße office, the Rua do Alecrim office, or work remotely from home.

This version supersedes version 1.0. The main changes clarify the refresh cycle, the returning process, and how losses are handled. If you have questions after reading this policy, please reach out to Workplace & Operations or your manager.

## 2. Standard setup

When you join Kranich, Workplace & Operations arranges your standard setup. The setup includes a laptop, a monitor, a keyboard, a mouse, a headset, and a docking station where needed. We also provide a mobile phone for roles where client contact or on-call duties make one necessary.

Your manager chooses the role category for your position before we order equipment. The two categories are Standard role and Engineering role. Engineering roles receive a more powerful laptop because of the development and testing workloads involved. If your role changes, your manager can request a different category, and we will adjust your equipment at the next refresh cycle.

All equipment remains the property of Kranich Software. You are responsible for the care of the devices assigned to you. Please keep them in a safe place, install updates promptly, and report any technical issue to it-help@kranich.example.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, received a Standard setup when she joined. Her laptop, monitor, and headset allow her to run client calls and demos without problems. When she moved to a role that required more processing power, her manager requested an upgrade, and Deniz received new hardware at the next refresh.

## 3. Equipment budget

Each role category has a budget for the initial equipment order. The budget covers the laptop, accessories, and any additional peripheral that your manager approves. If you need something beyond the standard setup, please discuss it with your manager first. We cannot order items that exceed the budget without a written approval from the Head of Workplace & Operations.

The budget values are as follows:

| Role category | Equipment budget |
|---------------|------------------|
| Standard role | €2,200           |
| Engineering role | €2,800        |

The budget is a guideline for the total value of your initial equipment. It is not a personal allowance. You cannot choose a cheaper laptop and use the difference for other purchases. If you have a specific need, for example an ergonomic chair or a second large monitor, your manager can request an exception. Exceptions are reviewed case by case.

## 4. Refresh cycle

Equipment is refreshed every 36 months. We count from the date the equipment was ordered, not from the date you joined. For example, if you joined in the middle of a refresh cycle, your refresh date may be earlier or later than your work anniversary.

Workplace & Operations tracks refresh dates and reaches out before your refresh is due. You will receive a return label for the old device and a date for the delivery of the new one. Please plan for a short transition period during which you may need to use both devices.

If your equipment breaks outside the refresh cycle and cannot be repaired economically, we will replace it earlier. The replacement does not reset your refresh cycle; you will still receive new equipment at the original refresh date.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, received his laptop in March 2024. His refresh is due in March 2027. If his laptop were damaged beyond repair in 2025, he would receive a replacement immediately, but his next refresh would still happen in March 2027.

## 5. Returning equipment

When you leave Kranich, you must return all company equipment. Equipment must be returned within 10 business days after the last working day. Please use the return label that Workplace & Operations sends you and pack the equipment carefully in the original boxes if you still have them.

Before you send the equipment, remove all personal data and log out of all accounts. Your manager will coordinate with you to ensure that any files you need for handover are stored in the company systems. Do not keep company files on personal devices.

If you are unable to return the equipment within the timeframe, please contact Workplace & Operations as soon as possible. We understand that travel or personal circumstances can sometimes delay a return. In that case, we will agree on an alternative date with you.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, decided to leave Kranich. Her last working day was a Friday. She packed her laptop, monitor, and accessories over the weekend and sent them back on the following Tuesday, well within the required timeframe.

## 6. Repairs and loss

Access: Finance only

If your equipment is damaged or stops working, please contact it-help@kranich.example. The IT team will diagnose the issue and arrange a repair or a replacement. Do not attempt to repair hardware yourself, as this may void the warranty.

If your equipment is lost or stolen, report it to the police and to security@kranich.example immediately. Also inform your manager. We will work with you to replace the device as quickly as possible. Please be aware that you may be asked to cover part of the cost if the loss is due to gross negligence.

Hardware with a net book value below €400 is written off without a separate approval. This means that for most standard accessories, such as a keyboard or a headset, we will simply replace the item without further process. For more valuable items, such as a laptop, Finance will review the circumstances and decide whether a charge to the employee is appropriate.

Example: Ines Duarte, an engineering manager in Berlin, lost her company phone while traveling. She reported it to the police and to security@kranich.example on the same day. Because the phone had a net book value below the threshold, it was written off and she received a replacement within a few days.

## 7. Questions

If you have questions about this policy, please contact Workplace & Operations at people@kranich.example or reach out to your manager. For technical issues with your equipment, contact it-help@kranich.example. For questions about the financial treatment of lost or damaged hardware, contact Finance.

Please refer to those documents for questions about costs that are not covered here.
