# Training & Development Policy

## 1. Purpose

At Kranich Software, we believe that growing our skills is part of doing great work. This policy explains how we support your professional development through learning opportunities, conferences, and internal knowledge sharing. It applies to all employees across our offices in Berlin and Lisbon and to everyone working remotely from our other countries of employment.

We want learning to feel accessible and practical. Whether you are deepening your expertise in routing algorithms, exploring a new programming language, or building skills in customer communication, this policy gives you a clear framework and the resources to make it happen. Your manager is your first conversation partner when thinking about what to learn next.

## 2. Learning budget

Every employee has a learning budget of €500 per year. You can use this budget for books, online courses, certifications, subscriptions to learning platforms, and other materials that support your professional growth. The budget resets at the start of each calendar year, matching our fiscal year.

You do not need to ask for permission before spending from your budget, but we ask you to be thoughtful. Choose resources that connect to your current role or to a direction you want to grow into. If you are unsure whether something qualifies, reach out to your manager or to People & Culture.

To use the budget, purchase the learning resource and submit the receipt through Ledgerly as a business expense. Select the appropriate category so the finance team can match it to this policy. If the resource is a subscription that renews automatically, please be mindful that the annual total stays within your budget.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, wants to take an online course on distributed systems. She checks that the course price fits within her budget, purchases it, and submits the receipt through Ledgerly. Her manager is happy to support this because it connects directly to her work on the Routing Core team.

If you have an idea for a learning opportunity that costs more than your annual budget, you can discuss it with your manager. In some cases, the company may contribute additional funds where there is a clear business need. These decisions are made case by case and are not guaranteed.

## 3. Conferences

Conferences are a valuable way to learn from peers, discover new tools, and represent Kranich in the wider community. Up to 5 working days per year may be spent at conferences without using vacation. This time covers your attendance at the conference itself, including travel days when they fall on working days.

Conference attendance is not automatic. We ask you to discuss the event with your manager first, ideally before you register. Your manager will consider the relevance to your role, the team's current workload, and how the learning will be shared with colleagues when you return.

The learning budget in §2 of this policy covers conference tickets and registration fees. Travel and accommodation costs are handled separately through the standard travel booking process in Ledgerly. Please book your travel through Ledgerly so that our Workplace team can support you and so that expenses are tracked consistently.

If you attend a conference on behalf of Kranich, we encourage you to share highlights afterwards. This could be a short summary on Grove, a brown-bag session with your team, or a note in your team channel. Sharing what you learned multiplies the value of your attendance for everyone.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, would like to attend a logistics industry conference in the autumn. She speaks with her manager about the agenda and agrees on which sessions are most relevant. She books her travel through Ledgerly and uses her learning budget for the ticket. After the conference, she posts a short summary on Grove for the Customer Success team.

If you speak at a conference, please let your manager know. Speaking engagements are a great way to build your professional profile and to raise awareness of Kranich. In some cases, your manager may ask you to coordinate with the Go-to-Market team so that the message aligns with our company positioning.

## 4. Internal learning

Learning at Kranich is not only about external resources. We have a strong culture of sharing knowledge across teams, and we encourage everyone to participate.

From time to time, teams organize internal sessions where colleagues present on topics they are exploring. These sessions are informal and open to anyone who is interested. You might hear about a new approach the Fleet Insights team is testing, or learn how the Platform & Infrastructure team handles a particular challenge. You do not need to be an expert to attend, and you do not need to be an expert to present.

We also use Grove as a place to collect and share learning resources. If you find an article, a talk, or a tool that helped you, consider posting it there. A short note on why it was useful is enough.

When you return from a conference or complete a significant course, think about what your colleagues could learn from your experience. This does not need to be formal. A conversation over lunch, a few slides in a team meeting, or a written summary are all valuable.

Mentoring is another form of internal learning. If you would like guidance from a more experienced colleague, or if you would like to offer guidance to someone else, speak with your manager or with People & Culture. We can help you find a good match based on your goals and interests.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, recently learned a new technique for presenting route optimization results to stakeholders. He offers to share this approach in a short internal session. Sofia, a product manager in Spain, attends and finds the technique useful for her work with Fleet Insights customers. A few weeks later, Sofia adapts the approach for her own team's demos.

## 5. Questions

If you have questions about this policy, please start with your manager. They can help you think through what makes sense for your role and your development goals.

For questions about the budget, expenses, or how to submit something through Ledgerly, please contact the Finance team or People & Culture. For anything related to travel arrangements for conferences, the Workplace team is happy to help.

You can also reach People & Culture directly at people@kranich.example. We aim to respond promptly and to help you find the right answer. If you are unsure whether an activity falls under this policy, it is always better to ask before you commit to a purchase or a registration.

We want this policy to support your growth in a way that feels fair and transparent. If you have suggestions for how we could improve our training and development offer, please share them. Your feedback helps us shape a better learning environment for everyone at Kranich.
