# Announcement: Security training reminder

## 1. Announcement

Hello everyone,

A quick and friendly reminder about our annual security awareness training. As part of our commitment to keeping Kranich Route Cloud — and each other — safe, everyone is expected to complete the training module assigned through the Grove policy library.

You can find the full requirements in POL-004. Please take a moment to review it if you have not done so recently. The training itself is short and designed to fit into your day without disruption.

**Why this matters**

We handle sensitive data for our logistics customers every day. A single overlooked detail — a suspicious attachment, a reused password, an unlocked screen — can have consequences far beyond one person's laptop. Security is a shared responsibility across all teams, from Routing Core to Go-to-Market.

**What to do now**

- Log in to Grove and open POL-004.
- Complete the assigned module at your earliest convenience.
- If you have questions about the content, reach out to security@kranich.example.
- If you experience technical issues accessing the module, contact it-help@kranich.example.

**Example:** Lea, a people operations specialist in Berlin, set aside a quiet moment between meetings to finish the module. She found the scenarios realistic and appreciated that the examples reflected everyday situations, like reviewing a request from a vendor she did not recognize.

**A note on phishing**

We have seen an uptick in phishing attempts across the industry recently. Always pause before clicking links in unexpected messages, even if they appear to come from a colleague. When in doubt, verify through another channel — a quick chat message or a call works well.

Thank you for taking this seriously. A few minutes of your time now helps protect our customers, our company, and each other.

If you have feedback on the training content itself, we would love to hear it — simply write to people@kranich.example.

Best regards,
