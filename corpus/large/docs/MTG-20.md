# Meeting Notes — Customer Success review (2025-09-07)

## 1. Attendees

- Deniz (Customer Success, Berlin)
- Anna (Routing Core, Lisbon — joined for the technical portion)
- Marek (Field Solutions, Poland)
- Sofia (Product, Fleet Insights, remote from Spain)

## 2. Discussion

- Deniz opened with a summary of the past few weeks across the main accounts. Overall sentiment remains positive, though a handful of customers have raised recurring questions about the new reporting module.
- Marek shared feedback from clients in Poland. Several operators find the route-export workflow confusing when handling multi-day trips. He suggested we walk through the interface together with Anna to confirm whether this is a usability issue or a missing feature.
- Anna explained that the Routing Core team has already seen similar reports from other markets. She noted that a fix is being discussed internally, and that a clearer labeling approach might resolve most of the confusion without a larger build.
- Sofia highlighted an upcoming Fleet Insights release that touches the same area. She proposed coordinating the Customer Success materials with the product launch so that support requests stay manageable.
- The group talked about how to share client feedback more systematically. Deniz mentioned that notes currently live in various places, which makes it hard to spot patterns. Everyone agreed that a lightweight, shared summary after each quarterly review would help.
- Marek raised one account that is considering expanding its usage next year. He asked whether Customer Success should prepare a tailored walkthrough. The group agreed this makes sense, provided the product changes are confirmed first.
- No urgent escalations were discussed. Follow-ups will happen through the usual channels.

## 3. Decisions

- The reporting module questions will be treated as a usability concern for now, pending Anna’s review.
- Sofia will align the Fleet Insights release notes with Customer Success talking points before launch.
- Deniz will set up a shared space for quarterly account summaries, open to Customer Success and Field Solutions.
- Marek will pause the tailored walkthrough until the product direction is confirmed.

## 4. Action items

- Anna: review the route-export flow and report back to the group with a recommendation.
- Sofia: share the draft release notes with Deniz and Marek for feedback.
- Deniz: create the shared space for account summaries and invite the relevant colleagues.
- Marek: check in with the expanding account and keep the group posted on timing.
- All: reconvene in a few weeks to review progress.
