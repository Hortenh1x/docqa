# All-Hands Notes — July 2025

## 1. Highlights

Marta opened the all-hands by thanking everyone for a strong first half of the year. She noted that the Routing Core team shipped its major platform upgrade on schedule, and that Fleet Insights has seen encouraging adoption among our existing customers. Jonas added that the Platform & Infrastructure team completed the migration of our monitoring stack, which has improved reliability across the board.

Tomasz briefly reviewed the company's financial health, confirming that revenue growth remains in line with the plan set out at the beginning of the year. He reminded everyone that the Finance team is available to answer questions through Ledgerly or directly.

Aylin highlighted the results of the recent engagement survey, sharing that participation was strong and that the feedback will inform the People roadmap for the coming months. She thanked everyone for their honesty and openness.

## 2. People

Aylin announced that the People team has finalized the new career framework for engineering roles. The framework will be rolled out in stages, starting with Routing Core and Fleet Insights, and will then extend to other teams. Managers will hold individual conversations with their team members over the next few weeks to walk through the new levels and expectations.

Lea reminded everyone that the summer holiday season is approaching. She asked that leave requests be submitted through Fern promptly so that teams can plan coverage. She also noted that the annual performance review cycle will open later this year, and that managers will receive guidance well in advance.

Aylin also shared that the company will host its annual offsite in the autumn. More details about the location and agenda will follow through Grove. She encouraged people to share any accessibility or scheduling needs with their managers or the People team.

Rui gave a quick update on the Berlin office. The Ritterstraße office will undergo light refurbishment in the coming weeks, and a temporary quiet floor will be available for deep work. He reminded everyone to book desks and meeting rooms through Perch, especially on days when many colleagues are in the office.

## 3. Customers

Deniz summarized recent customer feedback from the Customer Success team. She highlighted that customer NPS reached 45 points in the last survey, which reflects the hard work of many teams across the company. She thanked the Routing Core and Fleet Insights teams in particular for their responsiveness to customer-reported issues.

Marek shared a story from a recent client visit in Poland. A mid-size logistics operator had been struggling with manual route planning during peak season. With the help of our field solutions engineers, the client configured Kranich Route Cloud to match their specific constraints. The client reported that planning now takes considerably less time, and they have already expanded their use of the platform.

Sofia, joining remotely from Spain, gave an update on the Fleet Insights roadmap. The team is working closely with a group of design partners to refine the new driver-behavior dashboard. She noted that early feedback has been positive, and that the team is prioritizing clarity and ease of use over adding more features.

## 4. Questions from the floor

**Question:** Will the new career framework affect current salaries?

Aylin answered that the framework is primarily about growth and expectations, not immediate compensation changes. Any compensation adjustments will follow the normal review cycle, and managers will discuss this transparently with their teams.

**Question:** Can we expect more flexibility in remote work arrangements?

Marta confirmed that the remote-first policy remains unchanged. She acknowledged that some colleagues have expressed interest in working from additional countries for short periods, and said the Leadership Team is exploring what is feasible from a legal and tax perspective. She asked for patience while this is evaluated.

**Question:** How is the company preparing for the upcoming industry conference?

Deniz responded that the Go-to-Market team is leading the preparation. She invited anyone interested in attending or supporting the booth to reach out to their manager or the Customer Success team. Logistics and travel bookings will be handled through Ledgerly.

**Question:** What is the status of the security awareness training mentioned earlier this year?

Priya Nayar, Head of Information Security, answered that the training will be launched in the coming months. She reminded everyone to keep using 1Password for all credential management and to report any suspicious emails to security@kranich.example. Additional guidance is available in the policy library on Grove.

Marta closed the session by thanking everyone for their questions and contributions. She reminded the company that the next all-hands will take place in the autumn, and that recordings and notes from today's session will be posted on Grove shortly.
