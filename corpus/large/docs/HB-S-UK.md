# Travel Supplement — United Kingdom

## 1. Purpose

This supplement describes how travel expenses for business trips to the United Kingdom are reimbursed. It applies to all employees travelling to the United Kingdom on behalf of Kranich Software GmbH, regardless of their home office. The rules here complement the general travel policy; where they differ, this supplement takes precedence for trips to the United Kingdom.

We want travel to be straightforward: you should focus on your work and your customers, not on paperwork. If a situation is not covered here, reach out to Finance before you travel.

## 2. Per-diem

The per-diem for the United Kingdom is defined centrally and published for the current year. Please refer to FIN-RATES-2026 for the applicable daily rate. The rate covers meals and incidental expenses for full travel days. For partial days, the general rules in the travel policy apply.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, travels to London for a client workshop. She checks FIN-RATES-2026 before booking her meals, so she knows what she can claim without keeping every receipt.

## 3. Hotel cap

When you book a hotel for a business trip to the United Kingdom, the nightly rate must not exceed the cap shown below. This cap applies to the room rate before taxes and mandatory fees. If the only available hotels exceed the cap, please contact Finance in advance for approval.

| Location | Hotel cap |
|---|---|
| United Kingdom | €185 per night |

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, attends a conference in Manchester. He compares a few hotels and selects one within the cap, keeping his booking confirmation for his expense report.

## 4. Mileage

If you use your private car for business travel within the United Kingdom, mileage is reimbursed at €0.43 per km. This rate covers fuel, wear and tear, and insurance for the business portion of your journey. Keep a simple log of your trips, including the date, purpose, and distance travelled.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, is seconded to our London office for a few days. She rents a car through Ledgerly, so the mileage rate does not apply to her rental. The rate applies only when you drive your own private car.

## 5. Questions

If you have questions about this supplement or about a specific trip, please contact Finance. For questions about booking tools or expense submission, reach out to it-help@kranich.example. For anything related to your wellbeing while travelling, People & Culture is happy to help via people@kranich.example.

We review these supplements annually. Feedback from employees helps us keep them practical and fair.
