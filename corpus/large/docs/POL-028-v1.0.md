# Office & Meeting Room Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how to use our offices in Berlin (the Ritterstraße office) and Lisbon (the Rua do Alecrim office). We are a remote-first company, and most of us work from home within our country of employment. The offices are there for focused work, collaboration, and meeting with colleagues and guests.

We want everyone to feel welcome and productive in our spaces. This policy covers desk booking, meeting room use, and hosting guests. It applies to all employees, regardless of their home office or team.

If you have questions or need help with the tools described here, please reach out to Workplace & Operations or write to it-help@kranich.example. For anything related to building access or office safety, contact Rui Almeida, Head of Workplace & Operations.

## 2. Desks

Our offices offer open-plan seating with bookable desks. We do not have assigned desks; instead, you book a desk for the day you plan to be in the office. This keeps things flexible and fair for everyone.

Desks can be booked up to 8 days ahead in Perch. Perch is our internal tool for meeting-room and desk booking. You will find it in the same place as other internal tools, such as 1Password for passwords and Ledgerly for expenses.

When you book a desk, choose the office and the date. You can book a desk for a full day or for part of the day, depending on your plans. If your plans change, please release the desk in Perch so someone else can use it. This is especially important on busy days.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, knows she will visit the Ritterstraße office next week. She books a desk in Perch a few days ahead, as soon as her client meetings are confirmed. When one meeting moves online, she releases the desk for the afternoon so a colleague can take it.

Please keep your workspace tidy. At the end of the day, take your belongings with you. We do not have permanent storage for personal items. If you leave something behind, check with Workplace & Operations; items are kept for a short time before being cleared.

Some teams, such as Routing Core and Platform & Infrastructure, have areas where they often sit together. This is not a rule; it is a habit that helps with spontaneous conversations. You are free to sit anywhere with an available desk.

If you need a quiet space for calls or deep work, please use a meeting room or a phone booth if one is free. Please do not take long calls at an open desk when colleagues are working nearby. Use headphones when you can.

## 3. Meeting rooms

Each office has several meeting rooms of different sizes. You can book a room through Perch, just like a desk. Rooms are bookable for internal meetings, client calls, interviews, and workshops.

Please book a room only when you truly need one. If you are meeting online and everyone is remote, a room is not necessary. If you are in the office and need a quiet space for a call, you may use a free room for a short period without booking, as long as no one has reserved it.

For recurring meetings, be mindful of how much you reserve. Each person may hold at most 3 slots of recurring room bookings. A slot is one recurring booking, such as a weekly team meeting or a monthly review. If you need more recurring slots, please contact Workplace & Operations to discuss options; we may be able to suggest a different room or an alternative pattern.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, has a weekly team sync and a biweekly planning session. She books both as recurring meetings in Perch. When she starts a new working group that also meets weekly, she realizes she would hold a third recurring slot. That is fine, but she cannot add a fourth recurring meeting without releasing one of the existing slots.

If you book a room and do not need it, cancel the booking in Perch. Rooms that are booked but unused block colleagues who need them. The same applies if your meeting ends early: please release the room for the remaining time.

Please start and end your meetings on time. If you are running late, the room may be taken by someone else after a reasonable wait, especially if it was not booked. For booked rooms, Perch will show your booking, but we ask everyone to be considerate of others' time.

Meeting rooms have video conferencing equipment. If you have trouble with the equipment, please contact it-help@kranich.example. Do not move equipment between rooms. If you use a whiteboard, please clean it after your meeting.

## 4. Guests

Employees may host guests at our offices, including clients, partners, and candidates. Guests are welcome, but we ask you to register them in advance so we can prepare access and ensure safety.

To host a guest, please inform Workplace & Operations at least a few days before the visit. You will need to provide the guest's name and the purpose of the visit. This applies to both offices. For security reasons, guests should not be left unattended in the office; please accompany them during their visit.

When a guest arrives, please meet them at the entrance. In Berlin, the reception is on the ground floor of the Ritterstraße office. In Lisbon, the entrance is at the Rua do Alecrim office. If you are not available to meet your guest, arrange for a colleague to do so.

Guests may use the guest Wi-Fi network. Please do not share internal credentials or allow guests to access internal systems, including Perch, Fern, or Grove. If a guest needs access to a specific system for a demo, please coordinate with Platform & Infrastructure in advance.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer who works with clients in Poland, often brings clients to the Berlin office for demonstrations. Before each visit, he registers the guests with Workplace & Operations. On the day, he meets them at reception and escorts them to a booked meeting room. After the demo, he walks them back to the entrance.

If you host a guest for a full day, please book a meeting room for the duration. Do not host guests at an open desk, as this can disturb colleagues and raises privacy concerns. If you need a room for a guest and none is available, please talk to Workplace & Operations; we will help find a solution.

All guests must follow our general office guidelines, including health and safety rules. If you notice a guest acting in a way that concerns you, please speak to Workplace & Operations or security@kranich.example.

## 5. Questions

If you have questions about this policy, please contact Workplace & Operations. For technical issues with Perch or other tools, write to it-help@kranich.example. For people-related questions, such as how this policy interacts with flexible working arrangements, contact People & Culture at people@kranich.example.

We review our office policies regularly. Your feedback helps us improve the experience for everyone. If you have a suggestion, please share it with Rui Almeida or your manager. We also encourage you to read related policies in the Grove policy library, such as POL-004 for security awareness and the expense policy referenced in FIN-RATES-2026, to understand how office use connects to other parts of our ways of working.

Thank you for helping us keep our offices welcoming, productive, and safe for everyone.
