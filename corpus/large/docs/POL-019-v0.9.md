# Business Travel Insurance

## 1. Purpose

Travel is part of working at Kranich Software. Whether you are visiting a client in Poland, joining a workshop in Berlin, or meeting your team in Lisbon, we want you to travel with peace of mind. This policy explains the business travel insurance we provide, what it covers, and how to make a claim if you need to.

The insurance applies automatically when you travel for work. You do not need to enrol or pay anything. It complements any statutory health coverage you have in your country of employment and, where applicable, your private health insurance. It is not a substitute for your regular coverage; it is there to close gaps and cover travel-specific risks.

This policy is owned by Finance. If you have questions after reading it, see §4 for who to contact.

## 2. What is covered

Business travel insurance covers you for journeys you make for Kranich work purposes. A trip counts as business travel when it is approved in advance by your manager and booked through our travel tool in Ledgerly. This includes client visits, conferences, team offsites, onboarding weeks at one of our offices, and any other work-related journey that takes you away from your usual place of work.

The insurance is active from the moment you leave your home for the trip until you return home. If you combine a business trip with personal days, the insurance still applies for the whole journey, as long as the primary purpose is work. Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer, flies to Warsaw for a client workshop and stays an extra weekend to visit family. The insurance covers the entire time he is away, including the weekend.

The main areas of cover are:

- Medical costs abroad, including hospital treatment, doctor visits, prescription medicine, and emergency dental care. Medical costs abroad are covered up to €100,000 per trip.
- Emergency medical evacuation and repatriation, when a doctor advises that you need treatment at a suitable facility or need to return home.
- Trip cancellation or interruption, when you cannot travel or must cut a trip short due to a covered reason, such as sudden illness of you or a close family member.
- Lost, stolen, or damaged baggage and personal belongings, up to reasonable limits for items you carry for the trip.
- Travel delay, when a delay beyond your control causes you to miss a connection or incur necessary expenses such as accommodation or meals.
- Personal liability, if you accidentally cause injury to someone or damage their property while travelling for work.
- Legal assistance and emergency help lines, available around the clock by phone.

The insurance covers you in all countries where Kranich Software operates or where we send employees for work, including Germany, Portugal, Spain, Poland, France, and the United Kingdom, as well as any other destination approved by your manager.

Some situations are not covered. The insurance does not cover:

- Medical treatment that you knew you needed before the trip began, unless it is an emergency that arises during the trip.
- Activities that are unusually risky, such as motor racing, mountaineering with ropes, or extreme sports, unless you have prior written approval from your manager and Finance.
- The effects of alcohol or non-prescribed drugs.
- Travel that is not approved in advance or that is clearly personal in nature.
- Loss or damage caused by your own deliberate act or gross negligence.

If you have a pre-existing medical condition, you do not need to declare it for the insurance to be valid. However, if the condition requires ongoing treatment, please carry a letter from your doctor describing your condition and any medication you take. This helps medical staff abroad give you the right care.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, travels to Berlin for a team workshop. She develops a severe ear infection and sees a local doctor, who prescribes antibiotics. Her medical costs are covered under the policy, up to the limit in this section.

## 3. Making a claim

If something happens during a business trip that may lead to a claim, act promptly. The faster you inform us, the easier it is for us to help.

For medical emergencies, seek treatment first. Your health is the priority. After you have received care, contact the insurance help line using the number shown on your insurance card, which you can find in Ledgerly under your trip details. The help line can assist with finding a doctor, arranging payment, or organising evacuation if needed.

For non-medical claims, such as lost baggage or travel delay, follow these steps:

- Keep all receipts, tickets, boarding passes, and any written confirmation from the airline, hotel, or other provider involved.
- Take photos of damaged items or of the scene where something was lost or stolen.
- Report theft or loss to the local police if it is reasonable to do so, and keep the police report.
- Contact the insurance help line while you are still away, or as soon as you return, to register the claim.
- Submit the completed claim form along with your supporting documents.

Claims must be filed within 13 calendar days of the event. If you miss this deadline because you were hospitalised or otherwise unable to act, contact us as soon as you are able; we will consider the circumstances.

To file a claim, use the claim form in Ledgerly. The form asks for your trip details, a description of what happened, and the amounts you are claiming. Attach your supporting documents as scans or photos. If you have trouble with the form, email Finance at the address in §4 and we will guide you.

Once we receive your claim, we review it and, if needed, ask the insurer for a formal assessment. We aim to resolve claims promptly. If the insurer needs more information from you, we will let you know what is missing. You will receive the outcome in writing.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, flies to London for a client meeting. The airline loses her suitcase, which contains her laptop and presentation materials. She reports the loss at the airport desk, receives a written confirmation, and files a claim through Ledgerly when she returns. Finance helps her submit the documents to the insurer, and the claim is settled.

If your claim is approved, the insurer pays the covered costs directly to you or to the provider, depending on the situation. If you paid for something yourself and the claim is approved, you will be reimbursed to your bank account. Reimbursement is processed through Ledgerly, so please make sure your bank details there are current.

If your claim is declined, you will receive an explanation. If you believe the decision is wrong, you can ask Finance to review it. Finance will look at the policy wording and the insurer's reasoning, and will respond to you in writing.

## 4. Questions

If you have questions about this policy, about a specific trip, or about a claim, the first point of contact is Finance. You can reach us at the general Finance email alias listed on the Finance page in Grove. For urgent matters during a trip, use the insurance help line shown in Ledgerly.

For questions about how to book travel or about the expenses process, please see Ledgerly's help pages or contact the Workplace & Operations team. For questions about whether a trip qualifies as business travel, talk to your manager first.

Example: Sofia, a product manager in Fleet Insights, is planning a trip to meet a client in France. She is unsure whether a planned side visit to a supplier counts as business travel. She asks her manager, who confirms the itinerary, and then she checks Ledgerly for the insurance card and help line details.

We want travel to be straightforward. If anything in this policy is unclear, or if you face a situation not described here, please reach out. We would rather answer a question early than sort out a problem later.
