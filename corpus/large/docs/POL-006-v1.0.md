# Equipment & Hardware Policy

## 1. Purpose

At Kranich Software GmbH, we believe that great work starts with the right tools. This policy explains how equipment is provided, maintained, and returned so that everyone — whether you sit in the Ritterstraße office, the Rua do Alecrim office, or work remotely from your home — has a consistent and productive setup.

We want the process to be simple and fair. This policy covers the standard equipment you receive when you join, how the budget works, when equipment is refreshed, and what happens when you leave or when something breaks. If you have questions that are not answered here, reach out to Workplace & Operations or write to it-help@kranich.example.

## 2. Standard setup

Your standard setup is chosen to match your role. For most employees, this includes a laptop, a monitor, a keyboard, a mouse, a headset, and a docking station. The exact models may vary depending on your team and your work. For example, a backend engineer in Routing Core may receive a more powerful laptop, while a customer success manager may receive a setup optimised for many video calls.

If you work from an office, your equipment is set up at your desk. If you work remotely, we ship the equipment to your home address. In both cases, the equipment remains the property of Kranich Software GmbH. You are responsible for taking reasonable care of it and for keeping it secure. Do not share your laptop or let others use it, even briefly.

Software is installed centrally by Platform & Infrastructure. If you need additional software for your role, submit a request through the usual channel. Peripherals beyond the standard setup — for example, a second monitor or a special chair — can be discussed with your manager and Workplace & Operations.

## 3. Equipment budget

For each new role, Workplace & Operations sets an equipment budget. The equipment budget for a standard role is €2,000. This amount covers the laptop, monitor, and other core items listed in the standard setup. It is intended to be sufficient for a high-quality, reliable setup without requiring you to make a personal contribution.

If your role has specific needs that exceed the standard budget — for example, a field solutions engineer like Marek who needs a rugged device for client sites — your manager can request an exception. The request is reviewed by Workplace & Operations together with the relevant team lead. We ask that you discuss any special needs early in the onboarding process so that the order is not delayed.

The budget is not a personal allowance. You cannot use it to buy items outside the approved equipment list, and you cannot receive the difference in cash. If you have an idea for equipment that would improve your work but is not on the list, please share it — we are always open to suggestions.

## 4. Refresh cycle

We keep our equipment current so that you can work efficiently and securely. Equipment is refreshed every 36 months. When your equipment reaches this age, Workplace & Operations will contact you to arrange a replacement.

The refresh process is straightforward. You receive your new device, transfer your files, and return the old one. We handle the data migration carefully to make sure nothing is lost. Platform & Infrastructure will wipe the old device after you have confirmed that your files are moved.

If your equipment is still working well at the refresh point, you may keep it for a little longer. If your equipment fails before the refresh point and cannot be repaired, we will replace it earlier — see §6 for details. If you change roles within the company and your new role requires different equipment, we will adjust your setup at that time rather than waiting for the regular refresh.

## 5. Returning equipment

When you leave Kranich Software GmbH, for any reason, all company equipment must be returned. Equipment must be returned within 10 business days after the last working day. This includes your laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse, headset, docking station, and any other items that were provided to you.

Before you return the equipment, you should remove any personal files and sign out of personal accounts. Platform & Infrastructure will handle the secure wipe of the device after it arrives. You will receive a prepaid shipping label from Workplace & Operations. If you work near an office, you may also return the equipment in person.

Example: Sofia, a product manager in Fleet Insights who works remotely from Spain, receives a shipping label by email on her last day. She packs the laptop, monitor, and peripherals in the original boxes, attaches the label, and drops the parcel at the collection point within the required time.

If you do not return the equipment within the required period, Workplace & Operations will contact you. Continued non-return may affect your final payroll settlement, as described in the relevant finance policy. Please treat the return as a priority — it helps us equip your successor without delay.

## 6. Repairs and loss

If your equipment breaks or malfunctions, contact it-help@kranich.example as soon as you notice the problem. Do not attempt to repair the device yourself, and do not take it to an external repair shop. Our support team will diagnose the issue and arrange a repair or a replacement.

For minor issues, such as a faulty cable or a worn keyboard, we will send a replacement part directly to you. For more significant issues, we may ask you to send the device to our repair partner. In that case, you will receive a temporary device if you need one to continue your work.

If your equipment is lost or stolen, report it immediately to it-help@kranich.example and to security@kranich.example if the device may contain company data. You should also file a report with the local police where applicable. We understand that accidents happen, and we will not penalise you for a genuine loss. However, if we find that the loss resulted from gross negligence — for example, leaving your laptop unattended in a public place — you may be asked to contribute toward the replacement cost. Each case is reviewed individually by Workplace & Operations together with your manager.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, spills coffee on her laptop during a client call. She contacts it-help the same day, sends the device in for repair, and receives a temporary laptop within a few days. Her work continues with minimal interruption.

## 7. Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, please reach out to Workplace & Operations or write to it-help@kranich.example. For questions about the budget or exceptions, your manager can also help. For security-related concerns about your equipment, contact security@kranich.example.

We review this policy from time to time to make sure it stays useful and fair. Your feedback is welcome at any time — tell your manager or Workplace & Operations what works well and what could be better. We are here to support you with the tools you need to do your best work.
