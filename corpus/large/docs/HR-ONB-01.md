# Onboarding Guide — Routing Core

## 1. Purpose

Welcome to Kranich Software. This guide helps new members of Routing Core settle in during their first weeks. It explains who you will meet, what to expect, and where to find the tools and policies that support your work.

Routing Core builds the optimization engine at the heart of Kranich Route Cloud. Your colleagues are spread across Berlin, Lisbon, and remote locations. You will work with people from Platform & Infrastructure, Fleet Insights, and Customer Success. Aylin Demir, VP People & Culture, and your engineering manager will support you along the way.

## 2. Before you start

Your manager will send you a welcome note with your start date and your assigned buddy. Your buddy is an experienced engineer who can answer everyday questions and introduce you to the team.

Before your first day, please complete the following:

- Set up your Kranich account using the invitation you receive by email.
- Install 1Password and store your credentials there. You will use it for all internal services.
- Book a desk or a meeting room through Perch if you plan to work from an office.
- Review your personal details in Fern and add your emergency contact.
- Read the security basics in POL-004 before your first team meeting.

If you need equipment, your manager will explain the budget available to you. See POL-006 §3 for details on what you can request and how to order it. Please place your order early so everything arrives before you start.

## 3. Step by step

### First day

Your manager will welcome you in the team channel and schedule a short video call. You will meet your buddy and receive access to the repositories you need. Take time to read the team charter and the development workflow guide.

Set up your local environment. If you hit any issue, ask in the team channel — someone will help promptly. At the end of the day, join the team standup to introduce yourself.

### First week

Your buddy will walk you through the codebase and explain how Routing Core connects to other teams. You will attend your first planning session and observe how the team breaks down work.

Complete the security training required for all employees. The deadline is stated in POL-005 §3; please do not wait until the last moment. Your manager will confirm when you have finished.

Set up your profile in Grove and read the team pages. Book time with your manager for a one-on-one to discuss your goals for the coming months.

### First month

You will take on your first small task with close support from your buddy. Expect to pair with a colleague on at least one piece of work. You will join the Routing Core review session and present your work to the team.

Your manager will hold a check-in with you to review progress and answer questions. You will also meet your team's product manager to understand the roadmap and how your work contributes to it.

### First quarter

By now you should feel comfortable with the daily workflow. You will contribute to planning, participate in code reviews, and share feedback in the team retrospective. Your manager will schedule a formal review conversation to discuss your development and agree on focus areas for the next months.

## 4. Common questions

**Where do I find the team calendar?**  
All meetings appear in your Kranich calendar automatically. You can also check the team page in Grove.

**How do I request time off?**  
Use Fern to submit a leave request. Your manager will approve it there. Please give as much notice as you can so the team can plan.

**What if I work from a different time zone?**  
Routing Core is remote-friendly. We keep core collaboration hours in the afternoon Central European Time. Outside those hours, you can work flexibly. Your manager will help you agree on a rhythm that works for you and the team.

**How do I book a desk or a room?**  
Use Perch. You can see availability for the Berlin and Lisbon offices and reserve a spot for the day.

**Where do I report an IT issue?**  
Write to it-help@kranich.example. For security concerns, contact security@kranich.example.

**What if I need an adjustment to my workspace?**  
Talk to your manager. They will work with Rui Almeida, Head of Workplace & Operations, to arrange what you need.

**How do I submit expenses?**  
Use Ledgerly for any work-related costs. Attach the receipt and add a short note. Your manager approves the expense there.

**Whom do I ask about people-related questions?**  
Write to people@kranich.example. Lea, from People Operations, will help you or point you to the right person.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, joined Routing Core recently. On her first day, her buddy walked her through the routing service repository. She booked her desk in the Rua do Alecrim office through Perch and completed her security training within the window described in POL-005. When she had a question about her leave balance, she checked Fern and then wrote to people@kranich.example for clarification.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, joined Routing Core after moving from another team. His manager arranged a short handover with the previous engineer. Marek used Grove to review the team's decision records and joined the weekly review to understand recent changes.

## 5. Contacts

For everyday questions, start with your buddy or your manager. The following people and teams can also help:

- **People & Culture**: people@kranich.example — leave, personal data, and general support.
- **IT help**: it-help@kranich.example — access, accounts, and equipment issues.
- **Security**: security@kranich.example — report concerns or ask about policy.
- **Workplace & Operations**: Rui Almeida — office-related matters in Berlin and Lisbon.
- **People Operations**: Lea — practical support for onboarding and people processes.

Your engineering manager is Ines Duarte if you are based in Berlin, or your local manager if you work elsewhere. They will introduce themselves during your first week.

Thank you for joining Kranich Software. We are glad you are here, and we look forward to building great things with you.
