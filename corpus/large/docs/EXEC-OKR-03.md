# Company OKRs — Q3 2025

Access: Managers only

## 1. Objectives

This quarter we focus on three objectives that build on the progress we made earlier this year. First, we accelerate adoption of Kranich Route Cloud among mid-size logistics operators in our core European markets. Second, we deepen the value our platform delivers to existing fleets by improving the reliability and usefulness of the Fleet Insights module. Third, we strengthen the internal foundations that let us scale sustainably — our platform health, our customer-facing processes, and the way we collaborate across remote-first teams.

Each objective is supported by a small set of key results that are ambitious yet achievable. Owners are accountable for driving progress and for communicating openly with their teams about what is going well and where we need to adjust. We encourage managers to discuss these objectives with their direct reports and to connect individual work to the company priorities.

## 2. Key results

Objective one — accelerate adoption — is measured by growth in the number of active fleets using Kranich Route Cloud. The key result targets 35% growth in active fleets. Supporting results include shortening the average time from first demo to signed contract, and increasing the share of new customers who complete onboarding within the planned window.

Objective two — deepen value for existing fleets — is measured by the adoption rate of Fleet Insights among active fleets, by the reduction in support tickets related to data quality, and by the share of customers who renew their annual plan. We also track how many customers enable automated schedule optimisation, as this feature correlates strongly with retention.

Objective three — strengthen internal foundations — is measured by the availability of the core routing service, by the completion of the security improvement roadmap owned by Information Security, and by the employee engagement score from the most recent internal survey. We also track the number of cross-team initiatives that meet their agreed milestones.

## 4. Review

We review these OKRs at the leadership team offsite in the middle of the quarter and again at the end of the quarter. Managers receive a brief update after each review, and we encourage them to share relevant highlights with their teams promptly.

If market conditions or customer feedback require a change in priority, the leadership team will communicate the adjustment openly and explain the reasoning. We expect owners to flag risks early rather than waiting for the formal review. Cross references to related policies, such as POL-004, should be read in the context of this quarter's priorities.

The fiscal year remains aligned with the calendar year, and this document supersedes the previous quarter's OKR set. For any questions about interpretation, please contact the owner of the relevant objective or the People & Culture team.
