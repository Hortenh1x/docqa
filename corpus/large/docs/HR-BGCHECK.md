# Background Checks

Access: People & Culture only

## 1. Purpose

At Kranich Software GmbH, we build trust with our customers and with each other. Background checks help us confirm that the information shared during the hiring process is accurate, and they help us protect the security of the Kranich Route Cloud platform and the data our customers entrust to us.

This policy explains when we run background checks, what they cover, and how we handle the results. We aim to make the process transparent and fair. A background check is one part of a broader hiring decision — it is never the only factor.

## 2. Who this is for

This policy applies to all candidates who receive a conditional offer of employment with Kranich Software GmbH, regardless of location or team. It also applies to current employees in limited circumstances, described below.

We run checks for roles across all teams: Routing Core, Fleet Insights, Platform & Infrastructure, Customer Success, and Go-to-Market. The depth of the check may vary by role, but the principles in this policy apply equally to everyone.

## 3. Process

### When we run a check

We run a background check only after we have made a conditional offer of employment. The offer is contingent on a satisfactory result. We never run a check before this point, and we never run one without your knowledge.

For current employees, we may run a check again in rare situations, for example when you move into a role with substantially different responsibilities or when a customer contract requires a renewed check. We will always discuss the reason with you first.

### What we check

The scope of the check depends on the role and the country where you are employed. In general, we may verify:

- Your identity.
- Your professional qualifications and degrees.
- Your employment history, as you presented it during the hiring process.
- Your right to work in the country of employment.
- Relevant criminal records, where legally permitted and where the role justifies it.

For roles with access to sensitive customer data or significant platform privileges, the check may be more detailed. We will always tell you what the check covers before we begin.

### How we run it

We work with a third-party screening provider that operates in your country of employment. We choose providers that follow local data protection law. We share only the information needed to complete the check, and we instruct the provider to collect only what is relevant.

Where local law requires it, we will ask for your written consent before starting. You will receive a clear explanation of what will be checked and who will see the results.

### How we use the results

Results are reviewed by People & Culture only. Hiring managers receive a simple outcome — satisfactory or unsatisfactory — and never see the underlying report unless you choose to share it.

If a check raises a concern, we will contact you directly to discuss it. You will have the opportunity to explain the circumstances. We consider the nature of the finding, its relevance to the role, and how much time has passed. A finding does not automatically mean we withdraw the offer.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, had a minor administrative fine from several years ago that appeared on a routine records search. People & Culture contacted him, he explained the context, and the finding had no bearing on his work with clients. His offer proceeded without change.

### Confidentiality

Background check reports are confidential. We store them securely in our HR systems with access limited to People & Culture. We retain them only as long as necessary and delete them in line with applicable data protection rules.

## 4. Timelines

We aim to complete the process promptly so that you are not left waiting. The time it takes depends on the country, the scope of the check, and how quickly previous employers and institutions respond.

We will keep you informed if there is any delay. If a check takes longer than expected, we will let you know what is causing the delay and when you can expect an update.

We understand that waiting is stressful. If you have a start date already agreed and the check is still pending, we will work with you and your future manager to find a practical solution, such as starting on a later date.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, had her start date approaching while her previous employer was slow to confirm her employment dates. People & Culture contacted her promptly, explained the situation, and agreed a revised start date with her and her new manager. The check was completed a few days later without any issues.

## 5. Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, the scope of a check, or the results process, please contact People & Culture at people@kranich.example.

If you believe a result is incorrect, tell us as soon as you can. We will work with the screening provider to verify the information, and we will not make a final decision until the matter is resolved.

For questions about how your personal data is handled during a background check, you can also contact the Information Security team at security@kranich.example. They can explain our data protection practices in more detail.

For related policies, please refer to the hiring and data protection documents in the Grove policy library. This policy works together with those documents, and where there is a conflict, the more specific policy applies.

We are committed to making this process fair, respectful, and transparent. If you feel that a decision related to a background check was unfair, you can raise the matter with People & Culture, and we will review it thoroughly.
