# Corporate Card Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how corporate cards are issued, used, and reconciled at Kranich Software GmbH. It replaces version 1.0 of this policy, which was in effect until the effective date above. The goal is to make business spending simple and transparent: employees should not have to pay out of pocket for legitimate work expenses, and Finance should be able to track spending accurately.

We issue corporate cards to cover business-related costs such as travel, client meetings, software subscriptions, and office supplies. This policy applies to all employees, regardless of whether they work from our Berlin office, our Lisbon office, or remotely from another country of employment.

We keep the rules straightforward. If something is not covered here, ask Finance before using the card. When in doubt, use your own judgment, but be ready to explain the expense during reconciliation.

## 2. Who gets a card

Corporate cards are available to employees whose role regularly requires business spending. This includes people in Customer Success, Go-to-Market, Field Solutions, and leadership. It also includes people in other teams who travel or buy supplies on behalf of the company.

You do not need to ask for a card if you rarely spend company money. In those cases, you can use the expense reimbursement process instead, which is described in POL-004.

To request a card, speak with your manager. Your manager confirms that your role warrants one, and then Finance arranges the card. Cards are issued in your name and are for your use only. You may not lend your card to a colleague, a family member, or anyone else.

When you leave Kranich, your card is deactivated on your last working day. If you change roles and no longer need a card, please return it to Finance promptly.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, travels frequently to customer sites. His manager confirms that a card is appropriate, and Finance issues one to him. He uses it for trains, meals with clients, and the occasional overnight stay.

## 3. Using the card

Use the corporate card only for business purposes. Personal purchases are never allowed, even if you intend to reimburse the company later.

Acceptable uses include:

- Travel costs such as flights, trains, and hotels
- Meals with clients or during business travel
- Business software and online services
- Office supplies and equipment, with manager approval
- Conference fees and related event costs

Before making a purchase above a modest amount, check with your manager if you are unsure whether the expense is justified. For recurring charges, such as a monthly software subscription, make sure the subscription is already approved by your team lead.

You are responsible for keeping your card details secure. Do not store the card number in unapproved tools. If you suspect fraud or loss, contact security@kranich.example immediately and then it-help@kranich.example for a replacement.

Do not use the card for cash advances. Do not use it to pay fines, penalties, or personal legal fees. If you accidentally use the card for a personal expense, flag it in the reconciliation tool right away so Finance can arrange a correction.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, buys a book about a new programming framework for her team's library. That is a reasonable team expense. She also picks up a coffee for herself on the way back to her home office; she pays for that with her own money, not the corporate card.

## 4. Receipts and reconciliation

Every charge on the corporate card must be reconciled. You are responsible for submitting receipts and a short explanation for each transaction within the timeframe described in POL-004 §6. Do not wait for a reminder.

For each transaction, provide:

- A clear description of what was purchased
- The business reason
- The receipt, if one was issued

If a receipt is missing, write a short note explaining why. For example, a taxi ride may not produce a paper receipt; in that case, note the date, route, and purpose.

Your manager approves your reconciled expenses. Finance reviews them afterwards. If something is unclear, Finance will reach out to you directly. Please respond promptly so that reconciliation can be completed.

If you notice an error on your statement, such as a duplicate charge or an incorrect amount, contact Finance before the deadline in POL-004 §6 has passed.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, takes a client to dinner. She keeps the itemized receipt and submits it with a note saying which client and which project the dinner supported. Her manager approves it, and Finance closes the transaction.

## 5. Limits and approvals

Access: Finance only

The single-transaction limit on the corporate card is €1,500. If a purchase exceeds this amount, you cannot use the card for the full transaction. Instead, discuss the purchase with your manager, who may request a special approval from Finance. Finance will then decide whether to raise the limit temporarily or arrange a different payment method.

Monthly spend per card is capped at €4,500. This cap resets at the start of each calendar month. If you expect to exceed the cap, for example because of a large conference trip, talk to Finance in advance. They can review your plan and, if appropriate, approve a temporary increase.

Your manager does not need to approve every individual transaction at the time of purchase. However, your manager is responsible for reviewing your monthly reconciliation and confirming that the spending was appropriate.

Finance has the right to reduce limits or revoke a card if the policy is not followed. Repeated late reconciliations or unexplained transactions may lead to a card being suspended.

## 6. Questions

If you have questions about this policy, please reach out to Finance. For questions about how to record an expense or what counts as a business cost, refer to POL-004.

If you believe your card has been misused or you see a suspicious transaction, contact security@kranich.example. For lost or damaged cards, or technical issues with the card provider's portal, contact it-help@kranich.example.

For questions about whether your role qualifies for a card, speak with your manager first. If you need a card urgently, for example for an upcoming trip, ask your manager to flag the request to Finance so it can be processed promptly.

Example: Sofia, a product manager in Fleet Insights working remotely from Spain, wonders whether she can use the corporate card to buy a new headset for her home setup. She checks with her manager, who confirms that this is an approved home-office expense within the single-transaction limit. Sofia makes the purchase and submits the receipt during reconciliation.

Finance reviews this policy regularly to keep it aligned with how we work. If you have suggestions for making it clearer or more practical, we welcome your input.
