# First Aid & Emergencies

## 1. Purpose

This document explains how we handle first aid and emergencies across Kranich Software GmbH. It applies to everyone, whether you work from the Ritterstraße office, the Rua do Alecrim office, or remotely from home.

Our goal is simple: in an emergency, you know what to do and who to turn to. We keep the guidance practical and ask you to read it once so that you can act calmly if the situation arises.

## 2. First aiders

Each office has designated first aiders. They are colleagues who have completed certified first aid training and refresh their skills regularly. You can find their names and photos on the notice boards near the entrances and on Grove, our intranet.

If you need first aid, approach any first aider directly. If the nearest first aider is unavailable, ask a colleague to help you locate another one. First aiders are equipped to handle minor injuries and to stabilise a person until professional help arrives.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, once cut her hand on a broken coffee cup. She walked to the kitchen notice board, found the first aider on duty, and received prompt treatment without needing to leave the office.

If you are interested in becoming a first aider, speak to your manager or reach out to Workplace & Operations. We welcome volunteers from all teams. Training is arranged during working hours, and we cover the cost.

## 3. Evacuation

Evacuation procedures differ slightly between our offices, so please familiarise yourself with the specific plan for the location where you normally work. The plans are posted next to the lifts and stairwells, and they are also available on Grove.

When you hear the evacuation alarm, stop what you are doing immediately. Leave personal belongings behind. Follow the nearest marked exit route to the designated assembly point. Do not use the lifts. Help anyone who needs assistance, but do not put yourself at risk.

Once you reach the assembly point, stay there until the responsible person confirms that everyone is accounted for. If you know that a colleague is still inside, tell the person in charge. Do not re-enter the building until you are told it is safe.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer, was visiting the Ritterstraße office when the fire alarm sounded during a client call. He ended the call promptly, guided a visitor to the stairwell, and joined his colleagues at the assembly point. The all-clear came after a short wait, and everyone returned inside.

For our remote colleagues, an emergency may look different. If you work from home and experience a serious incident, first make sure you and those around you are safe. Then contact your manager or the relevant local emergency service. Workplace & Operations can help you think through an individual emergency plan if your home working environment has specific needs.

In any emergency, call the local emergency number before you contact internal support. Internal channels are helpful for coordination, but they should never delay professional help.

## 4. Questions

If you have questions about first aid, evacuation, or emergency preparedness, contact Workplace & Operations at it-help@kranich.example. For matters that involve your personal safety at home, you can also reach out to People & Culture at people@kranich.example.

We review this policy periodically and will communicate any changes through Grove. In the meantime, if something feels unclear or missing, please tell us. Your feedback helps us keep everyone safe.
