# Vacation FAQ

## 1. Questions

**How much vacation do I get?**

Full-time employees receive 25 days of vacation per calendar year. This is the standard entitlement for everyone employed on a full-time basis, regardless of which office or country you work from. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated amount based on their contracted hours.

**When does my vacation year run?**

Our vacation year is the calendar year. You can book vacation days for the current year at any time, and you should plan to use them within that same year. Please check POL-004 for the rules on carrying days over to the next year.

**How do I request vacation?**

Submit your request through Fern, our HRIS. Choose the dates you want, add a short note for your manager, and send it for approval. Your manager will review it promptly. If you need to change or cancel an approved request, you can do so in Fern as long as your manager has not already approved the change.

**Who approves my vacation?**

Your direct manager approves vacation requests. They will consider team coverage and business needs when making a decision. We encourage you to discuss your plans with your team early, especially if you are requesting popular dates such as school holidays or the period between Christmas and New Year.

**What if I am sick during my vacation?**

If you fall ill while on vacation, you can report your sickness through the usual process in Fern. You will not lose the vacation days for the period you were sick. Please let your manager know as soon as possible and follow the sickness reporting steps described in POL-004.

**Can I take vacation in my first months with the company?**

Yes. Vacation accrues from your first day of employment. You can request days once you have started, even if you joined recently. Your manager will help you plan around your onboarding and any initial training.

**What about public holidays?**

Public holidays are separate from your vacation days. They depend on the country where you work and, in Germany, on the federal state of your workplace. Public holidays do not reduce your vacation entitlement.

**Where can I find more details?**

The full policy is available in POL-004 on Grove. If you have questions that are not answered there, please reach out to people@kranich.example.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, uses Fern to request a week of vacation in May. Her manager approves it the same day, and Anna adds the dates to her calendar so her team knows when she is away.
