# Finance Update 2024 Q1

Access: Finance only

## 1. Summary

This quarterly update is intended for the Finance team only. It provides a brief overview of our performance in the first quarter of the current year. We continue to manage our resources carefully as we support the growth of the Kranich Route Cloud across our markets.

## 2. Revenue

Revenue grew 15.5% year over year in 2024 Q1. This increase reflects continued adoption of our routing and fleet insights products by mid-size logistics operators, particularly in the markets where we have a strong presence. Our subscription-based model remains the primary driver of this growth. We saw healthy expansion within our existing customer base, as well as new customer acquisitions. The performance in the quarter positions us well for the remainder of the year, although we remain mindful of market conditions.

## 3. Costs

Our cost base remains in line with the expectations we set at the start of the year. Personnel costs constitute the largest share, reflecting our remote-first setup across several countries. We continue to invest in our platform and infrastructure to ensure reliability and performance for our customers. Travel and office-related expenses are modest, as most employees work from home within their country of employment. We regularly review our spending to identify efficiencies without compromising quality or employee experience. Where cost increases are anticipated, we will address them in the next update.

## 4. Outlook

Looking ahead to the remainder of 2024, we expect revenue to continue on a positive trajectory, though growth rates may vary from quarter to quarter. We will focus on retaining our existing customers and supporting our teams in delivering value. Cost management remains a priority, and we will continue to monitor our spending closely. Any significant changes to our financial outlook will be communicated in subsequent updates. For details on specific expense policies or rates, please refer to the relevant finance documents, such as POL-004 or FIN-RATES-2026. We thank you for your continued diligence in managing our resources.
