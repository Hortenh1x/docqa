# Learning Budget FAQ

## 1. Questions

**How much learning budget do I get?**
Every employee has a learning budget of €500 per year. The budget is intended to support your professional development in areas that help you grow in your role at Kranich.

**What can I use the budget for?**
You can use it for a wide range of learning activities. This includes conferences, workshops, online courses, books, and professional certifications. It also covers exam fees and membership dues for professional bodies relevant to your work.

**Do I need approval before I spend?**
Yes. Please discuss your plan with your manager first. For most expenses, a quick conversation is enough. Your manager will confirm that the activity aligns with your development goals and the team's needs. For larger purchases, such as a multi-day conference, please get written approval in advance.

**How do I request reimbursement?**
Submit your expense through Ledgerly, our expenses tool. Attach the receipt and add a short note explaining how the activity supports your development. The Finance team reviews requests promptly. If you need to book travel or accommodation for a learning event, you can also do that through Ledgerly.

**What happens if I join Kranich partway through the year?**
The budget is available from your first day of employment. You can use the full amount in your first year, regardless of when you join.

**Can I carry unused budget over to the next year?**
No. The budget resets at the start of each calendar year. We encourage you to plan ahead so you can make the most of it.

**Are there any restrictions?**
The budget is for learning that is reasonably connected to your role or your professional growth at Kranich. It is not a general personal allowance. Expenses for equipment, software that is already provided by the company, or activities that are primarily recreational are not covered. If you are unsure whether something qualifies, ask your manager or People & Culture before you spend.

**What if my manager and I disagree about a purchase?**
Talk it through first. If you still cannot agree, reach out to People & Culture. We will help find a fair outcome. For guidance on related policies, see §3 of POL-004. Questions about how learning expenses are handled for tax or accounting purposes can be directed to Finance; refer to FIN-RATES-2026 where relevant.

**Example:** Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, wants to attend a conference on distributed systems. She checks the agenda with her manager, books her place, and later submits the expense through Ledgerly with her receipt. Her manager had already confirmed the plan, so the reimbursement is processed quickly.
