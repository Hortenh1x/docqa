# Finance Update 2025 Q2
Access: Finance only

## 1. Summary

This update provides a brief overview of the company's financial performance for the second quarter of 2025. It is intended for the Finance team only and complements the detailed reporting available in FIN-RATES-2026 and related policy documents. Please direct any questions to the Finance owner before the next quarterly cycle.

Overall, the quarter reflects steady execution across our commercial teams. We continue to monitor cost discipline and investment priorities closely. A fuller discussion of revenue and costs follows below.

## 2. Revenue

Revenue grew 16% year over year in 2025 Q2. This result was driven by continued adoption of the Kranich Route Cloud among mid-size logistics operators, particularly in markets where our field teams have deepened client relationships.

The growth was broad-based across our main product areas. Renewals remained healthy, and new customer additions contributed meaningfully to the quarterly figure. Our Customer Success and Go-to-Market teams collaborated well to expand usage within existing accounts.

We saw particular strength in the fleet insights segment, where customers increasingly value data-driven decision support. The Routing Core product also performed solidly, with several clients expanding their subscription scope. Revenue from services and support remained stable, reflecting our focus on recurring software revenue.

## 3. Costs

Operating costs were in line with expectations for the quarter. Personnel expenses, our largest cost category, developed as planned. We continue to invest in engineering capacity, primarily within the Routing Core and Platform & Infrastructure teams, to support product development and platform reliability.

Sales and marketing spend was moderate, consistent with our efficient go-to-market approach. We prioritize investments that show a clear link to pipeline and customer retention. Customer success-related costs increased slightly, reflecting our commitment to high-touch support for growing accounts.

General and administrative costs remained stable. We continue to use internal tools such as Ledgerly for expense management and travel booking, which supports timely and accurate cost tracking. No unusual or one-off items were recorded in the quarter.

Cost management remains a shared responsibility across all teams. We appreciate the care everyone takes in planning and approving expenditures. As always, please refer to the relevant finance policies for guidance on approvals and documentation.

## 4. Outlook

Looking ahead to the remainder of 2025, we expect the positive revenue trend to continue, supported by our product roadmap and market demand for route optimization. We will keep a close eye on cost development, particularly in areas where we are scaling teams or expanding client-facing activities.

Our hiring plans for the second half of the year remain focused and deliberate. We will prioritize roles that directly support product quality, customer success, and platform resilience. Any changes to these plans will be communicated through the usual finance and people processes.

We also continue to review our internal processes for efficiency. The Finance team will work with other departments to refine forecasting and reporting as we move into the next planning cycle. Further details will be shared in due course.

Thank you for your continued attention to financial discipline. Please reach out to the Finance team if you have any questions about this update or the underlying data.
