# Finance Update 2024 Q4

Access: Finance only

## 1. Summary

This update provides a high-level view of our financial performance for the final quarter of the calendar year. As always, the details here are intended for the Finance team only. We keep this brief, focusing on the headline revenue result and the main cost drivers we are watching into the new year.

The quarter closed as expected, with steady demand from our logistics customers across the markets we serve. Our teams continued to deliver on the roadmap, and we saw no material disruptions to billing or collections. The figures below reflect our usual accounting practices.

## 2. Revenue

Revenue grew 10.5% year over year in 2024 Q4. This growth came from a mix of existing customers expanding their usage of the Kranich Route Cloud and a modest number of new logos, particularly among mid-size operators in Northern and Central Europe. Our subscription model remains predictable, and renewal rates stayed in line with recent quarters.

We also saw healthy adoption of newer modules within our installed base, which contributed to the quarterly result. No single customer accounted for a disproportionate share of the growth. The sales pipeline entering the new year looks solid, though we do not comment on forward-looking revenue figures here.

## 3. Costs

Operating costs for the quarter were in line with our internal plan. The largest categories remain personnel and cloud infrastructure, both of which we manage closely. We continue to invest in the Routing Core and Fleet Insights teams, as these areas drive product differentiation.

Travel and customer-facing expenses were moderate, reflecting our remote-first setup and the mix of in-person meetings our field teams conduct. We saw a slight uptick in costs related to customer onboarding support, which is a deliberate investment in retention. We expect this trend to continue for a few more months.

No unusual or one-off charges arose during the quarter. We have not changed any internal cost policies; please refer to the relevant finance documents for expense guidance, such as the rates document referenced in the policy library.

## 4. Outlook

As we enter the new year, our priorities remain disciplined cost management and continued investment in product areas that support revenue growth. We will monitor infrastructure spend closely, as usage scales with customer activity.

We plan to review our internal charging model for platform usage, and any changes will be communicated through the usual channels. The Finance team will continue to issue these quarterly updates on a regular cadence. For any questions about this document, please reach out to the Finance team directly.

We thank everyone for their focus and collaboration throughout the year.
