# Finance Update 2024 Q2

Access: Finance only

## 1. Summary

This update covers our financial performance for the second quarter of the current year. The quarter was marked by steady progress across our key business lines, supported by continued investment in our platform. Overall, we are pleased with the momentum we see as we move into the second half of the year.

## 2. Revenue

Revenue grew 8% year over year in 2024 Q2. This growth reflects strong demand from mid-size logistics operators across our core markets in Germany, Poland, and the United Kingdom. Our recurring subscription base continued to expand, and we saw healthy adoption of new features within the Routing Core and Fleet Insights modules.

Customer retention remained solid, and we recorded several notable expansions with existing accounts. Our field solutions team, including colleagues like Marek working with clients in Poland, contributed to this positive trajectory by deepening relationships and identifying new use cases. While we do not disclose detailed figures here, the directional trend is encouraging.

## 3. Costs

Operating costs for the quarter were in line with our expectations. Our largest cost categories continue to be personnel and infrastructure, reflecting our remote-first setup and investment in platform reliability. We maintained discipline in discretionary spending while continuing to fund priority initiatives.

We also continued to invest in our security posture, working closely with the Head of Information Security to ensure our controls remain robust. Travel and client-facing expenses were moderate, as most customer engagements remain virtual. We expect cost growth to stay measured through the remainder of the year.

## 4. Outlook

Looking ahead, we anticipate continued gradual growth in the coming quarters. Our pipeline for new business remains active, and we are focused on converting opportunities in Spain, France, and Portugal. We will also continue to enhance the Fleet Insights offering, which we believe has strong potential.

We remain mindful of macroeconomic conditions and will adjust our plans as needed. Our leadership team, including the CEO and CFO, will monitor performance closely and provide further updates when appropriate. Any material changes to our outlook will be communicated promptly.

For questions regarding this update, please reach out to the Finance team directly. This document is confidential and intended for internal use only.
