# Announcement: Benefits changes 2026

## 1. Announcement

Dear colleagues,

As we look ahead to the new year, we want to share some updates to our benefits offering. These changes reflect what we have learned from your feedback and from how we work across our offices and remote locations.

The full details are described in POL-004, which has been updated accordingly. We encourage you to review that policy for the complete picture. This announcement highlights the main themes.

**Health and well-being.** We are broadening our health coverage to better support preventive care and mental health. The new offering gives you more flexibility in choosing what fits your personal situation, whether you are in Germany, Portugal, or any other country where we employ people.

**Family support.** We are strengthening our family-related benefits. This includes improvements to parental leave top-up arrangements and additional support for families with young children. We believe this helps everyone balance their career and home life.

**Learning and development.** We are introducing a refreshed approach to professional growth. Instead of a one-size-fits-all budget, you will be able to choose from a curated set of learning opportunities, including conferences, certifications, and coaching. More guidance will follow from your manager and from People & Culture.

**Flexible working.** We are making our remote-work and flexible-hours arrangements more explicit. The updated policy clarifies what you can expect as standard and how to request adjustments.

**Financial well-being.** We are simplifying how certain allowances are administered. The new structure is easier to understand and aligns with local practices in each country of employment.

**What happens next.** The changes take effect in the new year. Your local People partner will reach out to you over the coming weeks with details specific to your role and location. If you have questions before then, please contact people@kranich.example.

We are proud to support you in this way and look forward to growing together.

Warm regards,

Aylin Demir, VP People & Culture, on behalf of the team
