# Travel Supplement — France

## 1. Purpose

This supplement describes how travel expenses for business trips to France are handled at Kranich Software GmbH. It applies to all employees travelling to France on company business, regardless of their home office or country of employment. The rules here complement the general travel policy and the rate card in FIN-RATES-2026, which you should read together with this document.

We aim to keep travel arrangements straightforward and comfortable. When you travel to France, you can rely on the daily allowance, hotel reimbursement and mileage rules below. If you are unsure whether a particular expense is covered, ask your manager or the Finance team before you book.

## 2. Per-diem

The per-diem for business trips to France covers meals and incidental expenses while you are travelling. This daily rate is set centrally and published in FIN-RATES-2026, which is the single source of truth for the amount. Please consult that document for the current per-diem figure before you submit an expense report.

The per-diem applies for each full day of your trip. For partial travel days, follow the rules in the general travel policy. You do not need to itemise your meals when you claim the per-diem; it is meant to simplify your expense reporting.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, travels to Lyon to meet a client. She claims the per-diem for the days she is away and does not need to keep every restaurant receipt.

## 3. Hotel cap

When you book accommodation for a business trip in France, Kranich Software reimburses your hotel costs up to the nightly cap listed below. The cap applies to the room rate before taxes and any parking or room-service charges, which you should claim separately if they are business-related.

| Location | Hotel cap |
| --- | --- |
| France | €115 per night |

Please book within this cap whenever possible. If a suitable hotel within the cap is not available — for example, during a major trade fair — you may request an exception in advance through Ledgerly, explaining why the rate exceeds the cap.

## 4. Mileage

If you use your private car for business travel within France, mileage is reimbursed at €0.20 per km. This rate covers fuel, wear and tear, and insurance for the distance you drive on company business. It does not cover tolls or parking fees, which you can claim separately with receipts.

Keep a simple log of the purpose, date and distance of each business trip. The distance should be calculated using the shortest reasonable route between your origin and destination.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, drives across the border to meet a prospect in Strasbourg. He records the kilometres driven on company business and claims them at the rate above.

## 5. Questions

If you have questions about this supplement, please contact the Finance team or reach out to it-help@kranich.example for help with submitting an expense report. For questions about how this supplement interacts with other policies, see the general travel policy or POL-004. We are happy to clarify anything before you travel.
