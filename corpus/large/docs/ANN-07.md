# Announcement: New corporate card provider

## 1. Announcement

We are pleased to announce that Kranich Software GmbH is moving to a new corporate card provider. This change affects all employees who use a corporate card for business travel, client meetings, or approved purchases. The transition will begin shortly and will be completed over the coming weeks.

**Why we are changing providers**

Our previous arrangement no longer met our needs. The new provider offers a more streamlined experience, better integration with our internal expense tool, and clearer visibility for both employees and the Finance team. We selected this provider after a careful review of options that support the way we work across Germany, Portugal, Spain, Poland, France, and the United Kingdom.

**What you need to do**

Most of you will not need to take any action right away. Your current card will remain active until the new card arrives. When your new card is delivered, simply activate it using the instructions included in the package. After activation, use the new card for all business expenses and stop using the old one.

If you do not currently have a corporate card but believe your role would benefit from one, please speak with your manager. Requests are reviewed by Finance and approved based on business need.

**How expenses work**

The process for submitting expenses remains unchanged. Please continue to log your expenses in Ledgerly as you do today, and attach receipts for every transaction. For guidance on what can be expensed and how to handle unusual situations, please refer to the relevant policy in the Grove policy library.

**Questions and support**

If you have questions about your card, delivery, or activation, please reach out to it-help@kranich.example. For questions about expense rules or approvals, contact people@kranich.example, and we will direct you to the right team.

We appreciate your patience during this transition and thank you for helping us keep our expense processes smooth and transparent.
