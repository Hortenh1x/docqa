# Equipment FAQ

## 1. Questions

**What equipment do I get when I join Kranich?**
Everyone receives a laptop, a monitor, a keyboard and mouse, and a headset. If your role requires additional gear — for example, a second monitor for field work or a mobile hotspot for client sites — your manager can request it through the process described in POL-004.

**Can I choose my own laptop model?**
We offer a small selection of approved models. If you have a strong preference for something else, talk to your manager. We evaluate requests case by case, considering your role and the tools you need to do your work.

**I work remotely. How do I get my equipment?**
We ship everything to your home address. For colleagues in Germany, Portugal, Spain, Poland, France and the United Kingdom, delivery usually takes a few days. If you are travelling when you join, let People & Culture know and we will arrange an alternative.

**What if something breaks or stops working?**
Contact it-help@kranich.example promptly. For issues that block your work, we prioritise a quick response. In the meantime, your manager can help you find a temporary solution, such as borrowing equipment from the office.

**Can I use my personal devices for work?**
For everyday tasks like checking email or reading documents, yes. For anything involving customer data or internal systems, please use your Kranich device. Security requirements are outlined in POL-004 and in our information security policy.

**What happens to my equipment when I leave Kranich?**
You will receive return instructions from Workplace & Operations. Please send the equipment back within the timeframe stated in the instructions. Costs for return shipping are covered by Kranich.

**Do I need to pay for repairs or replacements?**
No. Kranich covers the cost of repairs and replacements for equipment issued to you. This does not apply to damage caused by gross negligence; such cases are handled individually.

**Can I request additional equipment during my employment?**
Yes. If your needs change — for example, you move to a role that requires more screen space — submit a request through the channel described in POL-004. We review requests regularly and aim to respond promptly.

**Example:** Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, needed a second monitor after starting to review code reviews more frequently. She submitted a request through the standard process and received it within a few days.

**What about equipment for visitors or contractors?**
Visitors and short-term contractors use loaner equipment from the office. For longer engagements, the responsible manager arranges dedicated equipment following the same process.

**Where can I find the full equipment policy?**
See POL-004 for the complete policy, including eligibility and the replacement schedule. For travel-related equipment questions, refer to FIN-RATES-2026.
