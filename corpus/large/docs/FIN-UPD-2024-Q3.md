# Finance Update 2024 Q3

Access: Finance only

## 1. Summary

This document provides a brief, finance-only overview of our performance in the third quarter of the current year. It is intended for the Finance team and is not to be shared beyond that group. The update covers revenue, costs, and a short outlook for the remainder of the year.

As always, we encourage you to reach out to the Finance team with any questions or for further context on the items described below. We keep this update concise and focus on the key developments that matter for planning and stewardship.

## 2. Revenue

Revenue grew 12% year over year in 2024 Q3. This growth reflects continued adoption of the Kranich Route Cloud among mid-size logistics operators, as well as steady expansion within our existing customer base.

The increase is consistent with the trajectory we have seen over the past year. While we do not break down figures by product line or region in this update, the overall trend is healthy. We remain attentive to the pace of new customer onboarding and to the retention of our current accounts, both of which contribute to this result.

We also note that the growth in revenue has been supported by the work of our Customer Success and Go-to-Market teams, whose efforts in fostering strong relationships with clients continue to pay off. We thank everyone involved for their contribution.

## 3. Costs

Costs for the quarter have been managed carefully. We continue to invest in the areas that matter most for our product and our people, while keeping a close eye on discretionary spending.

Operational costs remain broadly in line with expectations. We have seen some increases in areas such as cloud infrastructure and support, which are natural as our platform scales. These increases are modest and are being offset by efficiencies elsewhere.

Travel and meeting expenses have been kept reasonable, reflecting our remote-first way of working. We encourage everyone to continue being thoughtful about spending and to follow the usual expense guidelines. For any questions on cost categories or approvals, please refer to the relevant finance policies, such as POL-004.

We have not identified any material cost anomalies in the quarter. Should anything arise, we will communicate promptly and transparently.

## 4. Outlook

Looking ahead to the remainder of the year, we are cautiously optimistic. The momentum from the third quarter is expected to carry through, though we remain mindful of broader market conditions.

Our focus in the coming months will be on sustaining the positive revenue trend, managing costs prudently, and ensuring that our investments are aligned with our strategic priorities. We will continue to monitor performance closely and will provide a fuller update in due course.

We expect that the final quarter of the year will bring the usual seasonal patterns. As always, we will keep the Finance team informed of any material changes to our plans or expectations.

We appreciate your continued diligence and stewardship. If you have any questions about this update or wish to discuss any aspect of it in more detail, please do not hesitate to reach out to the Finance team. We are here to support you and to ensure that we manage our resources wisely for the benefit of the whole company.
