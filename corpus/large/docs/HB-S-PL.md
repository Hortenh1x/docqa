# Travel Supplement — Poland

## 1. Purpose

This supplement explains how travel expenses for business trips to Poland are handled. It accompanies the general travel policy and applies to all Kranich employees, regardless of where they are based. If you travel to Poland on behalf of Kranich, please read this supplement alongside POL-004 and the rates document FIN-RATES-2026.

## 2. Per-diem

The per-diem for Poland is set centrally. Please refer to FIN-RATES-2026 for the applicable daily amount. The rate covers meals and incidental expenses for each full day of travel. For partial travel days, the rules in POL-004 apply.

## 3. Hotel cap

When you book accommodation for a business trip to Poland, the nightly rate must stay within the cap below. This cap applies to the room rate before taxes and any mandatory fees. If you expect to exceed the cap, please discuss your options with your manager before booking.

| Destination | Cap |
|---|---|
| Poland | €150 per night |

## 4. Mileage

If you use a private car for business travel within Poland, you can claim mileage reimbursement. Mileage in a private car in Poland is reimbursed at €0.31 per km. This rate covers fuel, wear and tear, and insurance. You should record your journey honestly and submit it through Ledgerly, as described in POL-004. If you rent a car instead, the rental cost is handled separately and is not covered by this mileage rate.

## 5. Questions

If anything in this supplement is unclear, or if your trip involves an unusual situation, please reach out to the Finance team. For questions about how to submit a claim, contact it-help@kranich.example. For general travel advice, your manager or the People & Culture team can point you to the right resource. We want your business travel to be straightforward, so please ask promptly rather than guessing.
