# Sick Leave FAQ

## 1. Questions

**Who do I tell when I am sick?**
Tell your manager and the People team via people@kranich.example as soon as you know you will not be working. For short absences, a quick message is enough. For longer absences, your manager will agree with you on how to stay in touch.

**Do I need a doctor's note?**
It depends on the length of your absence and the rules in your country of employment. Please check POL-004 for the exact thresholds. When a note is required, send it to People & Culture promptly.

**What if I am sick while working remotely?**
The same rules apply. You report your absence the same way, and you follow the certification requirements of the country where you are employed. Working from home does not change your entitlements.

**Can I work a bit while I am sick?**
No. Sick leave is for recovery. Please do not log in, answer emails, or join meetings while on sick leave. If you feel well enough to work, you should be well enough to return to your normal schedule — talk to your manager first.

**What happens to my planned leave or time off?**
If you fall sick during a period of approved leave, you can ask People & Culture to rebook those days as sick leave. You will need to provide the same documentation as for any other sick absence. Your leave days are then restored for later use.

**Example:** Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, falls sick on the first day of her planned holiday. She informs her manager and sends her doctor's note to People & Culture. Her holiday days are credited back to her leave balance, and she takes them later in the year.

**How do I report a sick day for a dependent child?**
Caring for a sick child is handled separately from your own sick leave. Please contact People & Culture to understand what applies in your country of employment. The rules differ between Germany, Portugal, and our other locations.

**What about long-term illness?**
If you expect to be away for an extended period, speak with your manager and People & Culture early. We will work with you on a plan, including any statutory protections and your return to work. You can also reach out to security@kranich.example if your illness involves a data-protection concern.

**Is sick leave confidential?**
Yes. Your manager and People & Culture treat health information with discretion. We only share what is necessary to process your leave and to support your return.

**Where can I find the full details?**
See POL-004 for the complete sick leave policy. For questions about pay during sick leave, refer to FIN-RATES-2026. If anything is unclear, contact people@kranich.example — we are happy to help.
