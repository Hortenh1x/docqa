# Leave Administration Guide

## 1. Purpose

This guide explains how leave works at Kranich Software GmbH. It covers the requests you can make, how to submit them, and what happens next. We want the process to be straightforward so you can plan your time away with confidence and your team can plan around you.

Leave is an important part of working sustainably. Taking time off helps you recharge and return with focus. This guide is here to make that easy for everyone, whether you work from our Berlin office, our Lisbon office, or remotely from your home country.

## 2. Who this is for

This guide is for every employee at Kranich Software GmbH, regardless of your team, your role, or where you are based. The principles are the same for everyone. Some local rules may apply where you are employed, and your manager or the People & Culture team can help you understand those.

If you are a manager, you will also find this guide useful. It describes how to review requests, how to plan for absences on your team, and where to go when you need support.

New joiners should read this guide as part of their onboarding. Existing team members can use it as a reference whenever they need a reminder.

## 3. Process

### Submitting a request

All leave requests go through Fern, our HRIS. You will find the leave section under your personal profile. Select the type of leave you want to take, enter the dates, and add a short note for your manager if helpful.

Submit your request as early as you can. This gives your manager and your team time to plan. For longer periods of leave, please submit well in advance. For shorter breaks, a few days of notice is usually enough.

### Review and approval

Your manager receives your request in Fern and reviews it. They will consider the needs of the team, the time of year, and any other requests already approved. Your manager may approve the request directly or reach out to discuss it first.

You will receive a notification in Fern once a decision is made. If your request is declined, your manager will explain why and help you find an alternative.

### Cancelling or changing a request

If your plans change, update your request in Fern as soon as you know. Cancel or modify the dates so your manager and team have accurate information. There is no penalty for changing a request, but please be considerate of others who may be planning around you.

### Handover

Before you go on leave, agree with your manager on a handover. This is especially important for longer absences. Let your team know what you are working on and who to contact in your absence. A short note in your team channel is often enough.

### Returning to work

When you return, check in with your manager and your team. Catch up on anything you missed and pick up your tasks again. Your first day back is a good time to review your inbox and any messages in team channels.

## 4. Timelines

We aim to keep the process simple and predictable.

- Submit your request through Fern as early as possible.
- Your manager reviews requests promptly.
- You will receive a decision in Fern once your manager has responded.

For leave around busy periods, such as the end of the year, submit earlier than usual. This helps your team plan fairly when several people want time off at the same time.

If you need to take leave at short notice, for example due to a family matter, speak to your manager directly. They can help you arrange the time you need without waiting for the usual process.

Some types of leave follow different timelines. Your manager or the People & Culture team can point you to the right information. For example, the parental carryover exception lives in POL-008 §7, which explains how parental leave interacts with your leave balance.

## 5. Questions

If you are unsure about anything in this guide, start with your manager. They can answer most questions about planning and approval.

For questions about your leave balance, the types of leave available to you, or how local rules apply in your country of employment, contact People & Culture at people@kranich.example.

For technical issues with Fern, such as problems submitting a request or receiving notifications, contact IT support at it-help@kranich.example.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, wants to take time off to visit family. She opens Fern, selects the appropriate leave type, enters the dates, and submits the request to her manager. Her manager approves it the same day. Anna adds a short note to her team channel about her ongoing work, and her colleague covers for her while she is away.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, needs to change a leave request because a client meeting moved. She updates the dates in Fern and lets her manager know. Her manager adjusts the team plan accordingly, and everything runs smoothly.

We want leave to work well for you and for the company. If you have feedback on this guide or the process itself, please share it with People & Culture. We review our policies regularly and welcome your input.
