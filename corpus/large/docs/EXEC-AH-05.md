# All-Hands Notes — September 2025

## 1. Highlights

Marta opened the all-hands with a warm welcome, noting how good it was to see so many faces from both the Ritterstraße office in Berlin and the Rua do Alecrim office in Lisbon on the video wall. She thanked everyone for making the summer handover period smooth, especially those who covered for colleagues while others were away.

Jonas shared that the Routing Core team has been working closely with Platform & Infrastructure on a major reliability initiative. The work is progressing well and remains on track. He praised the team for their disciplined approach to incident reviews and for turning every post-incident discussion into a concrete follow-up.

Tomasz briefly spoke about the upcoming planning cycle. He reminded everyone that expense reports and travel bookings should continue to go through Ledgerly as usual, and that the Finance team is available for questions ahead of the annual budgeting conversations.

## 2. People

Aylin shared updates from People & Culture. The company continues to grow steadily, and several new colleagues have joined across Customer Success and Go-to-Market. She encouraged everyone to reach out and make the new joiners feel welcome, whether they sit in Berlin, Lisbon, or work remotely from another country.

Fern, our HRIS, now includes an updated org chart. Aylin asked everyone to take a moment to review their personal data and make sure their details are current. If anything looks off, Lea from People Operations is the first point of contact.

Aylin also reminded the room about the importance of using Grove for all policy questions. The policy library has been reorganised, and it is now easier to find the document you need. If you cannot find an answer, reach out to people@kranich.example rather than asking a colleague to forward an old version.

Priya joined briefly to remind everyone about the upcoming security awareness session. She stressed that phishing attempts have become more convincing and that taking a few extra seconds to verify a sender is always worth it. For anything suspicious, forward the message to security@kranich.example.

## 3. Customers

Deniz shared a heart-warming story from Customer Success. A long-standing client in the retail sector had been struggling with delivery windows that kept slipping. Our team helped them reconfigure their routing parameters, and the client described the improvement as transformative for their daily operations. Deniz thanked the Routing Core engineers who jumped in to help with the technical diagnosis.

Marek, our field solutions engineer, has been spending time with clients in Poland. He reported that the conversations there are shifting from "how does the software work" to "how can we get even more value from it." This is a positive sign that our customers see Kranich Route Cloud as a long-term partner, not just a tool.

Sofia gave a quick update from the Fleet Insights product team. She mentioned that customer feedback from recent discovery interviews has been clear and consistent, and the team is now synthesising those insights into the roadmap. She thanked the Customer Success team for making those introductions possible.

Rui reminded everyone that the Lisbon office has room for more desk bookings through Perch. If you are planning to visit the Rua do Alecrim office, book early so the Workplace team can plan catering and meeting rooms properly.

## 4. Questions from the floor

**Q: Will there be a company-wide offsite this year?**
Aylin answered that plans are being discussed but nothing is confirmed yet. She expects to share more before the end of the year, and any offsite will be announced with enough notice for people to plan travel.

**Q: How should we handle requests from clients for custom reports that are not in the product?**
Jonas suggested routing those requests through the Customer Success manager first. If it looks like a pattern across several clients, the request should be shared with the product teams. A single custom request can often be solved with an existing feature that the client simply does not know about.

**Q: What is the best way to give feedback about internal tools?**
Rui encouraged everyone to use the feedback option in Grove. He also mentioned that the Workplace team reviews tool-related feedback regularly and will follow up when a suggestion is implemented.

**Q: Can we see the roadmap for the next quarter?**
Sofia explained that the roadmap is shared on Grove under the Fleet Insights space. She invited anyone with questions to reach out directly or to join the monthly product open office hour.

Marta closed the session by thanking everyone for their attention and for the thoughtful questions. She reminded the team that the next all-hands will be announced through the usual calendar invitation, and that recordings of this session will be available on Grove shortly.
