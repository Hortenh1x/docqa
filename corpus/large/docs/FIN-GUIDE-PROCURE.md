# Procurement How-To

## 1. Purpose

This guide explains how to buy goods and services for Kranich Software. Whether you need a new laptop, a software subscription, or catering for a team offsite, the steps below help you get what you need without delay.

We keep procurement simple. Most requests are routine and follow the same path. For anything unusual, ask us early — Finance is happy to help you find the right way forward.

The spending thresholds that determine who approves what are in POL-018 §3. Please read that section before you start, so you know which approval path applies to your request.

## 2. Raising a request

Start by raising a request in Ledgerly. Choose the procurement option, describe what you need, and explain why. Attach any quotes or links you already have. The more context you give, the faster we can process your request.

Before you submit, check whether the item is already available internally. For hardware, look at what the company has in stock first. For software, check whether another team already holds a licence you can use. If you are unsure, ask your manager or the relevant team lead.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, needs a second monitor for her home setup. She checks the internal hardware list, finds nothing available, and raises a request in Ledgerly with a link to the model she prefers.

Once you submit, you will receive a confirmation. Finance reviews requests promptly. If anything is missing, we will come back to you with clear questions — nothing personal, we simply need the right information to proceed.

## 3. Approvals

Every request needs an approval before we place an order. The level of approval depends on the value of the purchase. POL-018 §3 defines the thresholds precisely; as a rule of thumb, higher values need more senior sign-off.

Your manager approves the business case first. For larger purchases, the responsible department head adds their approval. For the largest purchases, Finance signs off personally. We will tell you who needs to approve your request once we have reviewed it.

You do not need to chase approvers yourself. Ledgerly routes the request automatically and sends reminders. If an approver is away, the request waits until they return or until a delegate steps in. Please factor this into your planning.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, wants to attend a client event. Her manager approves the registration fee. Because the total stays within the lower threshold, no further approval is needed.

If your request is urgent, mention this when you raise it. We will do our best to prioritise, but we cannot bypass the approval process. Plan ahead where you can.

## 4. Suppliers

We work with a preferred list of suppliers for common categories such as hardware, software, and office supplies. Using these suppliers speeds things up because contracts and payment terms are already in place.

If you have a supplier in mind that is not on the preferred list, that is fine. Add their details to your request, including a quote if possible. Finance will check the supplier before we proceed. This check is standard practice and usually takes a few days.

We do not pay suppliers in advance. For new suppliers, we set up payment terms on delivery or after the service is rendered. If a supplier insists on prepayment, let us know — we will handle the conversation.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer working with clients in Poland, needs a custom demo device from a local vendor. He attaches the vendor's quote to his request. Finance runs the standard supplier check, and the order proceeds once the check comes back clean.

Always order through Ledgerly. Purchases made outside the system cannot be reimbursed through the standard process. If you have already paid for something out of pocket, contact Finance before you raise the request — we will advise you on the right steps.

## 5. Questions

If you are unsure about any part of the process, ask before you act. Finance is reachable through Ledgerly or by contacting the Finance team directly. For questions about policy, see POL-018. For questions about rates or billing codes, see FIN-RATES-2026.

You can also ask your manager or your local office coordinator. Rui Almeida and the Workplace team know the practical side of ordering for the Berlin and Lisbon offices and can point you in the right direction.

We would rather answer a simple question than untangle a purchase made the wrong way. When in doubt, reach out.
