# Board Memo 2 — 2025

Access: Leadership only

## 1. Summary

Kranich Software GmbH enters the second quarter of the current fiscal year with healthy momentum. Our route-optimization platform, Kranich Route Cloud, continues to earn trust among mid-size logistics operators across our core European markets. The Leadership Team presents this memo to the board to align on growth, cash position, the principal risks we see ahead, and the specific asks we bring to this meeting.

## 2. Growth

ARR grew 18% year over year, reflecting steady expansion within our existing customer base and measured new logo acquisition. The growth is broad-based: the Routing Core and Fleet Insights product lines both contributed, and our Customer Success team reports strong engagement with our enterprise-tier features.

Our remote-first operating model, with employees across Germany, Portugal, Spain, Poland, France and the United Kingdom, lets us hire for talent rather than proximity. The Lisbon office (Rua do Alecrim) and Berlin headquarters (Ritterstraße) remain important hubs for collaboration, but most colleagues work from home within their country of employment.

Example: Deniz, a customer success manager in Berlin, noticed that several German logistics clients were underusing the dynamic re-optimization feature. She worked with Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, to craft a simple enablement guide. Within a short period, those clients increased their weekly optimization runs, contributing to the positive ARR trend.

We see further headroom in the Polish and Southern European markets. Marek, our field solutions engineer based in Poland, has been running discovery sessions with prospects who have outgrown spreadsheets but are not yet ready for the largest enterprise suites. Our product roadmap, owned by Sofia in Fleet Insights, aligns well with the needs those sessions surface.

## 3. Cash

Cash runway stands at 28 months. Our disciplined approach to hiring and infrastructure spend keeps us comfortably funded without requiring external capital in the near term. We continue to invest in Platform & Infrastructure reliability, which underpins customer trust and reduces churn risk.

We are mindful that cash runway is a forward-looking measure. The Finance team, led by CFO Tomasz Zieliński, reviews our burn rate monthly against the budget approved at the start of the fiscal year. No material deviations are forecast.

Example: Lea, a people operations specialist in Berlin, recently processed a round of expense reports through Ledgerly. The reports showed a modest increase in travel costs as Customer Success teams met clients on-site. This is consistent with our plan, and Finance flagged it as within tolerance.

## 4. Risks

The principal risks we track are concentration, retention, and talent. On concentration, a handful of accounts contribute a meaningful share of ARR. We are mitigating this through the expansion work described in §2, but we ask the board to note it as a watch item.

On retention, our Customer Success team has seen that clients who adopt our fleet insights dashboards in the first months tend to stay longer. We are therefore prioritizing onboarding quality over raw feature velocity in the current quarter.

On talent, the market for engineers and product managers remains competitive. We have not changed our compensation philosophy, and we rely on our culture and mission to attract colleagues. We do not plan to introduce new benefits outside our current framework.

Example: Ines Duarte, engineering manager for Routing Core in Berlin, recently conducted a stay interview with a senior engineer who had received an external approach. The engineer cited meaningful work and flexible scheduling as reasons to remain. We take such signals seriously and will continue to monitor retention through our HRIS, Fern.

## 5. Asks

We bring three asks to the board.

First, we ask for endorsement of our current hiring plan for the remainder of the fiscal year, which prioritizes Platform & Infrastructure roles to support reliability and security. Head of Information Security Priya Nayar has reviewed the plan and confirms it aligns with our security roadmap.

Second, we ask the board to approve the updated internal policy set for the coming year. People & Culture, led by VP Aylin Demir, has revised several policies to reflect our remote-first reality. The relevant documents are POL-004 and FIN-RATES-2026, which the team will circulate for review before the next board meeting.

Third, we ask for guidance on our office strategy. Head of Workplace & Operations Rui Almeida has prepared a summary of utilization at both the Ritterstraße and Rua do Alecrim offices, based on booking data from Perch. We would value the board's perspective on whether to renew our current leases or explore flexible space in either city.

We welcome the board's questions and look forward to a productive discussion. CEO Marta Lindqvist and CTO Jonas Weber will be available to elaborate on any item in this memo.
