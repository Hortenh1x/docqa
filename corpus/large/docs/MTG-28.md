# Meeting Notes — Customer Success review (2025-05-04)

## 1. Attendees

- Deniz (Customer Success, Berlin)
- Marek (Field Solutions, Poland)
- Sofia (Product, Fleet Insights)
- Anna (Routing Core, Lisbon)
- Ines Duarte (Engineering Manager, Berlin)

## 2. Discussion

- Deniz opened the review by summarizing the past quarter’s overall sentiment across the customer base. Feedback has been generally positive, with particular praise for the onboarding experience and the responsiveness of the Customer Success team.
- Marek shared observations from recent client visits in Poland. A few mid-size operators are struggling to adopt the more advanced routing features. They find the user interface clear, but the configuration options can be overwhelming without guided setup.
- Sofia noted that several feature requests from customers overlap with work already planned in the Fleet Insights roadmap. She suggested better internal communication so that Customer Success can set accurate expectations.
- Anna raised a recurring technical issue: some customers see occasional sync delays between the route planner and their vehicle telematics. The Routing Core team has identified the likely cause and is testing a fix. No timeline was committed, but Anna expects the patch to be ready for review promptly.
- Ines proposed a closer collaboration between engineering and Customer Success. She suggested that Deniz and Marek share anonymized examples of common customer questions, so the team can improve tooltips and inline help.
- The group briefly discussed the upcoming renewal season. Deniz will prepare a shortlist of accounts that may need extra attention, based on usage patterns and support history.
- Everyone agreed that the regular weekly sync between Customer Success and Product has been helpful, and that the review format should continue on a quarterly basis.

## 3. Decisions

- Customer Success will collect and share anonymized examples of common configuration questions with the Routing Core team.
- The Routing Core team will prioritize the telematics sync fix and inform Customer Success once it is available for testing.
- Sofia will circulate the Fleet Insights roadmap summary to Customer Success before the next product release.
- The quarterly Customer Success review will become a standing meeting, with rotating participation from engineering.

## 4. Action items

- Deniz: prepare the shortlist of accounts for renewal support and share it with the team.
- Marek: write up a few field notes from client visits in Poland, focusing on adoption barriers.
- Anna: update Deniz and Marek on the status of the sync fix once the patch is ready.
- Sofia: send the roadmap summary and invite Customer Success to the next planning session.
- Ines: set up a shared space for the anonymized examples and coordinate with Deniz on the format.
