# Travel & Expense Policy

## 1. Purpose

Business travel should be easy to do right. This policy covers when travel is justified, how to book it, what Kranich pays for, and how reimbursement works. It is owned by Finance; the warm tone is on purpose — the rules are few and firm.

## 2. When to travel

Travel when presence changes the outcome: client workshops, go-lives, team planning weeks, conferences you speak at. For everything else, default to a call. Your manager approves trips before booking.

Example: Marek, a field solutions engineer, travels to a logistics client near Kraków for a two-day rollout. His manager approved the trip in Fern the week before; nothing else was needed.

## 3. Booking

Book through Ledgerly's travel module using your corporate card where possible. Choose reasonable options: economy flights, standard hotel rooms, public transport over taxis where practical. Trains are preferred over flights for routes under five hours door to door.

## 4. Per-diems (daily allowances)

Kranich pays a per-diem for meals and incidentals on business trips. **The current per-diem rates are maintained in the Expense Rates & Per-Diems sheet (FIN-001)** — always check there rather than relying on numbers you remember, because Finance updates the sheet annually. FIN-001 also defines how partial travel days are treated.

Per-diems are paid automatically with your expense report; you do not need meal receipts for them.

## 5. What is reimbursable

- Transport to, from and at the destination (flights, trains, local transit, reasonable taxis).
- Accommodation for the nights the trip requires.
- Client-related costs (a working lunch with a customer, printed materials).
- Roaming or a local SIM when your plan does not cover the destination.

Personal detours, minibar, and fines of any kind are not reimbursable.

## 6. Expense reports

Submit your expense report in Ledgerly no later than 30 calendar days after the trip ends. Attach receipts for everything except per-diems. Finance reviews weekly; approved reports are paid with the next payroll run.

## 7. Insurance and safety

Kranich provides business travel insurance for all approved trips. Save the emergency assistance number from Grove before departure. If anything happens on the road, your health comes first — sort receipts out later.

## 8. Questions

finance@kranich.example answers policy questions; booking issues go to it-help@kranich.example. See also the Workation Guideline (POL-003) — a workation is not business travel and has no per-diems.
