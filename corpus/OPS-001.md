# Office & Meeting Room Guide

## 1. Purpose

How the Berlin and Lisbon offices actually work: getting in, booking rooms and desks, hosting guests, and the few house rules that keep shared space pleasant.

## 2. Locations and access

- **Berlin (HQ):** Ritterstraße 24, 10969 Berlin — floors 3 and 4. Access with your badge, workdays 07:00–21:00.
- **Lisbon:** Rua do Alecrim 12, 1200-017 Lisboa — floor 2. Same badge, same hours.

Lost badges go to it-help@kranich.example immediately (a lost badge is handled like a lost device).

## 3. Desks and rooms: Perch

Meeting rooms and desks are booked via the internal Perch tool — the mobile app or the panel next to each room. Two rules keep Perch honest:

- Each person may hold at most 3 recurring meeting-room slots; more recurring bookings require Workplace & Operations approval, which is rarely granted.
- Release what you will not use. Rooms free themselves after ten minutes of no-show, and repeat no-shows get a friendly note from Rui's team.

## 4. Hosting guests

Register visitors in Perch the day before: reception prints badges, and client visits get a room with the good coffee machine reserved automatically. Guests are accompanied at all times — office access rules exist for the same reason POL-005 does.

## 5. Focus and phone areas

Floor 3 in Berlin and the window row in Lisbon are quiet zones: no calls, no meetings at desks. Phone booths are first-come; calls longer than an hour belong in a booked room, not a booth.

## 6. Kitchen and shared spaces

The kitchen is everyone's: label what is yours, and the Friday fridge cleanup discards everything unlabeled. Team lunches can be charged per the Travel & Expense Policy when a client is present; otherwise the kitchen budget covers the basics — coffee, tea, fruit, oat milk included.

## 7. Deliveries

Personal parcels to the office are fine within reason; reception holds them for five workdays. Bulky private deliveries need a heads-up to Rui's team so the mailroom does not drown.

## 8. Questions and problems

Broken chair, flickering light, too-cold meeting room: #workplace or workplace@kranich.example. Building emergencies out of hours: the number on the back of your badge.
