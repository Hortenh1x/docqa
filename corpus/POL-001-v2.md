# Vacation & Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose and status

This policy explains how vacation works at Kranich Software: your entitlement, how to request time off, and what happens to unused days. **This policy supersedes Vacation & Leave Policy v1.0 in its entirety.** If you find an older copy floating around, the version with the later effective date — this one — wins.

## 2. Scope

All permanent employees of Kranich Software GmbH in Germany and Portugal, full-time and part-time (pro rata). Contractors and working students follow their individual agreements.

## 3. Annual entitlement

Every full-time employee receives 27 vacation days per calendar year, on top of the public holidays of their place of employment. Days accrue monthly from your first day.

Tenure is rewarded: you gain one additional day for every 2 full years of service, up to a total of 30 days per year. The bonus applies from the calendar year in which the anniversary falls.

Example: Anna, a backend engineer in Lisbon, joined in 2021. In 2025 she has completed 4 full years of service, so her entitlement is 29 days.

## 4. Requesting vacation

Submit requests in Fern as early as possible; your manager approves them. Approval is the default — pushback happens only when critical coverage is at risk, and your manager will propose an alternative window.

For any absence longer than 5 consecutive working days, give at least 4 weeks of notice. Single days and short breaks are flexible.

## 5. Carryover

Unused days may be carried into the next calendar year and expire on March 31 of that year. We nudge everyone in January — carryover is a safety valve, not a savings account, and People & Culture reviews teams where large balances pile up.

## 6. Sickness during vacation

If you fall ill during booked vacation, affected days convert back to sick leave once you provide the documentation described in the Sick Leave & Medical Absence policy (POL-007). Tell your manager as soon as you reasonably can.

## 7. Leaving the company

Untaken accrued days are paid out with your final payroll; days taken beyond accrual are deducted. The exact numbers appear in your offboarding summary from People & Culture.
