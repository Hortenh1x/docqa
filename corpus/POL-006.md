# Equipment & Hardware Policy

## 1. Purpose

Good tools are cheaper than bad ergonomics. This policy defines what hardware Kranich provides, the budgets by role, and how ordering, refresh and returns work.

## 2. Standard setup

Every employee receives a company laptop plus the peripherals they need for a healthy setup — external monitor, keyboard, mouse or trackpad, headset, and a laptop stand. Home-office chairs and desks are supported within the budget below when your local setup needs them.

## 3. Budgets by role

The equipment budget covers everything beyond the laptop itself and renews with the refresh cycle.

| Role category | Budget |
| --- | --- |
| Standard role | €2,200 |
| Engineering role | €2,800 |

Engineering covers software engineers, data engineers, and field solutions engineers — roles that routinely run heavy local workloads. Everyone else, including management, uses the standard budget.

## 4. Refresh cycle

Laptops and budget renew every 36 months. If your device struggles earlier — battery health, repairs piling up — IT swaps it without ceremony; the cycle is a guideline for planning, not a wall.

Example: Marek's field laptop lives a rough life in warehouses. When its hinge failed after two years, IT replaced the device the same week and simply restarted his cycle.

## 5. Ordering

Order through the IT catalog in Grove; anything in the catalog within your remaining budget is approved automatically. Items outside the catalog need a one-line justification to it-help@kranich.example — the answer usually arrives the same day.

## 6. Ownership and care

All equipment remains the property of Kranich Software. Treat it like your own: use the provided sleeve, keep liquids at a respectful distance, and report damage or loss to it-help@kranich.example immediately (a lost device is also a security incident — see POL-005).

## 7. Returning equipment

When you leave the company, return all equipment within 10 business days after your last working day. The Offboarding Checklist (HR-002) includes the prepaid shipping option for remote employees; office-based colleagues hand devices to IT directly.

## 8. Questions

it-help@kranich.example, or the #it-help channel for quick ones.
