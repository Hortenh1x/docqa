# Parental Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose

Becoming a parent should not require a law degree. This policy explains how parental leave works at Kranich Software on top of the statutory rules of your country of employment, and how we handle the practical side — handovers, pay, staying in touch, and coming back.

## 2. Scope

All permanent employees in Germany and Portugal. Statutory frameworks differ by country; Fern shows the rules that apply to your contract, and People & Culture walks every expecting parent through them personally.

## 3. Telling us

Tell People & Culture as early as you feel comfortable — earlier means better planning, but the timing is yours. The conversation is confidential until you decide otherwise. Your manager learns the dates when you are ready to plan the handover.

## 4. Leave and pay

Statutory parental leave and benefits apply in full. Where the statutory benefit replaces only part of your salary, Kranich tops up as described in the Benefits Overview (HR-004). The top-up requires no separate application; payroll applies it automatically once the leave is registered.

## 5. Before the leave

Plan the handover with your manager in the last weeks before leave: owned systems, running projects, client relationships. Example: when Sofia from Fleet Insights went on leave, her handover page in Grove became the team's template — one page, owners named, links to everything.

## 6. During the leave

You are a guest, not an employee-on-call: no meetings, no expectations. If you *want* to stay loosely in the loop, opt in to the monthly team digest in Fern. Company gatherings remain open to you, family included.

## 7. Returning to work

### 7.1 Planning the return

Agree the return date with People & Culture at least a month ahead so access, equipment and a re-onboarding buddy are ready. Ramp-up weeks with reduced meeting load are the default, not a favor.

### 7.2 Vacation balance after leave

Vacation days accrued before and during parental leave are preserved in full. For employees returning from parental leave, the carryover deadline for those preserved days is extended to June 30 of the year of return. Fern shows the adjusted expiry date on your balance automatically, so no application is needed.

### 7.3 Flexible restart

A temporarily reduced schedule for the first months back is available where statutory rules allow it; discuss the setup with People & Culture and your manager.
