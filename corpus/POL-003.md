# Workation Guideline

## 1. Purpose

A workation is a **temporary** stay abroad while you keep working — a few weeks near family, a month by the coast. Unlike the Remote Work Policy (POL-002), which governs your permanent setup in your country of employment, this guideline covers short-term work from **another** country.

## 2. What is allowed

You may work from other EU/EEA countries for up to 30 working days per calendar year in total. Both your manager and People & Culture must approve the stay before you book anything — approval covers team coverage on the manager's side and tax/social-security exposure on ours.

Days are counted per working day actually spent abroad, across all trips combined.

Example: Sofia, a product manager living in Madrid, spends three weeks in Portugal near her parents each spring. That uses 15 of her annual workation days, and her February request last year was approved within two days.

## 3. What is not covered

- Stays outside the EU/EEA — these need a case-by-case review by People & Culture and are usually declined for tax reasons.
- Moving your work base permanently: a workation does not change your country of employment or your contract.
- Client-site travel — that is business travel under the Travel & Expense Policy (POL-004).

## 4. Your responsibilities while abroad

- Keep your normal working hours agreed with your team, including the 11:00–15:00 CET anchor window.
- Follow the IT & Information Security Policy (POL-005) exactly as at home: company device, VPN on untrusted networks, screen locked when away.
- Stay reachable: your emergency contact details in Fern must be current for the duration of the stay.

## 5. How to request

Request the stay in Fern at least two weeks before departure: destination country, dates, and a one-line coverage note. People & Culture confirms the tax side; your manager confirms coverage. You will get a single approval notification when both are done.

## 6. Questions

Ask in #people or write to people@kranich.example. For travel booked for client work, use the Travel & Expense Policy (POL-004) instead.
