# Code of Conduct

## 1. Why this exists

One hundred and forty people build Kranich together. This code describes the behavior we expect from everyone — employees, leadership, contractors, and guests at our events — so that "how we treat each other" never depends on who you ask.

## 2. The short version

Be professional, be kind, assume good intent, and speak up when something is off. Disagree with ideas as hard as you like; never with a person's dignity.

## 3. What we expect

- **Respect.** No harassment, discrimination, or demeaning behavior of any kind — in offices, remote channels, and at client sites alike.
- **Directness with care.** Feedback goes to the person first, in private, in a form you would accept yourself.
- **Reliability.** Commitments are kept or renegotiated early — silence is the only wrong option.
- **Inclusion.** Meetings and documents are written so a colleague from another team, timezone, or background can follow them.

## 4. What we do not tolerate

Harassment, bullying, retaliation against people who raise concerns, deliberate breaches of security or data-protection rules, and any behavior that would make a reasonable colleague feel unsafe. Alcohol at company events is fine; pressure to drink is not.

## 5. Conflicts of interest

Side projects are welcome as long as they do not compete with Kranich, use company resources, or crowd out your commitments. Financial interests in vendors, clients, or competitors are disclosed to People & Culture — disclosure protects you.

Example: Marek mentors at a local logistics-tech meetup that sometimes features Kranich clients. He disclosed it once in Fern; nobody has bothered him about it since, which is exactly the point.

## 6. Raising concerns

Talk to your manager, People & Culture, or — anonymously if you prefer — through the concern form in Grove. Every report is taken seriously, investigated confidentially, and retaliation is itself a violation of this code.

## 7. Consequences

Violations lead to consequences proportionate to the behavior, from a documented conversation up to termination. Deliberate breaches of the IT & Information Security Policy (POL-005) or the Internal Data Protection Policy (POL-010) are handled under this section.

## 8. A living document

Suggest improvements in #people or to people@kranich.example. The code belongs to everyone who works here.
